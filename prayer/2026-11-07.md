## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Righteous judgment as the mandate is decided

**In the news:** [Netanyahu warns against an Arab–Jewish coalition in first appearance since the election](https://haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-05/ty-article-live/netanyahu-warns-against-arab-jewish-coalition-in-first-appearance-since-election/000001a1-a9ee-defa-a7f7-adfff1720000)

> <sup>1</sup> Give the king thy judgments, O God, and thy righteousness unto the king’s son. <sup>2</sup> He shall judge thy people with righteousness, and thy poor with judgment.
>
> — **Psalm 72:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the righteous King who judges Your people with righteousness. Herzog is to decide on Sunday who receives the mandate to form a government, and Netanyahu has warned against a coalition that relies on Arab and Jewish parties together. I ask You to give every leader a heart for justice, to speak the truth without stirring up fear, and to care for the poor and the stranger as You do. Let whoever is chosen govern for the good of all citizens, Jew and Arab, and keep the nation from bitterness and deadlock. Teach those in power to seek Your judgments before their own advantage. Amen.

### 2. Deliverance from hatred in Philadelphia

**In the news:** [Philadelphia bicycle ride targeting Jewish-owned businesses draws condemnation](https://jta.org/2026/11/06/united-states/a-bicycle-ride-protest-targeting-philadelphia-jewish-businesses-spurs-outrage-from-city-and-state-leaders)

> <sup>1</sup> Deliver me, O LORD, from the evil man: preserve me from the violent man; <sup>2</sup> Which imagine mischiefs in their heart; continually are they gathered together for war.
>
> — **Psalm 140:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the Jewish families and business owners in Philadelphia, where a bicycle ride aimed at Jewish-owned businesses is set for the eve of the Kristallnacht anniversary. Deliver them from the violent man, as the psalm asks, and preserve them from those who plan mischief. Give city and state leaders the courage they have shown in speaking out, and let police keep every shopkeeper and customer safe. Restrain hatred before it becomes harm, soften hearts that have been taught to despise, and comfort those who feel afraid to open their doors. Amen.

### 3. Gathering in safety

**In the news:** [Report counts 244 attacks and threats on Jewish places of worship in 34 countries since 7 October 2023](https://ynetnews.com/jewish-world/article/bjiv12tamg)

> <sup>24</sup> And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works: <sup>25</sup> Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching.
>
> — **Hebrews 10:24-25** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, a new report counts 244 attacks and threats against Jewish places of worship in 34 countries since October 2023. I ask You to protect every congregation as it gathers, especially on Shabbat and holy days. Let those who meet to encourage one another in love and good works be guarded by wise security and calm hearts. Frustrate the plans of those who would attack, and bring them to repentance before they act. Strengthen communities so fear does not scatter them, and let Your peace rest on every doorway. Amen.

### 4. Teaching the next generation after Kristallnacht

**In the news:** [Montreal Jewish-owned bakery damaged in suspected arson](https://jta.org/2026/11/06/global/kristallnacht-survivor-calls-on-german-police-to-stand-firm-against-hate)

> <sup>4</sup> We will not hide them from their children, shewing to the generation to come the praises of the LORD, and his strength, and his wonderful works that he hath done. <sup>5</sup> For he established a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they should make them known to their children: <sup>6</sup> That the generation to come might know them, even the children which should be born; who should arise and declare them to their children: <sup>7</sup> That they might set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments:
>
> — **Psalm 78:4-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, a survivor of Kristallnacht has told German police leaders to learn the lessons of 1938. I thank You for the survivors who still speak, and I pray that their voices are heard. Help parents and teachers pass on both the memory of what hatred did and the praises of the Lord, so the next generation sets its hope in God. Give police and officials the resolve to stand firm against hate in our day. Comfort the elderly who carry these memories, and let truth outlast every lie. Amen.

### 5. Messengers of good tidings

**In the news:** [About 6,500 Chabad rabbis gather in Brooklyn for annual conference](https://jta.org/2026/11/06/ideas/in-the-face-of-tragedy-the-way-forward-is-to-build)

> *And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!*
>
> — **Romans 10:15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, about 6,500 Chabad rabbis from more than 100 countries are gathered in Brooklyn this weekend, and many serve Jewish communities in places that are often lonely or threatened. I ask You to keep them and their families safe as they return to their cities. Bless their care for the isolated and the grieving, and lead them into the whole truth about You. Send faithful messengers across the world with good tidings of peace, and let every Jewish community be met with kindness. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. If the Lord had not been on our side

> <sup>1</sup> If it had not been the LORD who was on our side, now may Israel say; <sup>2</sup> If it had not been the LORD who was on our side, when men rose up against us: <sup>3</sup> Then they had swallowed us up quick, when their wrath was kindled against us:
>
> — **Psalm 124:1-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, if You had not been on Israel's side, the nation would have been swallowed up. I thank You for every deliverance, known and unknown, and I ask You to keep the nation and its people alive and safe. Be on the side of the soldiers on the borders, the families who wait for them and the civilians under threat. Let Israel say again and again that her help was from You. Turn the wrath of those who rise against her into the knowledge that You alone are God. Amen.

### 7. The Lord roars from Zion

> *The LORD also shall roar out of Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heavens and the earth shall shake: but the LORD will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel.*
>
> — **Joel 3:16** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You roar out of Zion and utter Your voice from Jerusalem, and heaven and earth shake. I pray that Your voice would be heard in Jerusalem now, calming the fears of its people and stopping the plans of those who would harm them. Be the hope of Your people and the strength of the children of Israel. Guard the city, its holy places and everyone who lives or visits there, Jew, Christian and Muslim alike. Let those who trust You rest without fear. Amen.

### 8. The mighty God in the midst of them

> *The LORD thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing.*
>
> — **Zephaniah 3:17** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are in the midst of Your people, mighty to save, rejoicing over them with singing. I pray for Israelis who are weary of war and uncertainty, and for Jews in every nation who feel alone. Quiet them with Your love, and let them know that You rejoice over them. Heal the traumatised, comfort the bereaved, and renew the strength of those who serve. Save Your people from their enemies and from despair, and draw them to Yourself. Amen.

### 9. Not one sparrow falls

> <sup>29</sup> Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing? and one of them shall not fall on the ground without your Father. <sup>30</sup> But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. <sup>31</sup> Fear ye not therefore, ye are of more value than many sparrows.
>
> — **Matthew 10:29-31** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that not one sparrow falls without the Father and that the hairs of our heads are numbered. I pray this over every Jewish child, parent, soldier and elder who is afraid today. Tell their hearts that they are of more value than many sparrows. Protect the people who travel to and from synagogue, who walk the streets of Israel and who work in the markets and the schools. Give them courage that does not come from their own strength, and keep them in Your care. Amen.

### 10. The Lord is my helper

> *So that we may boldly say, The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me.*
>
> — **Hebrews 13:6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray that Jewish people everywhere may boldly say, The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man can do to me. Be their helper in Israel, in Judea and Samaria, and in every diaspora city. Strengthen those who face threats at work, at school and in their neighbourhoods, and raise up friends and neighbours who stand beside them. Give wisdom to authorities who must protect them. Let Your faithfulness to Abraham's children be seen and trusted. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Great sorrow for my kinsmen

> <sup>1</sup> I say the truth in Christ, I lie not, my conscience also bearing me witness in the Holy Ghost, <sup>2</sup> That I have great heaviness and continual sorrow in my heart. <sup>3</sup> For I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh:
>
> — **Romans 9:1-3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, like Paul I carry heaviness and continual sorrow for my brethren, the Jewish people. Give me and Your church the same love, free of pride and contempt. I pray that many Israelis and Jews abroad would come to know You as their Messiah and Lord. Open the way for conversations that are gentle and honest, and let my own life show Your kindness. Do not let me grow tired of praying for the people You first loved and called. Amen.

### 12. Born in Bethlehem

> *But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.*
>
> — **Micah 5:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You were born in Bethlehem Ephratah, as Micah foretold, and Your goings forth are from of old. I ask You to bring the prophets' words to Jewish readers with fresh clarity, so they see that the ruler promised in Israel is You. Let Micah's promise be read in synagogues, in homes and online, and let the Holy Spirit open eyes to the One who was born to rule. Show them Your humility and Your power. Amen.

### 13. A Prophet like Moses

> *The LORD thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken;*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 18:15** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Moses said that the Lord would raise up a Prophet from among his brethren, like himself, and that Israel should listen to Him. I pray that Jewish people would hear You, the Prophet greater than Moses. Take away every fear of betrayal or loss that keeps them from listening, and give them courage to ask honest questions of the Torah. Let those who read Moses in synagogue see that he wrote about You. Amen.

### 14. We have found Him

> *Philip findeth Nathanael, and saith unto him, We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, did write, Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.*
>
> — **John 1:45** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Philip told Nathanael, We have found Him of whom Moses and the prophets wrote. Raise up Jewish believers today who say the same to their families, their friends and their neighbours, with joy and without shame. Give them wisdom, patience and love when they are misunderstood or rejected. Draw Nathanaels who are honest and searching, and let them come and see. Bless the Jewish believers in Israel and in the diaspora, and keep them faithful. Amen.

### 15. Weeping over Jerusalem

> <sup>41</sup> And when he was come near, he beheld the city, and wept over it, <sup>42</sup> Saying, If thou hadst known, even thou, at least in this thy day, the things which belong unto thy peace! but now they are hid from thine eyes.
>
> — **Luke 19:41-42** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You wept over Jerusalem and longed for her to know the things that belong to her peace. I weep with You and ask that this day be a day of knowing. Lift the veil from the people of Jerusalem, and from every Jewish heart, and let them find in You the peace that no treaty can bring. Forgive me for the times I have prayed without tears. Bring many to see You and be saved. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I commit Israel, the Jewish people everywhere and all who live in Judea and Samaria into Your hands. Protect the innocent, comfort the grieving, give wisdom to leaders and restrain those who plan violence. Draw the Jewish people to know You as Messiah and Lord, and make me faithful in prayer and love until You come. Amen.*
