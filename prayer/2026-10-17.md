## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Wisdom for Israel's voters and leaders, with the election ten days away

**In the news:** [ToI poll: Eisenkot's Yashar widens lead over Likud, but both blocs short of a majority](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/toi-poll-eisenkot-opens-up-big-lead-on-netanyahu-but-both-blocs-short-of-majority); [Report says Netanyahu opted against targeting Hamas leaders weeks before Oct. 7](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-15-2026)

> *And he changeth the times and the seasons: he removeth kings, and setteth up kings: he giveth wisdom unto the wise, and knowledge to them that know understanding:*
>
> — **Daniel 2:21** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You change the times and the seasons, and You remove kings and set up kings. With Israel's election on 27 October and polls showing neither side close to a majority, I ask You to give wisdom to voters, to those who lead, and to those who must form a government afterward. Keep the campaign honest and the vote free from fear. Give understanding to the wise and humility to all who seek authority, so that whoever governs will seek the good of every person in the land. I trust the outcome to Your hand. Amen.

### 2. Calm and protection while the army is on higher alert

**In the news:** [Israel raises military alert over possible Iran or Hezbollah escalation ahead of election](https://turkiyetoday.com/region/israel-raises-military-alert-over-possible-iran-hezbollah-escalation-report-3230419)

> <sup>1</sup> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. <sup>2</sup> Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea; <sup>3</sup> Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof. Selah.
>
> — **Psalm 46:1-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a very present help in trouble. I thank You that Israel's military has raised its alert over a possible escalation with Iran or Hezbollah, and I ask You to keep the people of the north and all Israelis from fear. Where danger is real, restrain those who plan violence, and give calm and clear judgment to commanders and ministers. Where the alarm is only rumour, let it fade. Be the refuge of soldiers, reservists and families who wait anxiously, so that the earth may shake and Your people still not be moved. Amen.

### 3. Peace on the Lebanon border and the people of southern Lebanon

**In the news:** [Is Israel signalling a wider presence in southern Lebanon?](https://english.aawsat.com/arab-world/5330380-israel-signaling-wider-occupation-southern-lebanon)

> *Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.*
>
> — **Matthew 5:9** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You called the peacemakers blessed. I pray for the Israelis living near the Lebanon border and for the civilians of southern Lebanon, as Israel's defence minister speaks of widening a security zone. Raise up peacemakers on every side, and give restraint to those who hold weapons and wisdom to those who decide. Protect the innocent from harm, comfort families who have lost loved ones, and turn leaders toward the path of peace rather than a wider war. Let Your Spirit make peacemakers of us all. Amen.

### 4. Justice and safety during the olive harvest in Judea and Samaria

**In the news:** [Settlers attack Palestinian olive harvesters near Ramallah; Washington Post photographer injured](https://nbcnews.com/world/middle-east/israeli-settlers-burn-olive-trees-injure-journalist-west-bank-attack-rcna603864)

> *He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the LORD require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?*
>
> — **Micah 6:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You require us to do justly, to love mercy and to walk humbly with God. I bring You the olive harvest near Ramallah, where Palestinian farmers and a journalist were reported attacked and the army says it will examine what happened. Protect harvesters, shepherds and villagers, and protect Israeli soldiers and neighbours from violence too. Give the authorities courage to establish the truth and uphold justice, and soften the hearts of those who strike others. Let mercy and humility grow in that land. Amen.

### 5. Jews abroad who feel threatened, and the voters far from home

**In the news:** [Lithuania's Jewish leader warns of rising antisemitism after three bomb threats against a synagogue](https://ynetnews.com/jewish-world/article/sjapskojmg) (earlier this week); [Trump addresses Michigan synagogue at a DOJ-backed antisemitism event](https://jpost.com/diaspora/article-911886) (earlier this week); [ToI poll: Eisenkot's Yashar widens lead over Likud, but both blocs short of a majority](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/toi-poll-eisenkot-opens-up-big-lead-on-netanyahu-but-both-blocs-short-of-majority)

> *Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.*
>
> — **1 Peter 5:7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You care for us and invite us to cast all our care on You. I pray for Jewish communities abroad who live with threats, like the Lithuanian synagogue that received bomb threats, and for those who feel politicised by public events, as in Michigan. Give them security and peace in their homes and houses of prayer. Strengthen those who guard them, and bless the Israelis voting at missions abroad. Let no one carry fear alone, and draw every anxious heart to Yourself. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Dwelling in the shadow of the Almighty

> <sup>1</sup> He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. <sup>2</sup> I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
>
> — **Psalm 91:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask that Your people in Israel and across the world would dwell in the secret place of the Most High and rest in Your shadow. Be their refuge and fortress in the night and in the day. Guard families in their homes, children on their way to school, and elderly people in shelters. Grant them a quiet confidence in You that does not depend on circumstances, and let them say with peace, My God, in You I trust. Amen.

### 7. The Lord who fights for His people

> <sup>13</sup> And Moses said unto the people, Fear ye not, stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD, which he will shew to you to day: for the Egyptians whom ye have seen to day, ye shall see them again no more for ever. <sup>14</sup> The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.
>
> — **Exodus 14:13-14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, as You stood between Israel and the army at the sea, stand between Your people and every threat today. Give the soldiers and reservists steady courage, and tell the fearful not to be afraid. Where the way seems closed, open a path. Let Israel see Your salvation again, and let those who watch from the nations understand that You are faithful to Your covenant. I ask for deliverance, and I ask for peace. Amen.

### 8. A strong refuge for the nation and its capital

> *The LORD is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower.*
>
> — **Psalm 18:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a rock, a fortress, a deliverer, a shield and a high tower. I ask You to be all of these for Jerusalem and for every city and village of Israel. Protect those who guard the borders, the police and rescue workers, and every civilian who hears sirens. Cover the hospitals, the schools and the places of worship. Let Israel know that You are near, and let Your goodness reach all who live in the land. Amen.

### 9. Unity and keeping for God's people

> *And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.*
>
> — **John 17:11** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You prayed that the Father would keep through His name those He gave You, and that they might be one. I pray for Jewish people everywhere, in Israel and in the diaspora, divided by politics and tested by hatred. Keep them by Your name. Bring unity where there is bitterness, and protection where there is danger. Protect Jewish believers in You, and let Your own love be known among all Your people. Amen.

### 10. The peace of God guarding hearts and minds

> *And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.*
>
> — **Philippians 4:7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask that Your peace, which passes understanding, would keep the hearts and minds of the bereaved, the wounded, hostages' families, and soldiers who cannot sleep. Where trauma is deep, bring healing. Where grief is fresh, bring comfort. Surround Jewish families living in fear, in Israel and abroad, with a peace that the news cannot take away, and keep them in You. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The new covenant written on the heart

> <sup>33</sup> But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the LORD, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people. <sup>34</sup> And they shall teach no more every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the LORD: for they shall all know me, from the least of them unto the greatest of them, saith the LORD: for I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more.
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:33-34** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the One who makes the new covenant with the house of Israel. I ask You to write Your law on Jewish hearts, to be their God, and to let them know You from the least to the greatest. Forgive their iniquity and remember their sin no more, as You promised. Let this be true for many in Israel and in every nation where they live, as I pray in the love of Romans 10:1. Amen.

### 12. The child born, the Prince of Peace

> *For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.*
>
> — **Isaiah 9:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the child born and the Son given, Wonderful, Counsellor, the Mighty God, the Everlasting Father, the Prince of Peace. Open the eyes of Your people Israel to see You in their own prophets. Let Isaiah's words be read with understanding in synagogues and homes, and let the government that rests on Your shoulder be known as peace for all. I ask for tender, humble faith to rise among Your people. Amen.

### 13. Good tidings of great joy for all people

> <sup>10</sup> And the angel said unto them, Fear not: for, behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people. <sup>11</sup> For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.
>
> — **Luke 2:10-11** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You were born in the city of David as a Saviour, Christ the Lord, and the angel's news was first for Israel's shepherds. I pray that Jewish people today would hear those good tidings with joy and not fear. Send faithful messengers who love them and who speak gently and truthfully. Where hearts are weary of war, let Your birth, life and promise bring a living hope. Amen.

### 14. Calling on the name of the Lord

> <sup>13</sup> For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved. <sup>14</sup> How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher?
>
> — **Romans 10:13-14** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, whoever calls on Your name will be saved. Send preachers, neighbours and friends to Jewish people in Israel and abroad, so that they may hear and believe and call on You. Give those who share Your good news a love like Paul's, free from pride and from contempt. Open doors for honest conversation, and let Your Spirit stir hunger for the Messiah in many. Amen.

### 15. The veil taken away

> <sup>15</sup> But even unto this day, when Moses is read, the vail is upon their heart. <sup>16</sup> Nevertheless when it shall turn to the Lord, the vail shall be taken away.
>
> — **2 Corinthians 3:15-16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask You to take away the veil from Jewish hearts when Moses is read. Let them turn to You, the Lord, and see the One of whom the Law and the Prophets speak. Bless Jewish believers in You, in Israel and around the world, with courage, joy and a clear witness to their families. Let the turning of Your people be a blessing to the nations. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring You Israel, the Jewish people in the land and in every nation, and the people of Judea and Samaria. Keep the innocent, restrain violence, give wisdom to leaders, and bring peace to the borders. Heal the grieving, and draw Your people to Yourself as their Messiah and Lord. I trust all of this to You, who are faithful. Amen.*
