Draw them together, Lord, and draw them to You.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Dwelling safely in the land

> *And they shall no more be a prey to the heathen, neither shall the beast of the land devour them; but they shall dwell safely, and none shall make them afraid.*
>
> — **Ezekiel 34:28** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Good Shepherd, and through Ezekiel You promised that Your people would no more be a prey, but would dwell safely with none to make them afraid. I ask You to guard the land of Israel and its borders north and south, in the Galilee, the Negev and the Golan. Keep farms, kibbutzim and towns from rockets, drones and infiltration. Let children go to school and parents go to work without terror. Watch over the hills of Judea and Samaria and over every innocent person who lives there. Be the shepherd who keeps His flock, and let Your people know that their safety comes from You.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians covered in the day of battle

> *O GOD the Lord, the strength of my salvation, thou hast covered my head in the day of battle.*
>
> — **Psalm 140:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the psalmist said, O God the Lord, the strength of my salvation, thou hast covered my head in the day of battle. I pray for the soldiers of Israel on the Lebanon border, in Gaza and in Judea and Samaria, and for police and first responders in the cities. Cover their heads and keep them from harm and from doing wrong. Strengthen reservists and their families who carry long months of service. I pray too for civilians in shelters and on the roads, for the wounded in hospitals, and for those living with trauma. Be their strength, Lord, and their salvation.

### 8. Jerusalem defended by the LORD

> *In that day shall the LORD defend the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and he that is feeble among them at that day shall be as David; and the house of David shall be as God, as the angel of the LORD before them.*
>
> — **Zechariah 12:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through Zechariah You promised that in that day the LORD would defend the inhabitants of Jerusalem and that the feeble among them would be as David. I pray for Jerusalem, the city where You taught, suffered, died and rose again. Defend its residents, Jewish and Arab, from terror and from every plan of violence. Strengthen the weak and the elderly, guard its holy places, and keep its streets, buses and markets safe. Give its police and leaders wisdom and fairness. Let Jerusalem become a city of peace, and let Your presence be known there.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora numbered and known by God

> <sup>29</sup> Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing? and one of them shall not fall on the ground without your Father. <sup>30</sup> But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. <sup>31</sup> Fear ye not therefore, ye are of more value than many sparrows.
>
> — **Matthew 10:29-31** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said not one sparrow falls to the ground without the Father, and that the very hairs of our heads are all numbered. I pray for Jewish people in Germany, Britain, France, Russia, Ukraine, Australia, the Americas and Africa, many of whom live with threats against their synagogues, schools and homes. Remind them that they are of more value than many sparrows. Protect families walking to synagogue, students on campus and the elderly living alone. Give governments resolve to guard them, and give neighbours courage to stand beside them. Let them not fear, Lord, because You see and You care.

### 10. God's covenant faithfulness that cannot fail

> *If we believe not, yet he abideth faithful: he cannot deny himself.*
>
> — **2 Timothy 2:13** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says that if we believe not, yet You abide faithful: You cannot deny Yourself. I thank You that Your faithfulness to Israel does not rest on the strength or goodness of any generation but on Your own unchanging character. Keep the promises You made to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Preserve the Jewish people through every threat, as You have preserved them through the centuries. When they feel forgotten or alone, let them discover that You have never let go. Show Yourself faithful in this generation, Lord, so that many will trust You.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Scales falling from the eyes

> *And immediately there fell from his eyes as it had been scales: and he received sight forthwith, and arose, and was baptized.*
>
> — **Acts 9:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You met Saul of Tarsus, a zealous Jew, on the road to Damascus, and immediately there fell from his eyes as it had been scales; he received sight, arose and was baptized. I ask You to do the same for Jewish men and women in Israel and in every nation today. Let the scales fall from their eyes so they see You as Messiah and Lord. Meet sincere seekers, religious and secular alike, in Tel Aviv, Bnei Brak, New York, London and Kampala. Reveal Yourself through Your word, through dreams and through the kindness of Your people, and let many arise and follow You.

### 12. The Ruler born in Bethlehem

> *But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.*
>
> — **Micah 5:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Micah foretold that out of little Bethlehem Ephratah would come the Ruler in Israel, whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting. You were born in Bethlehem, just as the prophet said. Open the hearts of Jewish readers of the Tanakh to see that this promise points to You. Let rabbis, students and families reading the prophets be stirred to ask who this eternal Ruler is. As the season of Hanukkah and Christmas approaches, let the light of Your birth shine on Israel, and let many recognise their King.

### 13. Looking on Him whom they pierced, with repentance

> *And I will pour upon the house of David, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the spirit of grace and of supplications: and they shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him, as one mourneth for his only son, and shall be in bitterness for him, as one that is in bitterness for his firstborn.*
>
> — **Zechariah 12:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through Zechariah You promised to pour upon the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem the spirit of grace and of supplications, so that they would look upon Me whom they have pierced and mourn. You are the One who was pierced for us. Pour out that spirit of grace on the Jewish people now. Bring them to sincere repentance and to the forgiveness You purchased on the cross. Let mourning turn to joy as they find in You the Lamb of God. And let me, a Gentile who has received mercy, pray for them with humility and love.

### 14. Faithful messengers sent with good news

> <sup>14</sup> How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher? <sup>15</sup> And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!
>
> — **Romans 10:14-15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Paul asked how they shall believe in Him of whom they have not heard, and how they shall hear without a preacher. Send faithful messengers to the Jewish people: Messianic congregations in Israel, believers in the diaspora, Arab Christians in the land, and friends of Israel from Africa and Uganda. Give them the beautiful feet of those who bring glad tidings of peace. Make their lives gentle and their words clear. Protect them from harm and discouragement, and let their witness be marked by love, honesty and respect for the Jewish people.

### 15. Jewish believers who confess Jesus as King of Israel

> *Nathanael answered and saith unto him, Rabbi, thou art the Son of God; thou art the King of Israel.*
>
> — **John 1:49** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, when Nathanael met You he said, Rabbi, thou art the Son of God; thou art the King of Israel. I thank You for Jewish believers today who make the same confession. Strengthen them where they face rejection from family, community or employers. Keep them rooted in Your word and joined in love with believers from the nations. Give them courage to share their faith and wisdom to live it well. Multiply their number, Lord, until many more of Israel confess You as Son of God and King.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I thank You that You hear the prayers of Your people. Keep Israel and the Jewish people everywhere under Your hand, give wisdom to their leaders, comfort to the grieving and peace to every innocent person in the land. Above all, open their eyes to know You as Messiah and Lord, that all Israel may be saved. I ask this in Your holy name. Amen.*
