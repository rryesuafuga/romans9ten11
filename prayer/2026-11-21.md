## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Eisenkot's search for a government amid stalled talks

**In the news:** [Eisenkot outlines three paths to a government, open to Arab-party backing](https://jta.org/2026/11/20/israel/citing-urgency-eisenkot-outlines-3-paths-to-government-signaling-openness-to-arab-party-backing); [Eisenkot reported ready to seek a Knesset vote on a minority "national emergency government"](https://timesofisrael.com/eisenkot-said-planning-vote-on-new-government-before-mandate-ends-even-without-majority)

> *A man’s heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps.*
>
> — **Proverbs 16:9** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I bring You Gadi Eisenkot, Benjamin Netanyahu and every Knesset member as coalition talks remain stalled. You say a man's heart devises his way, but that You direct his steps. Direct the steps of these leaders. Give them humility, honest hearts and wisdom to put the good of all the people above ambition or pride. Where there is deadlock, open a way that serves justice and peace. Guard Israel from the dangers of a long political vacuum while soldiers and families wait for steady leadership. Keep this nation under Your hand, Lord Jesus, and let Your purposes stand.

### 2. Pressure to resume war with Iran

**In the news:** [Haaretz analysis: Netanyahu pushing to resume the Iran front while the IDF is sceptical](https://haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-11-20/ty-article/.premium/warmongering-netanyahu-is-pushing-to-strike-iran-but-the-idf-remains-skeptical/000001a1-f527-d9ff-a7b5-f5afb06d0000)

> <sup>6</sup> My soul hath long dwelt with him that hateth peace. <sup>7</sup> I am for peace: but when I speak, they are for war.
>
> — **Psalm 120:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Haaretz reports pressure to reopen the Iran front, while others fear another full war. Like the psalmist, my soul has dwelt long with those who hate peace, and I ask You for peace. Give Israel's leaders and military chiefs clear judgment and restraint before any decision that could cost many lives. Restrain those who plan violence, and protect the innocent on every side: Israeli families, Iranian civilians and soldiers who must carry out orders. Prince of Peace, make peacemakers of those in power, and keep the region from the grief of renewed war.

### 3. Soldiers and civilians on the Lebanon border

**In the news:** [Israel says it struck a former Hezbollah missile site in eastern Lebanon](https://english.aawsat.com/arab-world/5343521-israel-says-struck-hezbollah-weapons-site-east-lebanon); [Hezbollah reportedly wants to stay out of a new Iran war as Lebanon moves against it](https://algemeiner.com/2026/11/20/hezbollah-wants-out-next-iran-war-israel-threatens-retaliation-lebanon-moves-against-terror-group)

> *If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.*
>
> — **Romans 12:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the IDF has struck again in eastern Lebanon, and Israel holds a buffer zone in the south while Hezbollah refuses to disarm. Your word says that, if it is possible, as much as lies in us, we should live peaceably with all men. I pray for peace on this border. Protect the soldiers who serve there and the families in northern Israel, and shelter ordinary Lebanese from harm. Give Lebanon's government courage to act, give Israel's leaders wisdom, and let the ceasefire become a lasting peace. Be the shield of every life at risk.

### 4. Justice and safety in Judea and Samaria

**In the news:** [Board of Peace official warns Gaza could be handed to the UN if Israeli "intransigence" continues](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-20-2026)

> *That which is altogether just shalt thou follow, that thou mayest live, and inherit the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 16:20** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the family of the Palestinian man shot dead by a settlement security coordinator in the central West Bank. Comfort them in their grief. Your word commands that which is altogether just to be followed, so I ask that the IDF review be honest and complete, and that truth and justice be served. Restrain violence from every side, protect farmers, shepherds, settlers and soldiers alike, and soften hearts that harbour hatred. Lord Jesus, You love all people made in Your image. Bring peace and fair dealing to the hills of Judea and Samaria, and use Your people there as peacemakers.

### 5. A damaged synagogue in Ontario

**In the news:** [Police seek suspect after significant damage to a synagogue in Richmond Hill, Ontario](https://jns.org/wire/the-focus-project/collective-blame-wrongly-pushes-jews-from-areas-of-public-life)

> *Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.*
>
> — **Romans 12:21** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, police in Ontario are seeking the person who did significant damage to a synagogue in Richmond Hill. I pray for that congregation and for Jewish families across Canada who feel unsafe in their own place of worship. Help the police find the one responsible, and let justice be done. Comfort those who are shaken. Your word says not to be overcome by evil but to overcome evil with good. Let kindness from neighbours and officials meet this hatred, and soften the heart of whoever did it. Keep every synagogue in safety, and give Your people courage.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The Keeper who neither slumbers nor sleeps

> <sup>3</sup> He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber. <sup>4</sup> Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.
>
> — **Psalm 121:3-4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Keeper of Israel who neither slumbers nor sleeps. I thank You that no night is too dark for Your watching eye. Keep the soldiers on duty tonight, the families in border towns and the people in shelters. Guard the land and its borders from rockets, drones, infiltrators and every plan of the enemy. Steady the watchmen, strengthen the weary, and give Israel's people peaceful sleep. Keep Your promise to Your people, Lord Jesus, and be their protector every hour of the day and night.

### 7. The apple of His eye

> *For thus saith the LORD of hosts; After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you: for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.*
>
> — **Zechariah 2:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that whoever touches Your people touches the apple of Your eye. I lift up Jews in every country, in Canada, Europe, America, Australia, Africa and everywhere they live, who face threats, vandalism and fear. Protect families, children, teachers, rabbis and shopkeepers. Move police and governments to defend them. Turn the hearts of those who hate. I ask that Your people know they are precious to You, and that Your protecting love be seen over them in every city.

### 8. Casting every care on Him

> *Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.*
>
> — **1 Peter 5:7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You care for us, and I cast on You the heavy cares of Israel: the soldiers' mothers, the bereaved, the wounded in hospital, the people who cannot sleep from fear and the leaders carrying decisions of life and death. Lift this weight from their shoulders. Give those who mourn real comfort, give the injured healing, and give the anxious peace in their minds. Meet each person where they are, Lord Jesus, and let them feel that You carry them.

### 9. Peace that the world cannot give

> *Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.*
>
> — **John 14:27** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You left Your peace with us, not as the world gives. I ask for that peace on Jerusalem and on every home in Israel, in the middle of rumours of war and political uncertainty. Let hearts not be troubled or afraid. Calm the streets, the schools, the buses and the markets. Where fear drives people to anger, give Your peace that guards the heart. Make Israel's neighbours and Israel's own citizens people of peace, Prince of Peace, and rule over this land with Your quiet strength.

### 10. Never forgotten, engraved on His hands

> <sup>15</sup> Can a woman forget her sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? yea, they may forget, yet will I not forget thee. <sup>16</sup> Behold, I have graven thee upon the palms of my hands; thy walls are continually before me.
>
> — **Isaiah 49:15-16** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You say You will not forget Your people, and that You have engraved them on the palms of Your hands, with their walls always before You. Whatever the news shows, I thank You that Your covenant faithfulness does not depend on the strength of any government. Hold Jerusalem and every Jewish community in Your hands. Rebuild what is broken, gather the scattered, and show Your steadfast love to the generations. Keep Your promises, faithful Lord, and let Israel know it is remembered by You.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The Servant wounded for our transgressions

> <sup>5</sup> But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed. <sup>6</sup> All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned every one to his own way; and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.
>
> — **Isaiah 53:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask that Jewish people everywhere, in Israel and across the world, see You in Isaiah 53 as the One wounded for our transgressions, by whose stripes we are healed. Open the eyes of many to read this chapter with fresh hearts. Let them see that You carried the iniquity of us all. Give them hunger for the Scriptures and peace in You. Draw them in love, not pressure, and let the sheep who have gone astray find the Shepherd who laid down His life.

### 12. The righteous Branch of David

> <sup>5</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the LORD, that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth. <sup>6</sup> In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell safely: and this is his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS.
>
> — **Jeremiah 23:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the righteous Branch raised for David, a King who reigns and executes justice, and Your name is The LORD Our Righteousness. I pray that Jewish people who long for the Messiah would recognise You as that King. Where Israel is weary of political leaders and unfinished promises, point hearts to the One who will make Judah safe and Israel dwell in security. Come as Messiah to those who wait, and let them see Your righteousness and be saved.

### 13. No other name

> *Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.*
>
> — **Acts 4:12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, there is salvation in no other name under heaven among men. I pray with love for my Jewish friends and neighbours, and for those I do not know, that they hear Your name and know You as Messiah. Remove every obstacle of fear, of history and of hurt that has kept people from You. Forgive Christians for contempt and failure toward Jewish people, and make us gracious witnesses. Let many in Israel and the diaspora call on You and be saved.

### 14. Confess with the mouth, believe in the heart

> <sup>9</sup> That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. <sup>10</sup> For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.
>
> — **Romans 10:9-10** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, my heart's desire is that Israel be saved. Your word says that whoever confesses You as Lord and believes in the heart that God raised You from the dead will be saved. Bring Jewish people, in Israel and in every country, to this faith by Your Spirit. Give courage to those who secretly believe, strengthen Jewish believers who face rejection from family and community, and let their confession be bold and joyful. Make Yourself known, Lord Jesus.

### 15. Repentance and forgiveness beginning at Jerusalem

> <sup>46</sup> And said unto them, Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead the third day: <sup>47</sup> And that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.
>
> — **Luke 24:46-47** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You rose from the dead and sent Your followers to preach repentance and forgiveness of sins in Your name, beginning at Jerusalem. I pray for that city today. Send faithful witnesses, Jewish and Gentile, who speak with humility and love. Let Your mercy reach hearts in Jerusalem, in Judea and Samaria, in Tel Aviv and in diaspora communities, and let many receive forgiveness and new life in You. Fulfil Your word, Lord Jesus, and bring Your people home to You.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring all these requests to You and thank You that You hear. Protect Israel and Jewish people everywhere, bring comfort to the grieving, wisdom to the leaders and peace to the land. Open the hearts of Your people Israel to know You as Messiah and Lord, and fulfil Your promises in Your time. To You be the glory, now and forever. Amen.*
