## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Wisdom for Israel's voters as the election nears

**In the news:** [Polls: blocs struggle to reach a Knesset majority as Eisenkot leads Netanyahu](https://jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912004); [Former chief rabbi Yitzhak Yosef tells followers not to vote for Ben Gvir](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-18-2026)

> *A man’s heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps.*
>
> — **Proverbs 16:9** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, eight days before Israel votes, polls show no bloc near a majority and voters are weighing their choices, rabbis are giving guidance, and the campaign is tense. A man's heart deviseth his way, but You direct his steps, so I ask You to direct the steps of every voter, candidate and election worker. Give people honest hearts and clear sight, keep the process fair and peaceful, and let no one be pressured or frightened away from the ballot. Whatever government is formed, make it wise, humble and willing to seek peace. Lord Jesus, rule over this election for the good of all who live in the land. Amen.

### 2. Strength and peace while leaders warn of escalation in Lebanon

**In the news:** [Defense Minister Katz says Israel would seize more Lebanese land if Hezbollah or Iran attack](https://jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-911722)

> *The LORD will give strength unto his people; the LORD will bless his people with peace.*
>
> — **Psalm 29:11** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Israel's defence minister has spoken of taking more ground in southern Lebanon if Hezbollah or Iran attack, and families in the north and soldiers on the border are living under that tension. The LORD will give strength unto his people and bless his people with peace, so I ask You to give strength to the soldiers and calm to their parents, wives and children. Restrain those who plan violence, give wisdom to commanders and ministers, and protect innocent people on both sides of the border from harm. Turn the threat of war into lasting quiet. Lord Jesus, be the peace of that frontier. Amen.

### 3. Justice for victims of violence in Judea and Samaria

**In the news:** [Settlers attack Palestinians and a Washington Post photographer during the olive harvest](https://washingtonpost.com/world/2026/10/16/palestinians-washington-post-photographer-attacked-west-bank)

> *But let judgment run down as waters, and righteousness as a mighty stream.*
>
> — **Amos 5:24** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, during the olive harvest, villagers and a photographer were reported beaten in the West Bank, and the army condemned the violence. I ask You to comfort the injured and frightened, and let judgment run down as waters and righteousness as a mighty stream. Move the authorities to investigate honestly and protect every family, Jewish and Arab, working its land. Bring conviction and repentance to those who attack, and keep soldiers and neighbours from revenge. Lord Jesus, bring justice that heals rather than deepens the wounds. Amen.

### 4. Peace after the attack near Taybeh

**In the news:** [Belgium says settlers attacked Palestinians and European diplomats near Taybeh](https://abc.net.au/news/2026-10-18/israeli-settler-attack-palestinians-european-diplomats-west-bank/107280242)

> <sup>17</sup> But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy. <sup>18</sup> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.
>
> — **James 3:17-18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Belgium says masked settlers attacked Palestinians and European diplomats near the Christian village of Taybeh, and Israeli leaders are disagreeing publicly about what happened. Your word says the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, and that the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace. I pray for the people of Taybeh, for the diplomats, and for the many Israelis who are ashamed of the attack. Give leaders the courage to tell the truth and act on it, protect the Christians of that village, and make peacemakers of those who are angry. Lord Jesus, give Your people wisdom that is pure and peaceable. Amen.

### 5. Calm for the Jewish community in Paris and across France

**In the news:** [Paris synagogue and Jewish-linked sites damaged during riots; antisemitic motive unclear](https://jpost.com/diaspora/article-912093)

> *And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to the which also ye are called in one body; and be ye thankful.*
>
> — **Colossians 3:15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, a Paris synagogue and other Jewish-linked sites were damaged in the weekend riots, and community leaders say it looks like robbery while urging that no one spread unverified claims. Let the peace of God rule in the hearts of the Jews of France, who live with real fear, and let them be thankful for every friend who stands with them. Grant truthful reporting, wise security officials and swift justice for every criminal. Guard every synagogue, school and family, and let truth rather than rumour guide us. Lord Jesus, rule in their hearts with Your peace. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The apple of His eye

> *For thus saith the LORD of hosts; After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you: for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.*
>
> — **Zechariah 2:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the LORD of hosts, and You said that whoever touches Your people touches the apple of Your eye. I place the people of Israel, in every city and village, under Your watchful care. Guard children going to school, workers on the roads, families in shelters and farmers in the fields. Frustrate every plan to harm them, and keep the whole nation, and all innocent people around it, in the shadow of Your wing. Lord Jesus, let none of Your people be forgotten. Amen.

### 7. Watchmen on Jerusalem's walls

> <sup>6</sup> I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night: ye that make mention of the LORD, keep not silence, <sup>7</sup> And give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth.
>
> — **Isaiah 62:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have set watchmen upon the walls of Jerusalem, and I join them in prayer today. Protect the city, its residents and its pilgrims, and give peace to the neighbourhoods of Jews, Arabs and Christians who share it. Give wisdom to the police, the guards and the leaders responsible for its security. Make Jerusalem a praise in the earth, a city known for Your name and not for fear. Lord Jesus, I will not keep silent until You do it. Amen.

### 8. Angels around soldiers and civilians

> *For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.*
>
> — **Psalm 91:11** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You give Your angels charge over Your people, to keep them in all their ways. I pray this for every soldier on the northern, southern and eastern borders, and for reservists called away from their families. Keep them in all their ways, steady their courage and bring them home safe. Keep also the civilians in range of rockets and drones, the sick, the elderly and the children. Lord Jesus, give Your angels charge over them and keep us from the fear that paralyses. Amen.

### 9. Kept by the power of God

> *Who are kept by the power of God through faith unto salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.*
>
> — **1 Peter 1:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for Jews in every nation, in France, Britain, America, Africa, Australia and beyond, who feel exposed in their own streets. You keep Your people by the power of God, and I ask You to keep them in faith and in safety. Strengthen their synagogues, schools and neighbours, raise up friends who will stand with them, and restrain hatred before it becomes violence. Lord Jesus, hold each Jewish family in Your hand. Amen.

### 10. Delivered from every evil work

> *And the Lord shall deliver me from every evil work, and will preserve me unto his heavenly kingdom: to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.*
>
> — **2 Timothy 4:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are faithful to deliver and to preserve. I thank You that You keep covenant with Israel, and that none of the nation's enemies can undo what You have promised. Deliver Israel from every evil work: from terror, from hatred, from division and from pride. Preserve the nation until the day Your kingdom is seen in full. Lord Jesus, to You be glory for ever and ever. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. A clean heart

> *Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.*
>
> — **Psalm 51:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I long for Israel, and for every Jewish person, to know the cleansing You give. Create in them a clean heart and renew a right spirit within them, as David asked. Where there is hurt, history or hardness, bring a gentle opening to hear Your voice. Let them know that forgiveness is not earned but given by the Messiah who loves them. Lord Jesus, do the quiet work in hearts that no argument can do. Amen.

### 12. They shall look on Him

> *Behold, I will bring it health and cure, and I will cure them, and will reveal unto them the abundance of peace and truth.*
>
> — **Jeremiah 33:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to bring health and cure, and to reveal to Your people the abundance of peace and truth. I pray for that healing for Israel, which has known so much grief, and above all the healing of knowing You. Open the eyes of Your people to see You as the promised Messiah, and let sorrow turn into joy, as a child comforted by a Father's love. Lord Jesus, reveal Your peace and truth to Jerusalem and to every Jewish heart. Amen.

### 13. Repent and be refreshed

> *Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord;*
>
> — **Acts 3:19** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Peter told his own people to repent and be converted, that their sins might be blotted out and times of refreshing come from Your presence. I ask that many Jewish people hear that invitation today and receive it with joy. Send gentle, honest witnesses to them, and let every word be spoken in love and not pressure. Lord Jesus, blot out sin, bring refreshing to weary hearts and make them Your own. Amen.

### 14. We have found the Messiah

> *But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through his name.*
>
> — **John 20:31** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, these things are written that Jewish people might believe that You are the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing they might have life through Your name. I pray for Jewish believers in You, in Israel and around the world, that they would share this same joyful witness with their families and friends. Protect them from loneliness and rejection, strengthen their congregations, and use their testimony. Lord Jesus, let many find life in Your name. Amen.

### 15. A heart of sorrow for my people

> <sup>1</sup> I say the truth in Christ, I lie not, my conscience also bearing me witness in the Holy Ghost, <sup>2</sup> That I have great heaviness and continual sorrow in my heart.
>
> — **Romans 9:1-2** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, like Paul I carry heaviness and sorrow for my kinsmen in the Jewish people, and I want my prayer to be one of love, never of contempt. Give those of us who follow You the same tender heart, free of pride, and a deep thankfulness that salvation came through the Jews. Move many in Israel and the diaspora toward You, and keep us faithful in prayer until we see it. Lord Jesus, hear a heart that breaks for Israel. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring all this before You: the election in Israel, soldiers and families on the borders, the villages and diplomats near Taybeh, and Jewish communities in Paris and everywhere. Protect Your people, restrain the violent, and comfort the grieving. Above all, open the eyes of Israel to know You as Messiah and Lord, as Romans 10:1 teaches me to pray. You are worthy; hear us. Amen.*
