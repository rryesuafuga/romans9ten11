## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. A refuge in the secret place

> <sup>1</sup> He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. <sup>2</sup> I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
>
> — **Psalm 91:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask You to be the refuge and fortress of Israel and of every Jewish family. Let all who dwell in the secret place of the Most High abide under the shadow of the Almighty. Cover the cities, villages and farms of Israel, and the homes of Jews in every nation, with Your protection day and night. Where fear has settled on a family, let trust in You take its place. Amen.

### 7. The shield of Israel's help

> *Happy art thou, O Israel: who is like unto thee, O people saved by the LORD, the shield of thy help, and who is the sword of thy excellency! and thine enemies shall be found liars unto thee; and thou shalt tread upon their high places.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 33:29** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the One who saves the people of Israel and the shield of their help. I pray for the soldiers, reservists and civilians who stand watch on the borders. Shield them in the hour of danger, bring them home safe to their families, and give them courage that rests in You and not in their own strength. Frustrate every plan to harm the innocent. Amen.

### 8. The Shepherd who gathers and keeps

> *Hear the word of the LORD, O ye nations, and declare it in the isles afar off, and say, He that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock.*
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You scattered Israel and You have promised to gather and keep her as a shepherd keeps a flock. Watch over Jews who have returned to the land and Jews still scattered among the nations. Keep the lonely, the elderly and the young in every country, and let them know that the Good Shepherd has not forgotten His people. Amen.

### 9. With them always

> *Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.*
>
> — **Matthew 28:20** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to be with Your people always, even to the end of the world. Be present in every synagogue, school and street where Jews feel afraid, and in every shelter where families wait for the alarm to end. Let the presence of the risen Messiah bring peace to their rooms and strength to their steps. Amen.

### 10. Cast down, but not destroyed

> <sup>8</sup> We are troubled on every side, yet not distressed; we are perplexed, but not in despair; <sup>9</sup> Persecuted, but not forsaken; cast down, but not destroyed;
>
> — **2 Corinthians 4:8-9** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, many of Your ancient people have been troubled on every side, persecuted, and cast down, yet God has not let them be destroyed. I thank You for Your covenant faithfulness through every age. Sustain Israel and the Jewish people in this generation, and let them not be forsaken or perplexed to despair. Raise up the weary, and keep Your promises alive among them. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The Prince of Peace

> <sup>6</sup> For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace. <sup>7</sup> Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom, to order it, and to establish it with judgment and with justice from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of the LORD of hosts will perform this.
>
> — **Isaiah 9:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the child born and the son given, Wonderful, Counsellor, the mighty God, the Prince of Peace. Open the eyes of Your people Israel to see You in the prophecy of Isaiah. Let Your government of peace and justice, upon the throne of David, be recognised as the hope of Israel, and let many Jews come to know You as their promised King. Amen.

### 12. The stone the builders refused

> <sup>22</sup> The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner. <sup>23</sup> This is the LORD's doing; it is marvellous in our eyes.
>
> — **Psalm 118:22-23** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the stone the builders rejected, now become the head of the corner. This is the Lord's doing, marvellous in our eyes. I pray that many in Israel and the diaspora will read this psalm and see You as the One God has set in honour. Lift every veil, and let the marvel of Your resurrection be believed. Amen.

### 13. The way, the truth and the life

> *Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.*
>
> — **John 14:6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that You are the way, the truth and the life, and that no one comes to the Father except by You. I pray with love for my Jewish friends and neighbours, and for all in Israel, that they may know the Father through You. Draw them gently, answer their honest questions, and give them the joy of knowing the Messiah. Amen.

### 14. Know assuredly that He is Lord and Christ

> <sup>36</sup> Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ. <sup>37</sup> Now when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles, Men and brethren, what shall we do? <sup>38</sup> Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.
>
> — **Acts 2:36-38** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, at Pentecost the house of Israel was told to know assuredly that You are both Lord and Christ, and many were pricked in their hearts and turned to You. Do the same now. Let the Holy Spirit convict, bring repentance and forgiveness, and give the gift of the Spirit to Jewish people who turn to You. Send faithful witnesses in love, with no contempt. Amen.

### 15. People who say, We will go with you

> *Thus saith the LORD of hosts; In those days it shall come to pass, that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, We will go with you: for we have heard that God is with you.*
>
> — **Zechariah 8:23** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised that people from all nations would take hold of a Jewish man and say, we will go with you, for God is with you. Strengthen Jewish believers in You, in Israel and around the world, to be a living witness. Protect them from rejection and fear, and make Your church love the Jewish people as You do. Bring many more to faith in You. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I commit Israel, the Jewish people everywhere, and the families of Judea and Samaria into Your hands. Comfort the grieving, protect the vulnerable, give wisdom to leaders, and restrain those who plan violence. Draw Your people Israel to know You as Messiah and Lord, and let Your peace come. In Your name I pray. Amen.*
