>
> — **Daniel 2:21** · KJV · Old Testament

Three days before Israel votes, with the final polls showing neither bloc holding a majority and one party withdrawing from the race, I ask You to give wisdom to every voter and understanding to every leader. Keep the campaign free of violence and fear, and let the result be received with peace. Raise up leaders who fear God, love justice and seek the good of all who live in the land. I entrust this election to You, for You alone are wise and rule over every nation. Amen.

### 2. The soldiers killed and the wounded after the Hebron attack

> *Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.*
>
> — **Matthew 5:4** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that those who mourn will be comforted. I pray for the families of the two soldiers reported killed near Hebron, and for the wounded and those who love them. Come close to the grieving parents, wives, husbands and children in the first hours of their loss. Heal the injured in body and mind, and comfort their comrades who saw it happen. Be the Man of sorrows who knows their pain, and walk with them through the valley. Give them Your own comfort, which no news cycle and no election can give. Amen.

About two thousand people in Hebron have been forced out of their homes, and I lift them up to You, especially the children, the elderly and the sick. Give them shelter, food and medicine tonight, and a safe way home. Show Your mercy to every innocent family in this conflict, whether Israeli or Palestinian. Restrain those who plan violence, and give the leaders making these decisions wisdom, justice and compassion. Let no one suffer for the crimes of others. Amen.

I pray for calm in every town and road, and for the farmers, workers and families whose lives are disrupted. Protect Israeli civilians and soldiers from attack, and protect Palestinian neighbours from reprisals. Soften hard hearts and give those in authority restraint and wisdom. Make Your peace more than a hope and let it become real where there is fear. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. A refuge in the shadow of the Almighty

> <sup>1</sup> He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. <sup>2</sup> I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
>
> — **Psalm 91:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the refuge and the fortress of all who trust in You. I ask You to cover the people of Israel with the shadow of the Almighty, in their homes, their schools and their streets. Hide them from the snare and the sudden attack. Teach every Israeli family to dwell in Your secret place, and to say of You, He is my refuge. Guard the borders and the cities, and bring true and lasting security that no weapon can give. In You I trust for them, and I do not stop. Amen.

### 7. Strength for soldiers and civilians

> *Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.*
>
> — **Isaiah 41:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You tell Your people not to fear, for You are with them. I pray for the soldiers on the borders and in Judea and Samaria, and for the civilians who wait for the next siren or the next phone call. Strengthen them, help them and uphold them with Your right hand. Keep them from despair, from rash decisions and from hatred. Protect their lives, their minds and their families, and bring every one of them home safe. Let the fear in their hearts give way to Your presence. Amen.

### 8. A very present help in trouble

> <sup>1</sup> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. <sup>2</sup> Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea;
>
> — **Psalm 46:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a very present help in trouble. When the ground seems to shake under Israel, with war on several fronts and talks that could fail, be the strength of this people. I pray for Jerusalem and for the cities of the north and south, that You would hold them firmly. Calm the fears that rise with every rumour of war. Give Israel's leaders clarity and restraint, and let Your people say with confidence, we will not fear. Be a refuge now, and do not let go. Amen.

### 9. The peace of God guarding hearts and minds

> <sup>6</sup> Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. <sup>7</sup> And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.
>
> — **Philippians 4:6-7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You invite us to bring every request to God with thanksgiving. I bring You the anxiety of Jewish families in Israel and in every diaspora city where fear has grown, such as the congregation in Ogden, Utah, where a bomb threat was foiled. Give them the peace of God that passes understanding, and let it guard their hearts and minds. Calm the nights of the bereaved, the soldiers and the children who have learned too much about danger. Protect Jewish communities from those who would harm them. Let Your peace stand guard where no human guard can reach. Amen.

### 10. The Lord is with them always

> *Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.*
>
> — **Matthew 28:20** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to be with Your people always, to the end of the world. I thank You that Your faithfulness does not depend on election results or polls. Be with the Jewish people in Israel and in every nation, in every hour of fear and every hour of joy. Keep Your covenant promises to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and remember Your mercy toward them. Remain at their side in difficult days, protect them from every harm, and bring them at last into the fullness of Your peace. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The Prince of Peace given to Israel

> *For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.*
>
> — **Isaiah 9:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the prophet foretold that a child would be born and a son given, and that His name would be Wonderful, Counsellor, the mighty God and the Prince of Peace. You are that Son, born into Israel. I ask You to reveal Yourself to Jewish people everywhere as the Prince of Peace they have longed for. Let them hear Isaiah's words with open hearts and see You in them. Give them the peace no treaty, army or election can give, and let many find it in You. Amen.

### 12. The new covenant written on the heart

> <sup>33</sup> But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the LORD, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people. <sup>34</sup> And they shall teach no more every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the LORD: for they shall all know me, from the least of them unto the greatest of them, saith the LORD: for I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more.
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:33-34** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised a new covenant with the house of Israel, with Your law written on their hearts and their sins forgiven and remembered no more. I pray that Jewish people would know You, from the least to the greatest, and receive that forgiveness through You. Write Your word on their hearts and make them Your people. Do this in Israel and in every diaspora community, and let the love of God win them. I ask it with love for my Jewish neighbours. Amen.

### 13. The Son of man who seeks the lost

> *For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.*
>
> — **Luke 19:10** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You came to seek and to save what was lost. I thank You that You seek every Jewish man and woman, in Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Hebron, Kampala, New York and every town where they live. Find those who feel far from God, those who grieve, and those who are afraid. Meet them in their sorrow and show them that You love them. Draw them to Yourself by Your kindness, and let none be lost. Send them faithful friends who share Your love without pressure or contempt. Amen.

### 14. Whoever calls on the Lord shall be saved

> *For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.*
>
> — **Romans 10:13** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says whoever calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved, and that includes the Jew first. I pray that Jewish people would call on Your name. Where there is no one to tell them, send someone. Where they have been told wrongly of You, correct it with Your truth and grace. Forgive Your church for the times it has shown contempt for Your people. Give me a heart like Paul's, with great sorrow and a longing that Israel be saved. Hear the cries of Israel today and answer each one. Amen.

### 15. The veil taken away when they turn to the Lord

> <sup>15</sup> But even unto this day, when Moses is read, the vail is upon their heart. <sup>16</sup> Nevertheless when it shall turn to the Lord, the vail shall be taken away.
>
> — **2 Corinthians 3:15-16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the One to whom Moses points, and Your word says that when Israel turns to the Lord the veil is taken away. I ask You to lift the veil from the hearts of Jewish people when the Scriptures are read, in synagogue and at home. Let them see You in the Law and the Prophets and know You as Messiah and Lord. Be gentle with those who fear what turning to You may cost them. Keep and strengthen every Jewish believer in You. Bring the day of Israel's turning, for Your glory. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I commit Israel, the Jewish people everywhere, and the people of Judea and Samaria into Your hands. Comfort the grieving, protect the vulnerable, give wisdom to leaders, and bring peace where there is fear. Open the eyes of Your people Israel to know You as their Messiah and Lord. My heart's desire and prayer for Israel is that they might be saved. You are faithful, and I trust You. Amen.*
