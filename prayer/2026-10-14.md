### 1. Righteous leadership as Israel heads to the 27 October election

> *When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn.*
>
> — **Proverbs 29:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You rule over the nations, and I bring Israel's election on 27 October to You. The polls point to a close and uncertain result, and the campaign is full of sharp accusations. I ask that those in authority would be righteous, that voters would choose with a clear conscience, and that the result would be accepted peacefully. Let the people rejoice, not mourn, in those who lead them. Guard the ballot from fear and violence, and give wisdom to every candidate, whatever the outcome. Amen.

> <sup>1</sup> I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; <sup>2</sup> For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.
>
> — **1 Timothy 2:1-2** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I obey Your word and make supplications for all who are in authority in Israel. Many parties are uncertain whom to support, and the conscription dispute remains unresolved. I ask that leaders would seek the good of the whole nation and not only the survival of their own party. Give them honesty, humility and wisdom about security and about those who serve and those who study. Grant Israel a quiet and peaceable life in godliness, and bless the people with leaders who fear You. Amen.

> <sup>1</sup> Put them in mind to be subject to principalities and powers, to obey magistrates, to be ready to every good work, <sup>2</sup> To speak evil of no man, to be no brawlers, but gentle, shewing all meekness unto all men.

> — **Titus 3:1-2** · KJV · New Testament

I ask that the law would be upheld fairly and that no one take justice into their own hands. Give officials, officers and community leaders the gentleness and meekness Your word teaches. Restrain anyone who plans violence, protect those living on every side of the line, and make Your peace stronger than anger. Amen.

> <sup>33</sup> And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not vex him. <sup>34</sup> But the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God.

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You love the stranger and the neighbour. I pray for Palestinian farmers and families in the villages of the West Bank, where reports describe attacks around the olive harvest, and for Israelis who also live in fear. I do not know every detail of what happened, so I ask You to bring truth to light. Protect the innocent, comfort anyone who was hurt or lost their livelihood, and move your people to treat one another with justice and love. Amen.

### 5. Jews in Israel and abroad watching the election with anxiety

**In the news:** [JTA: Netanyahu faces a daunting playing field ahead of the 27 October election](https://jta.org/2026/10/13/israel/checkmate-netanyahu-faces-a-daunting-playing-field-ahead-of-oct-27)

I ask that You would speak Your word to them: fear not, for I am with you. Strengthen, help and uphold them. Give calm to parents, soldiers and students, and let Your peace guard their hearts while the nation decides. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Under His wings

> <sup>4</sup> He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler. <sup>5</sup> Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day;
>
> — **Psalm 91:4-5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask You to cover the people of Israel under Your wings. Be their shield and buckler against the terror by night and the arrow by day, against rockets, drones, ordnance left in the ground and every hidden danger on the northern and southern borders. Protect children on school trips, families in shelters, and civilians everywhere. Let Your truth be their shield. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and defenders

> *And I looked, and rose up, and said unto the nobles, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people, Be not ye afraid of them: remember the Lord, which is great and terrible, and fight for your brethren, your sons, and your daughters, your wives, and your houses.*
>
> — **Nehemiah 4:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the soldiers, reservists, police and rescue workers of Israel. Take away needless fear and keep them from harm. Give their commanders wisdom to protect life, restraint in the use of force, and care for the innocent. Remember the brothers, sons, daughters and wives they defend, and bring them home safely to their families. Amen.

### 8. The peace of Jerusalem

> <sup>8</sup> For my brethren and companions’ sakes, I will now say, Peace be within thee. <sup>9</sup> Because of the house of the LORD our God I will seek thy good.
>
> — **Psalm 122:8-9** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I say with the psalmist, peace be within Jerusalem. Guard the city and everyone in it, Jew, Arab and visitor. Quiet the tensions around the holy places, protect worshippers and pilgrims, and give wisdom to the police and city leaders. For the sake of Your house and Your people I seek Jerusalem's good, and I ask You to be her peace. Amen.

### 9. Those who suffer adversity

> *Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them; and them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body.*
>
> — **Hebrews 13:3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You remember the prisoners and the afflicted. I pray for hostages' families, for the bereaved and wounded, and for Jews in many countries who suffer hatred and threats. Let me share their burden in prayer as though I were beside them. Comfort the grieving, heal the injured and give courage to every community that lives under watch. Amen.

### 10. Enduring to the end

> *But he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved.*
>
> — **Matthew 24:13** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised that whoever endures to the end will be saved. I pray for the Jewish people through years of war, exile, and threat, that they would endure by Your mercy and be preserved as a people. Keep faith, hope and love alive in every home. Hold Your covenant people secure, and let their endurance end in salvation and peace. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Salvation is of the Jews

> *Ye worship ye know not what: we know what we worship: for salvation is of the Jews.*
>
> — **John 4:22** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that salvation is of the Jews, and You came as a Jew to Your own people. I ask that Jewish people in Israel and around the world would hear it clearly and with love. Give Your believers boldness and gentleness, free them from shame and fear, and open ears that have never heard Your name spoken kindly. Amen.

### 12. The despised and rejected One

> *He is despised and rejected of men; a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief: and we hid as it were our faces from him; he was despised, and we esteemed him not.*
>
> — **Isaiah 53:3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Isaiah described You as a man of sorrows, despised and rejected. I ask that Jewish readers of Isaiah would see You there, bearing grief for them. Remove old wounds caused by those who claimed Your name and acted without love. Let the Scriptures themselves open hearts to You as Messiah and Lord. Amen.

### 13. Blessed is He who comes

> *For I say unto you, Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.*
>
> — **Matthew 23:39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You wept over Jerusalem and promised that she would one day say, Blessed is He that cometh in the name of the Lord. I long for that day. Bring many in Jerusalem and throughout Israel to say it from the heart now, and prepare the whole nation for Your return. Amen.

### 14. Witnesses who are welcomed

> *Thus saith the LORD of hosts; In those days it shall come to pass, that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, We will go with you: for we have heard that God is with you.*
>
> — **Zechariah 8:23** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for those who live among Jewish neighbours and share Your love with them. Make us people whose lives show that God is with them, so that they will want to walk with us. Raise up Jewish believers who speak to their own families with joy, and protect them from rejection and harm. Amen.

### 15. The Deliverer out of Zion

> <sup>26</sup> And so all Israel shall be saved: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob: <sup>27</sup> For this is my covenant unto them, when I shall take away their sins.
>
> — **Romans 11:26-27** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Deliverer who comes out of Zion to turn away ungodliness from Jacob and to take away sins. I ask You to fulfil this covenant promise, for my heart's desire for Israel is that they might be saved. Draw Your Jewish people to Yourself, family by family, and make them one with every believer in You. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I give Israel, the Jewish people, and the land of Judea and Samaria into Your hands. Protect the innocent, restrain those who plan violence, comfort the grieving, give wisdom to leaders, and draw Your people to Yourself as Messiah and Lord. You are faithful, and I trust You. Amen.*
