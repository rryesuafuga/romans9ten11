## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Israel's votes counted and a government to form

**In the news:** [Exit polls after 27 October vote show no clear winner](https://nbcnews.com/world/israel/live-blog/israel-election-live-updates-netanyahu-eisenkot-voters-war-iran-gaza-rcna604979); [Turnout of 77.7% is highest since 1999](https://npr.org/2026/10/27/nx-s1-6019381/israel-elections)

> *Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.*
>
> — **Proverbs 11:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the votes of Israel's election are being counted, and the exit polls say neither bloc has a majority. I thank You for the record turnout, and I ask You to give the nation's leaders good counsel, for where no counsel is the people fall, but in many counsellors there is safety. Guard the count from fear and dispute. Give wisdom to those who must now negotiate, humility to winners and losers alike, and calm in the streets during the weeks ahead. Be the true King of Israel, and let Your justice shape what is decided. Amen.

### 2. Families displaced in Hebron

**In the news:** [Hebron displacement rises as residents protest on election day](https://jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-913039)

> <sup>18</sup> He doth execute the judgment of the fatherless and widow, and loveth the stranger, in giving him food and raiment. <sup>19</sup> Love ye therefore the stranger: for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.
>
> — **Deuteronomy 10:18-19** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the families who have left their homes in Hebron, whose number the reports put between three and five thousand, and for the people who protested on election day. You loved the stranger and cared for the fatherless and the widow, and You call Your people to do the same. Shelter the children, comfort the frightened, and give a roof to every household without one. Move leaders to protect the innocent while still seeking those who killed the two soldiers. Comfort the soldiers' families. Let justice and mercy meet in Hebron. Amen.

### 3. Wisdom for soldiers and leaders in Gaza and over Iran

**In the news:** [Katz urges voters to back finishing the Iran campaign; Netanyahu denies plan to remove army chief](https://cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-trump-us-diplomacy-oil-prices-strait-of-hormuz); [IDF says it killed four militants in Gaza operation](https://jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/2026-10-26/live-updates-912856)

> <sup>17</sup> But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy. <sup>18</sup> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.
>
> — **James 3:17-18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the Defence Minister speaks of finishing the Iran campaign, and in Gaza the IDF reports killing four militants beyond the yellow line. I ask You for the wisdom from above that is pure, peaceable and full of mercy. Restrain every hand that plans violence, protect soldiers and spare bystanders, and give the leaders of Israel and the region clear heads and honest counsel. Raise up peacemakers on every side, for the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace. Let Your peace, not fear, guide decisions in these days. Amen.

### 4. Diaspora Jews anxious about the election's fallout

> *Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.*
>
> — **John 14:27** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Jewish leaders in Norway, Mexico, the United States and elsewhere tell reporters they fear what this election may mean for Jews everywhere, while others reaffirm their bond with Israel whatever the result. You said, Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you. Let Your peace guard these communities from fear, and keep their hearts from being troubled or afraid. Unite Jewish people who differ, give courage to their leaders, and protect families and synagogues during these uncertain weeks. Draw every heart to You, the Prince of Peace. Amen.

### 5. A synagogue arson attempt and the memory of Pittsburgh

> *He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.*
>
> — **Psalm 147:3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the congregation in West Hempstead, where a man has been charged over an arson attempt at a synagogue, and for the Tree of Life community in Pittsburgh as they remember the eleven killed eight years ago. You heal the broken in heart and bind up their wounds. Comfort the bereaved who still grieve, heal the wounded in body and spirit, and steady every worshipper who now enters a synagogue with caution. Protect Jewish houses of prayer everywhere, and turn hearts from hatred to Your love. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. No weapon formed against Israel

> *No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is of me, saith the LORD.*
>
> — **Isaiah 54:17** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Lord of hosts, and Your word says that no weapon formed against Your people shall prosper. I ask You to guard the land and the borders of Israel, to turn aside missiles, drones and the plans of those who seek to destroy, and to make Your promise to Your servants real in these days. Keep the nation steady through the political uncertainty, and let it know that its security rests in You. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians escaping the snare

> <sup>7</sup> Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we are escaped. <sup>8</sup> Our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth.
>
> — **Psalm 124:7-8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth. I ask You to break every snare set for soldiers at checkpoints, for reservists, and for families in towns near Gaza and the northern border. Bring them out like a bird from the fowler's net. Cover the wounded and the grieving, and let every Israeli, whatever their politics, feel Your hand holding them safe. Amen.

### 8. The angel of the LORD encamped round about

> *The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.*
>
> — **Psalm 34:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for Jerusalem and for every neighbourhood and road where people live in fear. Let Your angel encamp round about those who fear You, and deliver them. Guard pilgrims, worshippers, children going to school and the elderly in shelters. Keep the holy city from violence and give it days of quiet, until the peace of Jerusalem becomes the peace You have promised. Amen.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora and Your faithful presence

> <sup>5</sup> Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee. <sup>6</sup> So that we may boldly say, The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me.
>
> — **Hebrews 13:5-6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee. I pray for Jewish families in Europe, the Americas, Australia and Africa who feel more exposed than they once did. Let them say boldly that the Lord is their helper, and let them not fear what man can do. Protect schools, synagogues and students, and move governments and neighbours to stand beside them with real courage. Amen.

### 10. Kept from the evil

> *I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil.*
>
> — **John 17:15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You prayed that the Father would keep Your people from the evil, even while they remain in the world. I ask the same for the Jewish people: keep them from the evil one, from violence, from despair and from hatred that eats at the soul. Keep Israel faithful to the covenant promises that You will not break, and keep Your own purposes for this people bright and sure through every storm. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Looking on the One they pierced

> *And I will pour upon the house of David, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the spirit of grace and of supplications: and they shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him, as one mourneth for his only son, and shall be in bitterness for him, as one that is in bitterness for his firstborn.*
>
> — **Zechariah 12:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to pour out on the house of David and the people of Jerusalem the spirit of grace and of supplications. I ask You to do it now. Let them look on You, whom they have pierced, and mourn with a mourning that leads to life. Open the eyes of Your own people to see You as their Messiah, and let sorrow turn to joy, because You are the Son who was given for them. Amen.

### 12. A circumcised heart

> *And the LORD thy God will circumcise thine heart, and the heart of thy seed, to love the LORD thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, that thou mayest live.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 30:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the God who circumcises hearts. I ask You to do this for Your people Israel, that they may love the LORD their God with all their heart and soul, and live. Take away what is hard and cold, and give a new heart that trusts in You. Let those who have long loved the Scriptures find You in them, and let that love lead them to You as Lord. Amen.

### 13. Redemption for Israel

> <sup>7</sup> Let Israel hope in the LORD: for with the LORD there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption. <sup>8</sup> And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.
>
> — **Psalm 130:7-8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that with You there is mercy and plenteous redemption. Let Israel hope in the LORD, and let that hope find You, the One who redeems from all iniquity. Meet those who carry grief after the war, those who are tired of fear, and those who ask hard questions about God. Forgive, cleanse and receive every Jewish person who turns to You in repentance. Amen.

### 14. Opened understanding of the Scriptures

> <sup>45</sup> Then opened he their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures, <sup>46</sup> And said unto them, Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead the third day: <sup>47</sup> And that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.
>
> — **Luke 24:45-47** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You opened the understanding of Your disciples that they might understand the Scriptures. Do the same today among Your people. Open the Law, the Prophets and the Psalms to Jewish readers in Israel and in the nations, and show them that You are the one of whom they speak. Let repentance and the forgiveness of sins be preached in Your name, beginning at Jerusalem, and let many hear it gladly. Amen.

### 15. Witnesses who bless

> <sup>25</sup> Ye are the children of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying unto Abraham, And in thy seed shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed. <sup>26</sup> Unto you first God, having raised up his Son Jesus, sent him to bless you, in turning away every one of you from his iniquities.
>
> — **Acts 3:25-26** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You were sent first to bless the children of the prophets and of the covenant by turning each of them from their iniquities. Raise up faithful, loving and humble witnesses among Jewish people and among the nations, who speak of You without contempt. Strengthen Jewish believers in Israel, in Uganda and across the diaspora who follow You, protect them from harm, and let their lives show Your blessing. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring before You Israel, the Jews in Israel, the Jewish people in every nation, and Judea and Samaria. Comfort the bereaved, shelter the displaced, protect the innocent, and give wisdom to every leader. Keep Your covenant people safe, and open their eyes to You as Messiah and Lord, so that Your name is glorified on the earth. Amen.*
