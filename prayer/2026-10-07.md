## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Passengers and crews saved from the flydubai attack

**In the news:** [FlyDubai co-pilot intended to crash plane into Tel Aviv airport, investigators find](https://npr.org/2026/10/05/nx-s1-5990802/midair-attack-flydubai)

> <sup>7</sup> Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we are escaped. <sup>8</sup> Our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth.
>
> — **Psalm 124:7-8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You for the passengers who stopped the attack on that flight, and for the captain who survived. Like a bird out of the snare of the fowlers, they escaped, and I give You the praise. Our help is in the name of the LORD. I ask You to heal the shaken, to give peace to every family who flew in fear, and to guard the skies over Israel and every traveller. Expose and break every plan of hatred before it is carried out, and keep Your people safe in the air and on the ground. Turn the heart of anyone drawn toward violence, and let Your mercy reach even them. Amen.

### 2. Wisdom for leaders and voters in Israel's election

**In the news:** [Israel's Supreme Court overturned the disqualification of Arab-majority parties from the 27 October election](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/9/israeli-parties-submit-slates-for-october-knesset-elections)

> *If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.*
>
> — **James 1:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Israel goes to the polls on 27 October, and the court has just ruled on which parties may stand. I ask for the wisdom that You give liberally and without reproach. Give every leader, judge, candidate and voter a clear mind and a clean heart. Let justice be done and fairness be seen, for Jewish and Arab citizens alike. Quiet the bitterness of the campaign, guard the vote from violence and deceit, and let the outcome serve peace and the good of all who live in the land. I ask in Your name, trusting that You hear when I ask. Amen.

Restrain those who plan violence, whoever they are. Give soldiers and police wisdom and self-control, give farmers a safe olive harvest, and bring justice to those who suffered wrong. Prince of Peace, be near to Israelis and Palestinians who long to live without fear. Amen.

Be strong for those who feel weak. Let them hear Your word, fear not, for the LORD goes with them and will not fail or forsake them. Protect every synagogue, school and family during these days around 7 October. Give police and neighbours the will to stand beside them, and let the hatred that spreads in dark places be exposed to light. Comfort the frightened and keep them close to You. Amen.

### 5. The bereaved three years after 7 October

**In the news:** [Israelis mark three years since the 7 October attack](https://deccanherald.com/world/middle-east/israelis-mark-3-years-of-october-7-attack-that-triggered-assault-on-gaza-4173281); [British Jewry to hold 7 October vigil](https://bod.org.uk/bod-news/jewish-community-to-gather-for-7-october-vigil-of-remembrance)

> <sup>3</sup> Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort; <sup>4</sup> Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.

> — **2 Corinthians 1:3-4** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, three years on, I remember the families who still grieve those murdered on 7 October, the survivors who carry what they saw, and the soldiers and hostages' families who live with loss. You are the Father of mercies and the God of all comfort, and I ask You to come to each one in their tribulation. Sit with them at the memorials in Israel and at the vigil in London. Comfort them so that they may comfort others. Give sleep to the sleepless, and hope to those who have none. Remember also the innocent in Gaza who mourn, and draw near to them. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–8)

### 6. The LORD round about His people

> *As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.*
>
> — **Psalm 125:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, as the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so I ask You to be round about Your people, now and for ever. The National Security Council has warned of heightened danger around this anniversary, and Hezbollah, Hamas and Iran continue to threaten. Surround the cities, towns and borders of Israel, and every soldier and civilian who stands in harm's way. Let no attack succeed, and give the nation's defenders discernment and restraint. Be a wall of peace for families in the north, the south, the cities and the hills, and let them sleep without fear. Amen.

### 7. The Lord is faithful and keeps from evil

> *But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil.*
>
> — **2 Thessalonians 3:3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are faithful, and You stablish and keep Your people from evil. I pray this for Jewish families in every nation who are watching the news with fear this week. Keep them from the violent, and keep their hearts from despair and bitterness. Strengthen those who guard synagogues and community centres, and those who travel, study and work as Jews in places where it is hard. Let them know You are faithful, today and every day, and surprise them with kindness from their neighbours. Amen.

### 8. The LORD of hosts defends Jerusalem

> *As birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem; defending also he will deliver it; and passing over he will preserve it.*
>
> — **Isaiah 31:5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised that as birds flying, so the LORD of hosts will defend Jerusalem, defending, delivering, and passing over to preserve it. I hold You to the love in that promise and ask You to guard Jerusalem and every city of Israel. Preserve its children, its elderly, its sick and its visitors. Overrule the plans of those who wish it harm, and keep Your people from every sudden terror. Let Jerusalem be a city of peace, and let Your name be honoured in it. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people (points 9–10)

### 9. The Lord laid on Him the iniquity of us all

> <sup>5</sup> But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed. <sup>6</sup> All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned every one to his own way; and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.
>
> — **Isaiah 53:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, with the love Paul had for his own people, I pray that Jewish people may see You in the words of Isaiah. You were wounded for our transgressions, and by Your stripes we are healed. Open the eyes of those who read this chapter, in Israel and in every nation, and let them recognise their Messiah. Remove every barrier of fear, family pressure and old hurt, including the harm done to Jews in Your name. Draw them gently, and let them find in You forgiveness and peace. Amen.

### 10. No other name under heaven

> *Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.*
>
> — **Acts 4:12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You alone are the salvation of Israel and of all nations, and there is no other name given among men whereby we must be saved. I pray for Jewish friends, neighbours and strangers who do not yet know You. Send them honest, loving witnesses, give them dreams and questions that lead to You, and bring them to call on Your name and be saved. Let them find in You the promised Messiah and Lord, and let me love them as You do. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring all of this to You: Israel, Jews in every land, Judea and Samaria, and every person afraid or grieving today. Hear me for the sake of Your mercy. Keep Your people, comfort the bereaved, give peace to the land, and bring Jewish people to know You as Messiah and Lord. Let Your will be done, and let Your name be loved. Amen.*
