Sit with those for whom the commemorations reopen grief, give rest to the sleepless, and send comforters to every home with an empty chair. Bring true and lasting peace. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–8)

### 6. The LORD around His people as the mountains are around Jerusalem

> *As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.*
>
> — **Psalm 125:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask You to surround Jerusalem and the whole nation of Israel with Your protection, as the mountains stand around the city. Guard her towns from missiles, drones and attacks, her soldiers and civilians in danger on every border, and her people in the shelters at night. Give her leaders wisdom, her defenders courage and restraint, and her children rest. Let Israel know that You are round about her, now and for ever, and bring the peace that only You can give. Amen.

### 7. God's care for the apple of His eye

> *For thus saith the LORD of hosts; After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you: for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.*
>
> — **Zechariah 2:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the LORD of hosts who spoke of Your people as the apple of Your eye. I ask You to shield Jewish people everywhere, in Israel, Europe, the Americas, Africa and every city where they live, from those who would harm them. Frustrate violent plots, protect synagogues, schools and families, and lead nations to defend their Jewish citizens. Fill Your people with courage and let Your faithfulness toward them be seen. Amen.

### 8. No one can pluck them from the Shepherd's hand

> *And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand.*
>
> — **John 10:28** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Good Shepherd, and You said that none shall pluck Your sheep out of Your hand. I ask You to hold close every Jewish family tonight, those in fear, those in grief and those in danger. Be their security when the world around them is shaken. Keep Your promise to Your people, preserve them through every trial, and let them one day know the safety of Your hand. Watch over those who serve in the Israeli army, and bring them home safely. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people (points 9–10)

### 9. A law written on the heart: the new covenant with the house of Israel

> *But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the LORD, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people.*
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:33** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the promised mediator of the new covenant that the LORD announced to the house of Israel. I pray with love for my Jewish friends and neighbours, and for all Israel, that You would write Your law in their hearts and be known to them as their God and Messiah. Open the eyes of the secular and the religious, remove every barrier of fear and misunderstanding, and send faithful believers to love them with humility and truth. Fulfil Your promise in Your time. Amen.

### 10. The way, the truth and the life

> *Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.*
>
> — **John 14:6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that You are the way, the truth, and the life. I long with all my heart for Jewish people to come to know You as their Messiah and Lord. Let those who are grieving, afraid or searching meet You through Your Word, a kind believer, a dream or Your Spirit. Remove contempt from every Christian heart, and give me a love like Paul's for his own people. Bring many in Israel and in the diaspora to the Father through You. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I commit Israel, the Jewish people and all the peoples of this land to You. Be the refuge of the frightened, the healer of the broken and the peace of Jerusalem. Defend the innocent, restrain violence, give wisdom to leaders, and draw Your people to Yourself as Messiah and Lord. I trust You with what I cannot fix. Amen.*
