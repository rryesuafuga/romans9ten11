You will one day judge among the nations and turn swords into plowshares. Until then, restrain every hand that plans violence, give wisdom to those who negotiate, and keep civilians in Israel, Iran and the region safe. Let threats be set aside and let peace be found. I ask this in Your name. Amen.

I remember the families in Gaza, where hospitals report seven more people killed in recent strikes, and I remember every family in Israel still grieving. Comfort the bereaved, heal the wounded, and protect the innocent on every side. Open the way for food and medicine to reach those in need. Bring an end to the killing, and give all who lead the courage to choose mercy. Amen.

Restrain those who attack, protect farmers and their children, and move the authorities to act with justice. Heal what has been broken, and make a way for peace to grow in a land that You love. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Peace within the borders

> <sup>12</sup> Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Zion. <sup>13</sup> For he hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; he hath blessed thy children within thee. <sup>14</sup> He maketh peace in thy borders, and filleth thee with the finest of the wheat.
>
> — **Psalm 147:12-14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I praise You for Jerusalem and Zion. You strengthen the bars of her gates and bless her children within her. Make peace in the borders of Israel, north and south, and along every line of danger. Let the families in border towns sleep without fear, let the farmers harvest in safety, and let the land be filled with good things. Protect the whole nation by Your power, and be glorified in her, for You are faithful to keep what You have promised. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and their families

> <sup>1</sup> The LORD hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee; <sup>2</sup> Send thee help from the sanctuary, and strengthen thee out of Zion;
>
> — **Psalm 20:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, hear Israel's soldiers and their families in the day of trouble. Defend them by the name of the God of Jacob, send them help from the sanctuary, and strengthen them out of Zion. Guard the young men and women on every front, the reservists called away from their homes, and the wounded in hospital. Give their commanders wisdom and restraint, and protect the civilians near them. Bring each one home safe, and comfort the families who wait with fear. Amen.

### 8. Jerusalem, the city of the great King

> <sup>1</sup> Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness. <sup>2</sup> Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, is mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King. <sup>3</sup> God is known in her palaces for a refuge.
>
> — **Psalm 48:1-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, great is the LORD and greatly to be praised in the city of our God. I pray for Jerusalem, her residents and her visitors, her holy places and her streets. Be known in her palaces as a refuge. Keep her from terror, from violence, and from every plan to harm those who gather there. Let people of every background who live in her find safety, and let her be a place of joy as You intend. Reign in her, Lord, and draw her people to You. Amen.

### 9. Jews everywhere kept from falling

> <sup>24</sup> Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy, <sup>25</sup> To the only wise God our Saviour, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever. Amen.
>
> — **Jude 24-25** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are able to keep Your people from falling. I ask You to keep Jewish families, synagogues, schools and communities in every nation, from Paris and London to Budapest, New York and beyond. Shield them from hatred, threat and attack, and give them hope in the midst of fear. Give authorities the will to protect them, and neighbours the courage to stand beside them. May all glory and majesty be Yours, now and always. Amen.

### 10. God's covenant faithfulness

> <sup>28</sup> As concerning the gospel, they are enemies for your sakes: but as touching the election, they are beloved for the fathers’ sakes. <sup>29</sup> For the gifts and calling of God are without repentance.
>
> — **Romans 11:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that the gifts and calling of God are without repentance. The Jewish people are beloved for the fathers' sakes, and You do not forget Your promises. In every season of war, election or uncertainty, remind Israel that You are faithful. Preserve her through what she faces, keep Your word to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and let Your faithfulness be seen by all nations. Hold fast to Your people, and bring them into the fullness of Your blessing. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Open eyes to see

> *Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law.*
>
> — **Psalm 119:18** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask that You open the eyes of Jewish people everywhere to behold wondrous things, and above all to see You in the Law and the Prophets. Remove whatever veils the heart and mind. Awaken a hunger to read the Scriptures with fresh eyes, and give each one the Holy Spirit's light. Surround them with believers who love them well and answer questions with gentleness. May many see Your glory as Israel's Messiah. Amen.

### 12. The Messiah foretold

> *Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.*
>
> — **Isaiah 7:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Immanuel, God with us, You are the sign the prophet promised. I pray that Jewish people who read Isaiah and the Prophets will recognise You as the Son born of a virgin, the Messiah sent by God. Show them how the promises find their fulfilment in You. Meet every honest seeker with a clear answer and a quiet heart, and let the old prophecies become the road that leads them to You. Amen.

### 13. Confessing Jesus as Lord

> <sup>9</sup> That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. <sup>10</sup> For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.
>
> — **Romans 10:9-10** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray that many in Israel and among the Jews of the diaspora will confess with the mouth that You are Lord and believe in the heart that God raised You from the dead. Bring them to repentance and forgiveness of sins. Give courage to those who fear the cost of confessing You, and give them family and community that will welcome them. Make Your salvation real to each person who calls on Your name. Amen.

### 14. Faithful messengers sent out

> *And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!*
>
> — **Romans 10:15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, send out messengers with the good news of peace to Jewish people in Israel and in every nation. Raise up believers who speak with love, humility and truth, and who are patient with questions and grief. Protect them from fear and discouragement, and give them open doors. Make their feet beautiful, as the Scripture says, and bless every conversation, book, broadcast and friendship used to carry Your gospel to Your people. Amen.

### 15. The remnant of grace

> *Even so then at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace.*
>
> — **Romans 11:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You for the Jewish believers who follow You today, a remnant according to the election of grace. Strengthen them in Israel and around the world, in their congregations, families and workplaces. Give them joy, courage and unity, protect them from rejection and harm, and make them a bright witness to their own people. Add to their number, and let the remnant grow into a great harvest. For Your glory and Israel's blessing. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I have laid before You the election, the talks over Iran, the grief in Gaza, the strain on Jewish communities abroad and the unrest in Judea and Samaria. Keep Israel and Jewish people everywhere under Your protection, and bring them to know You as Messiah and Lord. Let peace and justice come, and let Your name be honoured. You are my hope and theirs. Amen.*
