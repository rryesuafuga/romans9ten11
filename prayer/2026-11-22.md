## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Wisdom as Eisenkot seeks a government before his mandate ends

**In the news:** [Eisenkot and Netanyahu race to build a government; Knesset vote could come by 28–29 November](https://jpost.com/israel-news/2026-11-21/live-updates-915622)

> *If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.*
>
> — **James 1:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promise that whoever lacks wisdom may ask God, who gives to all liberally and does not upbraid. I ask You now for Gadi Eisenkot, Benjamin Netanyahu and every party leader in the Knesset as talks continue and the mandate runs toward 6 December. Give them wisdom from above in every meeting and every vote, whether on a broad coalition or a minority government. Guard them from pride, deceit and fear. Let whatever government is formed serve the good of all who live in the land, protect the weak, and seek peace. Let Your people in Israel pray rather than despair. Amen.

### 2. Strikes in southern Lebanon and a fragile ceasefire

> *And he shall judge among the nations, and shall rebuke many people: and they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruninghooks: nation shall not lift up sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more.*
>
> — **Isaiah 2:4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the prophet foretold a day when You will judge among the nations, and swords will be beaten into plowshares and nations will not learn war any more. Lebanon's news agency reports strikes across four southern districts, and Israel is reported to be pressing Lebanon to keep Hezbollah out of any renewed war with Iran. Protect Israeli soldiers and families in the north, and protect innocent Lebanese families in their villages. Restrain all who plan violence, give the mediators patience and honesty, and keep the ceasefire from unravelling. Hasten the day of Your peace. Amen.

### 3. Olive harvesters and record settler attacks in Judea and Samaria

**In the news:** [UN agencies report a record number of settler attacks during this year's olive harvest](https://manilatimes.net/2026/11/21/world/un-news/west-bank-record-settler-attacks-mar-olive-harvest-as-un-urges-israel-to-protect-farmers/2475469)

> <sup>9</sup> Thus speaketh the LORD of hosts, saying, Execute true judgment, and shew mercy and compassions every man to his brother: <sup>10</sup> And oppress not the widow, nor the fatherless, the stranger, nor the poor; and let none of you imagine evil against his brother in your heart.
>
> — **Zechariah 7:9-10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says, Execute true judgment and show mercy and compassion every man to his brother; oppress not the widow, the fatherless, the stranger or the poor. UN agencies report more than four hundred settler attacks during this olive harvest, a record. I pray for the farmers and families who have been hurt or have lost their crops, and that the authorities will act justly and protect them. Turn the hearts of those who imagine evil against their neighbours. Let Jews and Arabs in Judea and Samaria live without fear, and let Your people be known for mercy. Amen.

### 4. Atara village after an outpost was removed

**In the news:** [After the army evacuated an outpost, settlers attacked homes in the village of Atara](https://haaretz.com/israel-news/twilight-zone/2026-11-21/ty-article-magazine/.highlight/israeli-army-razed-a-settler-outpost-then-left-palestinians-to-face-a-pogrom/000001a1-fce4-d02c-a7eb-fde6565c0000)

>
> — **Luke 6:27-28** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You taught, Love your enemies, do good to them that hate you, bless them that curse you, and pray for them which despitefully use you. Haaretz reports that after soldiers removed an unauthorised outpost near Ramallah, hundreds of settlers attacked homes in Atara. I pray for the families of Atara, for their safety and healing. I pray for the soldiers who must keep order, that they act fairly and in time. And I pray for the angry young men who did this, that You would turn them from violence to repentance and to the love You command. Amen.

### 5. Words that wound: antisemitic slurs in public life

**In the news:** [Comedian Bowen Yang apologises for calling singer Regina Spektor a "Zio"](https://jta.org/2026/11/20/ny/bowen-yang-apologizes-for-calling-regina-spektor-a-zio)

> <sup>29</sup> Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers. <sup>30</sup> And grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption. <sup>31</sup> Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice: <sup>32</sup> And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.
>
> — **Ephesians 4:29-32** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says, Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, and let all bitterness, wrath and evil speaking be put away. JTA reports that comedian Bowen Yang apologised for calling singer Regina Spektor a slur used against Jews. I pray for Jewish artists and ordinary Jewish people who hear such words, that they will not be crushed or afraid. Let apologies be sincere and lead to real change. Guard my own tongue too, Lord. Make me kind, tenderhearted and forgiving, even as God for Your sake has forgiven me. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Peace within Israel's borders

> <sup>12</sup> Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Zion. <sup>13</sup> For he hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; he hath blessed thy children within thee. <sup>14</sup> He maketh peace in thy borders, and filleth thee with the finest of the wheat.
>
> — **Psalm 147:12-14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the psalm calls Jerusalem to praise the Lord, who strengthens the bars of her gates, blesses her children within her, and makes peace in her borders. I ask You to strengthen the gates and borders of Israel on the north, the south and the east. Bless the children of Israel in their homes and schools. Give peace on every frontier and provision in every household. Let the people praise You for Your protection, and let them come to know You, the One who gives that peace. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians through fire and water

> *When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee: when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned; neither shall the flame kindle upon thee.*
>
> — **Isaiah 43:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promise, When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee, and when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned. I lift up the soldiers of Israel on the Lebanese, Gazan and Syrian fronts and in Judea and Samaria, and the civilians who live near those lines. Be with them in danger. Keep them from fire and harm, and from doing harm to the innocent. Comfort the families who wait at home, and let every one of them know that You are near. Amen.

### 8. Watchmen on the walls of Jerusalem

> <sup>6</sup> I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night: ye that make mention of the LORD, keep not silence, <sup>7</sup> And give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth.
>
> — **Isaiah 62:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have set watchmen on the walls of Jerusalem who will not hold their peace day or night, and who give the Lord no rest until He makes Jerusalem a praise in the earth. Make me one of those watchmen in prayer. Guard Jerusalem from terror and division, and protect its residents, pilgrims and holy places. Grant peace among Jews, Christians and Muslims in the city. Establish Jerusalem as a praise in the earth, the city where You died and rose again and to which You will return. Amen.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora under Your care

> *But there shall not an hair of your head perish.*
>
> — **Luke 21:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said, There shall not an hair of your head perish. I pray for Jewish people in New York, Toronto, London, Paris, Sydney, Johannesburg and every city where hatred against them has grown. Protect synagogues, schools, shops and homes. Keep families safe as they walk to prayer and gather for Shabbat. Give wisdom to police and leaders who must protect them, and courage to neighbours who will stand beside them. Let them know that You see and care for every hair of their heads. Amen.

### 10. Beloved for the fathers' sakes

> <sup>28</sup> As concerning the gospel, they are enemies for your sakes: but as touching the election, they are beloved for the fathers’ sakes. <sup>29</sup> For the gifts and calling of God are without repentance.
>
> — **Romans 11:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your apostle wrote that Israel is beloved for the fathers' sakes, for the gifts and calling of God are without repentance. Thank You that Your covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob has not been cancelled. Preserve the Jewish people as You have preserved them through exile, pogroms and the Holocaust. Let no plan to destroy them succeed. Let Your Church honour them and pray for them, and let Your unbreakable love draw them, in Your time, to You. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The veil taken away

> <sup>14</sup> But their minds were blinded: for until this day remaineth the same vail untaken away in the reading of the old testament; which vail is done away in Christ. <sup>15</sup> But even unto this day, when Moses is read, the vail is upon their heart. <sup>16</sup> Nevertheless when it shall turn to the Lord, the vail shall be taken away.
>
> — **2 Corinthians 3:14-16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says that when Moses is read a veil lies on the heart, but when it turns to the Lord the veil shall be taken away. I pray for Jewish men, women and children who read the Torah this Shabbat in Israel and around the world. Take away the veil, Lord. Open their eyes to see You in the Law and the Prophets, and open their hearts to receive You. Let them find that the veil is done away in You, their Messiah. Amen.

### 12. Immanuel, the sign the Lord Himself gave

> *Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.*
>
> — **Isaiah 7:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through Isaiah the Lord promised a sign: a virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and shall call His name Immanuel, God with us. You are that Son, born of Mary in Bethlehem of Judea. As the season of remembering Your birth draws near, I pray that Jewish people would read Isaiah's words with fresh eyes and recognise You as Immanuel, the promised Messiah of Israel, God come among His people. Amen.

### 13. Repentance and times of refreshing

> <sup>19</sup> Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord; <sup>20</sup> And he shall send Jesus Christ, which before was preached unto you: <sup>21</sup> Whom the heaven must receive until the times of restitution of all things, which God hath spoken by the mouth of all his holy prophets since the world began.
>
> — **Acts 3:19-21** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Peter told the people of Jerusalem, Repent and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord. I pray that Jewish people in Israel and the diaspora would turn to You and receive the forgiveness You bought on the cross. Blot out their sins and bring times of refreshing from Your presence. Let them know You as the One whom all the holy prophets foretold. Amen.

### 14. Faithful messengers of good tidings

> *How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, that publisheth peace; that bringeth good tidings of good, that publisheth salvation; that saith unto Zion, Thy God reigneth!*
>
> — **Isaiah 52:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, how beautiful are the feet of those who bring good tidings, who publish peace and salvation, and who say to Zion, Thy God reigneth. Raise up faithful witnesses among Your people in Israel, in Uganda and across the nations, who will share the gospel with Jewish friends with humility, patience and love. Give them courage and wisdom, and protect them from harm. Let their lives commend the good news they speak. Amen.

### 15. A remnant according to grace

> *Even so then at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace.*
>
> — **Romans 11:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says that at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace. Thank You for Jewish believers who already know You as Messiah and Lord, in Israel and across the diaspora. Strengthen their congregations, protect them from hostility, and supply their needs. Let them stand firm and shine as lights among their own people. Grow the remnant, Lord, until many more come to know You. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, thank You that You keep Israel and that Your gifts and calling do not change. I commit to You Israel's leaders, its soldiers and civilians, the Jewish people in every land, and all who suffer in Judea and Samaria and Lebanon. Remove the veil from hearts, and bring Your people to know You as Messiah and Lord. Let Your peace and Your salvation go forth from Zion. Amen.*
