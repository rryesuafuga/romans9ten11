### 1. Wisdom for leaders and voters as polls show a divided Israel, 19 days before the vote

> *And he changeth the times and the seasons: he removeth kings, and setteth up kings: he giveth wisdom unto the wise, and knowledge to them that know understanding:*
>
> — **Daniel 2:21** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You change the times and the seasons, You remove kings and set up kings, and You give wisdom to the wise. I pray for Israel as polls show a nation divided and the vote of 27 October draws near. Give wisdom and humility to every candidate and every voter. Keep the campaign free from violence, fear and contempt, and let the nation choose leaders who will protect life, pursue peace and honour You. Lord, no result surprises You, and no coalition is beyond Your hand. I trust You with this election and ask that Your purposes be done in Israel. Amen.

### 2. Truth about the failures of 7 October, and a hearing for the allegations made

**In the news:** [Former military secretary alleges Netanyahu's chief of staff tampered with 7 October phone records](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-08-2026)

> *These are the things that ye shall do; Speak ye every man the truth to his neighbour; execute the judgment of truth and peace in your gates:*
>
> — **Zechariah 8:16** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I bring You the allegation that records connected with 7 October were tampered with. I do not know what is true, but You do, and You love truth. I ask that every claim be examined fairly, that the accused be heard, and that the grieving families receive honest answers about what happened. Teach Israel's leaders and officials to speak every man the truth to his neighbour and to judge with truth and peace in the gates. Let no one use the memory of the dead for gain, and let justice be done without bitterness. Lord, bring what is hidden to light and heal trust in the nation. Amen.

### 3. Negotiators in Tehran and a fragile ceasefire with Iran

**In the news:** [Qatari negotiators travel to Tehran to try to finalise a US–Iran deal](https://nbcnews.com/middle-east-conflict)

> *Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.*
>
> — **Matthew 5:9** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that the peacemakers are blessed and shall be called the children of God. I pray for the Qatari negotiators and every diplomat working on the talks over Iran, while the ceasefire is described as hanging by a thread. Give them patience, honesty and wisdom. Restrain those who plan violence against Israel and her neighbours, and hold back any renewed war that would bring death to families on every side. I ask that agreements made would protect innocent life and not merely delay danger. Lord, You are the Prince of Peace; make peace where only You can. Amen.

> — **Isaiah 32:17-18** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the olive harvest has opened amid reports of attacks on farmers and groves, and of injuries in Aqraba. You see every field and every family who depends on the trees. I ask for protection for harvesters, for shepherds and for every Jewish and Arab family in Judea and Samaria. Let righteousness bring peace, and quietness and assurance in the place of fear. Restrain anyone who would use violence, and give authorities courage to protect the vulnerable and to uphold the law. Comfort those who were hurt, and let every person live in a peaceable habitation. Amen.

### 5. Peace of mind for the grieving and fearful after the anniversary

**In the news:** [Former military secretary alleges Netanyahu's chief of staff tampered with 7 October phone records](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-08-2026); [Qatari negotiators travel to Tehran to try to finalise a US–Iran deal](https://nbcnews.com/middle-east-conflict)

> <sup>6</sup> Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. <sup>7</sup> And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

> — **Philippians 4:6-7** · KJV · New Testament

Teach them to be careful for nothing and to make their requests known to You with thanksgiving. Let Your peace, which passes all understanding, guard their hearts and minds. Be near the bereaved, the wounded, the soldiers who still carry what they saw, and the parents who are afraid for their children. Where threats are warned of, protect them. Where sleep is hard, give rest. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Jerusalem and the land

> <sup>6</sup> Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee. <sup>7</sup> Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces.
>
> — **Psalm 122:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I obey Your word and pray for the peace of Jerusalem. Let peace be within her walls and prosperity within her palaces. Guard the city, its streets, its holy places and every person who lives or visits there, Jewish, Christian and Arab. Keep the land's borders from attack, and frustrate every plan to harm the people of Israel. Give those responsible for security wisdom and restraint, and let the city be a place of safety for all who love it. Lord, You chose Zion; keep her in Your care and fill her with Your peace. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians in danger

> <sup>1</sup> He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. <sup>2</sup> I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
>
> — **Psalm 91:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for every Israeli soldier, reservist and civilian who faces danger today, in the north, on the borders and in the cities. Let them dwell in the secret place of the Most High and abide under the shadow of the Almighty. Be their refuge and their fortress. Keep them from the sword, the rocket, the knife and the roadside. Protect their minds as well as their bodies, comfort the families who wait, and bring the wounded home. Lord, let every person in uniform and every family sheltering at home say, in You will I trust. Amen.

### 8. Jews scattered among the nations

> *Hear the word of the LORD, O ye nations, and declare it in the isles afar off, and say, He that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock.*
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised that He who scattered Israel will gather and keep him as a shepherd keeps his flock. I pray for Jewish people in every nation, in Australia, the United States, Britain, Europe, Africa and Latin America. Keep their synagogues, schools and homes from hatred and attack. Give courage to families who now hesitate to show who they are, and give their neighbours and governments the will to protect them. Shepherd them, Lord, and let no flock of Yours be left defenceless. Amen.

### 9. God's faithfulness to His covenant people

> *I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.*
>
> — **Romans 11:1** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the faithful One. Your word says that God has not cast away his people, and Paul himself was an Israelite of the seed of Abraham. I thank You that Your promises to Israel do not fail, even when the nation is weak or divided. Remind Israel in this season that You have not forgotten her. Strengthen believers from the nations to stand with the Jewish people in love and not in pride. Let Your covenant faithfulness be a rock under the nation and under every Jewish family tonight. Amen.

### 10. Held in the Father's name

> *And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.*
>
> — **John 17:11** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You prayed that the Father would keep through His own name those He has given You. I ask that same keeping for the people of Israel and for Jewish people everywhere. Hold them through the fear of war and the weight of grief. Guard the unity of Your people, and let families, congregations and communities stand together. Keep Jewish believers in You from discouragement, and keep every Israeli safe in the hand of the Father. Lord, You never lose those You hold; bring Israel through this season. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Veils lifted from hearts

> <sup>15</sup> But even unto this day, when Moses is read, the vail is upon their heart. <sup>16</sup> Nevertheless when it shall turn to the Lord, the vail shall be taken away.
>
> — **2 Corinthians 3:15-16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Paul wrote that a veil lies upon the heart when Moses is read, and that when it turns to You the veil is taken away. I ask You to lift that veil from many Jewish hearts. Open the eyes of friends, neighbours, soldiers, students and elders to see Your glory in the Scriptures they already love. Let them turn to You freely and gladly. Draw them in love, not by pressure, and let this year be a year when many find in You the Messiah of Israel. Amen.

### 12. The Messiah foretold

> <sup>6</sup> For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace. <sup>7</sup> Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom, to order it, and to establish it with judgment and with justice from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of the LORD of hosts will perform this.
>
> — **Isaiah 9:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Isaiah foretold a child born and a son given, the Prince of Peace whose government will never end. I ask that Jewish people reading these prophets would recognise You in them. Let the Scriptures of Israel speak clearly to those searching for peace in a time of war. Show them that the zeal of the LORD of hosts has performed this in You. Where there is fear, give hope, and where there is grief, show them the Prince of Peace. May many in Israel meet You as the child and the King. Amen.

### 13. Forgiveness and a new covenant

> <sup>33</sup> But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the LORD, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people. <sup>34</sup> And they shall teach no more every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the LORD: for they shall all know me, from the least of them unto the greatest of them, saith the LORD: for I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more.
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:33-34** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the One who brings the new covenant, writing the law on the heart and forgiving iniquity and remembering sin no more. I pray for Jewish people who long for forgiveness, for a clean conscience and for a close relationship with God. Draw them to repentance and to You. Let them know the LORD, from the least to the greatest. Pour out this forgiveness on soldiers, mourners, the young and the old. Write Your word on their hearts, and make Israel a people who know You personally. Amen.

### 14. Faithful witnesses

> <sup>14</sup> How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher? <sup>15</sup> And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!
>
> — **Romans 10:14-15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, how can they call on You if they have not believed, and how can they believe if they have not heard? Send faithful messengers to the Jewish people in Israel and across the world. Raise up Jewish believers, Hebrew speakers and friends from the nations who share Your good news with love, respect and courage. Protect them in places where speaking of You costs something. Give them gentle words and open doors, and let their feet be beautiful as they bring glad tidings of good things. Amen.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus

> *For I would not, brethren, that ye should be ignorant of this mystery, lest ye should be wise in your own conceits; that blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the Gentiles be come in.*
>
> — **Romans 11:25** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You for every Jewish person who already trusts in You as Messiah and Lord. Strengthen the believers in Israel and in the diaspora, especially those who face rejection from family or community. Make them bold, humble and full of joy. Paul spoke of a mystery, that blindness in part has come to Israel until the fulness of the Gentiles comes in. Lord, hasten that day, and let the number of Jewish believers grow until a great multitude of Israel knows You. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I place Israel, the Jewish people everywhere, and the people of Judea and Samaria in Your hands. Keep the grieving and the frightened, restrain violence, give wisdom to leaders, and above all open the hearts of Your people Israel to know You as Messiah and Lord. I pray this in Your name. Amen.*
