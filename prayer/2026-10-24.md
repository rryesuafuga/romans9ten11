### 1. Israel's voters and leaders as the 27 October election nears

> *The king’s heart is in the hand of the LORD, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.*
>
> — **Proverbs 21:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the king's heart is in the hand of the LORD, and I bring You Israel's election three days away. Polls disagree and the campaign has been bitter. I ask You to turn the hearts of voters and leaders toward wisdom, honesty and the good of all the people. Calm the anger of the final days. Whoever is chosen, give them humility, a fear of God, and a love of justice, and let the result be received peacefully. Be Lord over this vote, as You are over every nation. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. A people who trust in the LORD, like mount Zion

> *They that trust in the LORD shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever.*
>
> — **Psalm 125:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promise that those who trust in the LORD are like mount Zion, which cannot be removed. I ask You to establish the people of Israel in that trust. Be their steady ground when elections, rumours of war and sudden attacks shake the nation. Let no season of uncertainty uproot Your people, and keep their hope fixed on You. Be their security that does not depend on polls or leaders. Amen.

### 7. The defence of Jerusalem

> *As birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem; defending also he will deliver it; and passing over he will preserve it.*
>
> — **Isaiah 31:5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the LORD of hosts who defends Jerusalem as birds flying over their nest. I pray for Your defence over this city, its residents, pilgrims and visitors. Guard its streets, its schools and its holy places. Deliver Jerusalem from hands that would harm it, and preserve every life within its walls. Let the city of the great King know peace, and let Your watchful care be over it day and night. Amen.

### 8. The eternal refuge for a weary nation

> *The LORD of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.*
>
> — **Psalm 46:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the LORD of hosts is with us, and the God of Jacob is our refuge. I pray this over Israel's soldiers, reservists and border communities, and over every civilian who sleeps near a siren. Be a refuge for the exhausted and for families separated by service. Guard those on the northern and southern borders, and bring them home safely. Give them rest, courage and clear judgment, and be near to all who stand watch tonight. Amen.

### 9. The Lord's faithfulness to keep the Jewish people from evil

> *But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil.*
>
> — **2 Thessalonians 3:3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are faithful, and You stablish Your people and keep them from evil. I ask You to keep the Jewish people from the evil of hatred, whether it comes as rockets, rifles, graffiti or threats. Establish Jewish families in Israel and across the world in peace. Give them strength for each day, and keep their hearts from bitterness and despair. Let Your faithfulness be seen by their neighbours and by the nations. Amen.

### 10. Held in the hand that no one can break

> <sup>28</sup> And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. <sup>29</sup> My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.
>
> — **John 10:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You say that no one can pluck Your own out of Your hand, or out of the Father's hand. I pray for every Jewish person who lives with anxiety about tomorrow, in Israel or abroad. Let them know they are not forgotten by God. Give Your protection to the vulnerable, the elderly and the children, and hold them safe. Show Yourself as the Shepherd who watches over His sheep, and be near to all who feel alone. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. A heart's desire and prayer for Israel

> *Brethren, my heart’s desire and prayer to God for Israel is, that they might be saved.*
>
> — **Romans 10:1** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, with Paul I say that my heart's desire and prayer to God for Israel is that they might be saved. I pray this with love and not contempt, for the Jewish people gave us the Scriptures and the Messiah. Open the eyes of Your people to see You as Messiah and Lord. Stir hunger for Your word in Israelis and Jews worldwide, and let this longing for their salvation be in me and in Your church. Amen.

### 12. The Messiah wounded for our transgressions

> <sup>5</sup> But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed. <sup>6</sup> All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned every one to his own way; and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.
>
> — **Isaiah 53:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Isaiah wrote that You were wounded for our transgressions and bruised for our iniquities, and that by Your stripes we are healed. I ask You to reveal this chapter to Jewish readers everywhere, in synagogues, homes and on screens. Let them see that the LORD laid on You the iniquity of us all. Bring peace with God to those who read, forgive their sin, and heal their hearts through the One who bore it. Amen.

### 13. A new heart and a new spirit

> <sup>26</sup> A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. <sup>27</sup> And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.
>
> — **Ezekiel 36:26-27** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised through Ezekiel to take away the stony heart and give a heart of flesh and a new spirit. I pray this promise over the Jewish people, over those weary of conflict and those grieving their dead. Soften hearts that have grown hard from fear, and put Your Spirit within them. Cause them to walk in Your ways, and bring many to know You as the one who makes all things new. Amen.

### 14. Salvation in the name of Jesus

> *Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.*
>
> — **Acts 4:12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Peter told the rulers of Jerusalem that there is salvation in no other name under heaven. I pray that Jewish people in Israel and abroad would hear that name with open hearts. Raise up believers who speak of You gently and clearly, in Hebrew, Russian, English, French and every language. Protect them in their witness. Let no fear, pride or sorrow stop anyone from calling on You and being saved. Amen.

### 15. Every knee bowing and every tongue confessing Jesus as Lord

> <sup>10</sup> That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth; <sup>11</sup> And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.
>
> — **Philippians 2:10-11** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I look to the day when every knee will bow at Your name and every tongue confess that You are Lord, to the glory of God the Father. I pray for Jewish believers in You today: strengthen them, keep them faithful, and comfort those who face rejection from family or community. Draw their brothers and sisters to the same confession, and hasten the day when Israel and the nations worship You together. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I have brought before You the election, the wounded, the West Bank, the threatened synagogues and the region's civilians. You are faithful. Protect Your people Israel, comfort the grieving, restrain violence, and draw the Jewish people to Yourself as Messiah and Lord. Let Your peace rule in every heart. I ask it in Your name. Amen.*
