## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Israel's election and the formation of a government

**In the news:** [Final polls before 27 October vote show Eisenkot's Yashar narrowly ahead, no bloc at 61](https://jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912743)

> <sup>1</sup> Give the king thy judgments, O God, and thy righteousness unto the king’s son. <sup>2</sup> He shall judge thy people with righteousness, and thy poor with judgment.
>
> — **Psalm 72:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, today Israel's voters go to the polls, and the polls say no bloc is near a majority. Lord Jesus, I ask You to give the nation's leaders and voters Your righteousness and Your judgments, as this psalm asks for the king. Let the poor and the needy be remembered whatever the result. Guard the count from fear and dispute, and keep the peace in the streets. Grant wisdom to those who must now build a government, and humility to those who win and those who lose. You are the true King of Israel; let Your justice shape what is decided. Amen.

### 2. Palestinian families displaced in Hebron

**In the news:** [UN secretary-general alarmed by sweeping closures after Hebron attack](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/10/1168660); [UN rights chief warns Hebron operation raises concerns of ethnic cleansing](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/10/1168672); [Hebron businesses count cost of the Israeli operation](https://aljazeera.com/features/2026/10/26/everything-destroyed-hebron-businesses-count-cost-of-israeli-assault)

> <sup>3</sup> Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy. <sup>4</sup> Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked.
>
> — **Psalm 82:3-4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the thousands of families the Palestinian Authority says have left their homes in Hebron, and for the shopkeepers counting what was lost. You call us to defend the poor and needy and to do justice to the afflicted. Comfort the frightened, shelter the children, and move leaders to protect the innocent while seeking those who carry out attacks. Let no family be left without a roof, and let justice and mercy meet. Be near to every person suffering there. Amen.

### 3. Closures, soldiers and the search for peace

**In the news:** [Israeli military closes West Bank crossings ahead of the election](https://cbsnews.com/news/israel-military-operation-west-bank-elections); [IDF says it killed four Hamas and Islamic Jihad operatives in weekend Gaza strikes](https://jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/2026-10-25/live-updates-912732)

> *Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.*
>
> — **Matthew 5:9** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said the peacemakers are blessed. I pray for the soldiers who guard the crossings and checkpoints on this election day, and for the families of the two soldiers killed near Hebron. Comfort the bereaved. Protect Israelis and Palestinians who must pass through these closures, and keep tempers cool. In Gaza, where strikes continue under the ceasefire, restrain violence and spare the bystanders. Raise up peacemakers on every side, and make Your peace the end of this weary cycle. Amen.

### 4. Tension over Iran and the region

**In the news:** [Reports of dispute over a pre-election Iran strike plan](https://timesofisrael.com/live)

> *Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.*
>
> — **Isaiah 26:3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I hear of disagreement among Israel's leaders over whether to strike Iran, and of talks to avoid renewed war. Lord Jesus, You are the Prince of Peace. Keep the minds of Israel's leaders stayed on You, and give them perfect peace in a time of pressure. Give wisdom to those negotiating in other capitals, and restrain every plan of violence. Protect the people of Israel, Iran and the Gulf from the horror of a wider war. Let truth and sober counsel prevail over haste and fear. Amen.

### 5. Jewish people attacked in New York

**In the news:** [Woman charged with hate crimes over attack on two Jewish men in New York](https://cnn.com/2026/10/25/us/nyc-jewish-attack-hate-crime)

> <sup>27</sup> But I say unto you which hear, Love your enemies, do good to them which hate you, <sup>28</sup> Bless them that curse you, and pray for them which despitefully use you.
>
> — **Luke 6:27-28** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that a woman has been charged over the attack on two Jewish men in New York, and I pray for them and their families. Heal their bodies and calm their fear. Protect Jewish people walking to synagogue or home in every city. You teach us to love our enemies and to pray for those who mistreat us, so I also pray for the one charged: soften her heart and bring truth and repentance. Give courage and kindness to neighbours who stand beside the Jewish community. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The Lord as Israel's keeper

> <sup>5</sup> The LORD is thy keeper: the LORD is thy shade upon thy right hand. <sup>6</sup> The sun shall not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night.
>
> — **Psalm 121:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the keeper of Israel, a shade at her right hand. I ask You to watch over every Israeli family, by day and by night, in cities, kibbutzim and border towns. Be shade in the heat of danger and a light in dark hours. Shield children in school, shift workers, and those who cannot sleep for fear. Let the sirens fall silent and let Your care rest on the whole land. Amen.

### 7. The apple of His eye

> *For thus saith the LORD of hosts; After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you: for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.*
>
> — **Zechariah 2:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have said that whoever touches Your people touches the apple of Your eye. I pray for Jewish communities around the world who feel exposed and alone. Protect them from those who plot harm, and turn the hearts of their enemies. Let them know they are precious to You, and let the nations learn to treat them with honour. Hold them close, and keep them from hatred and despair. Amen.

### 8. Soldiers and civilians

> <sup>11</sup> For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways. <sup>12</sup> They shall bear thee up in their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.
>
> — **Psalm 91:11-12** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You give Your angels charge over those who trust You. I lift up the soldiers and reservists of Israel, and every civilian near a front line. Keep them in all their ways. Guard them from ambush, missiles and mistakes. Give commanders clear judgment and restraint, and bring each one home to those who wait. Let no innocent life be taken through carelessness. Make Your presence real in the barracks and at the kitchen tables. Amen.

### 9. Nothing can separate us

> <sup>38</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, <sup>39</sup> Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
>
> — **Romans 8:38-39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your love holds fast when everything around us shakes. I pray that Jewish believers and all Jewish people living under threat may be sure of Your love, which no danger can break. Where families have lost someone, wrap them in that love. Where people are tempted to hate, replace it with hope. Be their anchor in the long strain of war and rising hostility. Amen.

### 10. Deliverance from every evil

> *And the Lord shall deliver me from every evil work, and will preserve me unto his heavenly kingdom: to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.*
>
> — **2 Timothy 4:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You deliver Your people from every evil work and preserve them for Your kingdom. I ask You to deliver Jerusalem, Judea and Samaria, and Israel's cities from terror, from wicked schemes, and from the quiet slide into violence. Preserve what is good in this land and in the Jewish communities of the world. Let Your kingdom come, and let all glory be Yours for ever. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Waiting for the God who saves

> *And it shall be said in that day, Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is the LORD; we have waited for him, we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation.*
>
> — **Isaiah 25:9** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, The prophet says that in that day Your people will declare that this is the God for whom they have waited. I pray that many Jewish people will see that You, Lord Jesus, are the One they have waited for. Open their hearts to recognise You as Messiah and Saviour. Bring them into joy in Your salvation, and let it begin in this season. Amen.

### 12. Seeking the Lord while He may be found

> <sup>6</sup> Seek ye the LORD while he may be found, call ye upon him while he is near: <sup>7</sup> Let the wicked forsake his way, and the unrighteous man his thoughts: and let him return unto the LORD, and he will have mercy upon him; and to our God, for he will abundantly pardon.
>
> — **Isaiah 55:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You invite everyone to seek the Lord while He is near. I pray for Jewish men and women who are searching, in Israel and abroad, even as they argue and grieve. Draw them to call on You. Show them that You will have mercy and abundantly pardon. Let no one feel too far away or too ashamed to come to You. Amen.

### 13. Not ashamed of the gospel

> *For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.*
>
> — **Romans 1:16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Paul was not ashamed of the gospel, which is the power of God to salvation, to the Jew first. I pray that Your church would never be ashamed or silent toward the Jewish people. Give believers love, respect and courage to share Your good news gently. Make the gospel plain to every Jewish neighbour, coworker and friend, and let Your power bring them to faith. Amen.

### 14. God so loved

> <sup>16</sup> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life. <sup>17</sup> For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved.
>
> — **John 3:16-17** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You were sent not to condemn but to save. I ask You to show Jewish people the love that sent You, and to remove every barrier of fear and old pain. Let them see that whosoever believes in You will not perish. Meet each person as You met Nicodemus by night. Bring new life, and let them find peace with God. Amen.

### 15. Our High Priest who intercedes

> *Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them.*
>
> — **Hebrews 7:25** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are able to save to the uttermost all who come to God by You, and You live always to intercede. I thank You for Jewish believers in Israel and across the world, and I pray You will strengthen them under pressure. Keep them faithful and joyful, and raise up more. Make them bold witnesses among their own people. Hear Your own prayers for them and let many come. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring You Israel, the Jewish people in the land and around the world, and Judea and Samaria. Turn this election, these closures and every threat toward peace and safety. Protect the innocent, comfort the grieving, and open the eyes of Your people to You as Messiah and Lord. Be glorified in Jerusalem and to the ends of the earth. Amen.*
