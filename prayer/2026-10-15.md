## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The land held in the LORD's hand

> <sup>1</sup> They that trust in the LORD shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever. <sup>2</sup> As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.
>
> — **Psalm 125:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are round about Your people as the mountains are round about Jerusalem. I ask You to surround the land of Israel and its borders, its towns and farms, with Your peace and protection. Let those who trust You be unmoved, and give the nation steadiness when rockets, drones and threats come. Keep every family, city and village in safety, and let Your presence be their security today. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and defenders

> *Be strong and of a good courage, fear not, nor be afraid of them: for the LORD thy God, he it is that doth go with thee; he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 31:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for every soldier, reservist and first responder who stands guard for Israel. Be strong in them, as You told Your people to be strong and of good courage. Let them not fear, and go with them in every patrol and operation. Protect their bodies and their minds, comfort their families at home, and keep them from needless harm. Give them wisdom to protect the innocent. Amen.

### 8. Jerusalem defended

> *As birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem; defending also he will deliver it; and passing over he will preserve it.*
>
> — **Isaiah 31:5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to defend Jerusalem as birds flying over their young, to deliver it and preserve it. I ask You to guard Jerusalem and everyone who lives in or visits the city, Jews, Christians and Muslims. Turn aside every plan of violence against it, give its leaders wisdom, and let Jerusalem know Your protecting care in these uncertain days. Amen.

### 9. Kept from evil, at home and abroad

> *But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil.*
>
> — **2 Thessalonians 3:3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are faithful, and You stablish and keep from evil. I pray for Jewish communities around the world, in Britain, Europe, the Americas, Australia and Africa. Keep them from hatred and violence, protect their synagogues, schools and shops, and give police and governments the will to guard them. Be especially near to those who live in fear, and keep civilians in Israel safe from attack. Amen.

### 10. Your covenant faithfulness

> *Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.*
>
> — **Matthew 28:20** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said You are with Your people always, to the end of the age. I thank You that Your faithfulness does not depend on the strength of nations or on the outcome of elections. Remind Israel and Jews everywhere that You are with them, keep Your promises over them, and bring them safely through every trial to know You as their Lord. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. There is salvation in no other name

> *Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.*
>
> — **Acts 4:12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the one name given under heaven by which we must be saved. With the love of Romans 10:1, I ask that many Jewish people in Israel and around the world would hear Your name and come to know You as their Messiah and Lord. Remove fear and prejudice, open their ears, and let Your name be spoken with love and honour. Amen.

### 12. The wells of salvation

> <sup>2</sup> Behold, God is my salvation; I will trust, and not be afraid: for the LORD JEHOVAH is my strength and my song; he also is become my salvation. <sup>3</sup> Therefore with joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of salvation.
>
> — **Isaiah 12:2-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the salvation Isaiah promised. I pray that Israel would say, God is my salvation, I will trust and not be afraid. Give Jewish people today a thirst for living water, and lead them to the wells of salvation in You. Let joy replace anxiety, and let those who are weary of conflict find true rest and peace in You. Amen.

### 13. A fountain opened for cleansing

> *In that day there shall be a fountain opened to the house of David and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem for sin and for uncleanness.*
>
> — **Zechariah 13:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the fountain opened for sin and for uncleanness. I ask You to open the eyes of the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to the forgiveness You have won on the cross. Draw those who carry guilt and grief to repentance and cleansing. Let them find in You pardon, a new heart, and lasting peace with God. Amen.

### 14. Faithful witnesses

> *And straightway he preached Christ in the synagogues, that he is the Son of God.*
>
> — **Acts 9:20** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Paul went straight to the synagogues and preached that You are the Son of God. Raise up faithful, humble witnesses today, in Israel and in every Jewish community, who share You with courage and gentleness. Give them wisdom, protect them from fear and contempt, and let their lives match their words. Let no one speak of You in pride, only in love. Amen.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus

> *He first findeth his own brother Simon, and saith unto him, We have found the Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ.*
>
> — **John 1:41** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Andrew found his brother and said, We have found the Messiah. I thank You for Jewish believers in Israel and around the world, and I ask You to strengthen them. Protect them from rejection and pressure, give them joy and boldness, and let them bring their families and friends to You. May many more say, We have found the Messiah. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring all these needs to You: an election, a tense border, villages in the West Bank, grieving communities in Britain, soldiers and families, and Your people who do not yet know You. You are the Prince of Peace and the Messiah of Israel. Protect the innocent, restrain the violent, comfort the mourning, and bring Jewish people everywhere to faith in You. Hear us for Your name's sake. Amen.*
