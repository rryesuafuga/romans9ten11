### 1. Wisdom for voters and leaders six days before the election

> *For the LORD is our judge, the LORD is our lawgiver, the LORD is our king; he will save us.*
>
> — **Isaiah 33:22** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are our Judge, our Lawgiver and our King, and You save. Six days before Israel votes, with the polls showing no bloc near a majority, I ask You to give voters, candidates and every leader a hunger for righteousness and a fear of You. Guard the election from violence, fear and deceit. Let those who must form a government seek the good of all the people and not only their own advantage. Be the true King over Israel, and over every nation that watches her. Amen.

> <sup>6</sup> My soul hath long dwelt with him that hateth peace. <sup>7</sup> I am for peace: but when I speak, they are for war.
>
> — **Psalm 120:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Prince of Peace, I come to You about the Strait of Hormuz, the fragile ceasefire with Iran, and the talk of renewed strikes. You know how long many have dwelt among those who hate peace. I ask You to restrain every hand that plans war and to give wisdom to every leader weighing a decision. Protect Israeli families and civilians in Iran and the region from harm. Where words of peace are spoken, let them be heard, and turn hearts toward peace. Amen.

Protect the innocent on every side, comfort the grieving, and bring an end to the cycle of killing, that no more children grow up afraid. Amen.

Restrain the violent, whoever they are, and let the law protect farmers, diplomats and neighbours alike. Make Your people kind and tenderhearted, so that Jewish and Arab families in the land may live without terror. Amen.

Shield them from injury, and guard their hearts and consciences. Be near those who carry heavy burdens in the north, in Gaza and on the roads of Judea and Samaria, and bring them home in peace. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The Keeper of Israel who never slumbers

> <sup>3</sup> He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber. <sup>4</sup> Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.
>
> — **Psalm 121:3-4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Keeper of Israel, and You neither slumber nor sleep. Through the nights of sirens and uncertainty I ask You to keep watch over the land and its borders, over cities and villages, and over every home. Do not let Israel's foot be moved. Frustrate the plans of those who intend harm, give early warning where danger gathers, and let the people of Israel lie down and sleep in peace under Your care. Amen.

### 7. Standing at God's right hand

> *I have set the LORD always before me: because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved.*
>
> — **Psalm 16:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I set You always before me, and I ask the same for Your people Israel. With You at their right hand, let soldiers, pilots and first responders not be moved. Let parents who send children to school, and workers who travel on dangerous roads, know Your nearness. Calm the fear that rises with every alert, and give leaders steady nerves in hard decisions, so that Your protection is real to those who trust in Your name. Amen.

### 8. A good and a stronghold in the day of trouble

> *The LORD is good, a strong hold in the day of trouble; and he knoweth them that trust in him.*
>
> — **Nahum 1:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are good, a stronghold in the day of trouble, and You know those who trust in You. I pray that for Israel and for Jews in every nation, You will be a refuge from the threat of missiles, drones and terror. Hold up those in shelters, hospitals and mourning homes. Let none who trust in You be left alone. Make known Your goodness in the very places where the pressure is greatest. Amen.

### 9. Lifting up the head in a time of fear

> *And when these things begin to come to pass, then look up, and lift up your heads; for your redemption draweth nigh.*
>
> — **Luke 21:28** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You told Your disciples to look up when trouble comes, for redemption draws near. I pray for Jewish communities in Paris, London, Manchester and elsewhere who have seen synagogues damaged or attacked. Guard their places of worship, their schools and their families. Give courage to those who stand guard, and wisdom to the police and officials who serve them. Lift up the heads of the frightened and let them know that You hold history in Your hands. Amen.

### 10. He delivered, He delivers, He will yet deliver

> *Who delivered us from so great a death, and doth deliver: in whom we trust that he will yet deliver us;*
>
> — **2 Corinthians 1:10** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have delivered, You do deliver, and in You we trust that You will yet deliver. I thank You for every life spared in Israel this year, and I ask for Your deliverance again from the dangers still ahead. Be faithful to Your covenant promises to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Protect Your people from the hands of enemies, from fear, and from despair, and keep them for the day You have appointed. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The righteous Branch and King

> <sup>5</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the LORD, that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth. <sup>6</sup> In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell safely: and this is his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS.
>
> — **Jeremiah 23:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the righteous Branch of David, the King who reigns and prospers, and THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS. I ask You to reveal Yourself to Israel in this way. Open the eyes of Jewish men and women, in Israel and in the nations, to see that Judah is saved and Israel dwells safely in You. Let them read the promises of the prophets and recognise their fulfilment in You. Amen.

### 12. The Redeemer who comes to Zion

> *And the Redeemer shall come to Zion, and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob, saith the LORD.*
>
> — **Isaiah 59:20** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Redeemer who came to Zion, and You call those who turn from transgression in Jacob. I pray that many in Israel will turn to You in repentance and trust. Draw them by Your kindness, not by fear. Soften the hardness that comes from grief and anger, and give them a new heart. Let the day come when Jacob calls on Your name as Redeemer. Amen.

### 13. He came to His own

> <sup>11</sup> He came unto his own, and his own received him not. <sup>12</sup> But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:
>
> — **John 1:11-12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You came to Your own, and Your own received You not. I grieve for that, and I ask for Your mercy on my Jewish friends and on the whole people. Let many receive You and be given the right to become children of God. Remove fear of rejection and family pain from those who consider You. Strengthen every Jewish believer who confesses Your name, and keep them safe and joyful. Amen.

### 14. Forgiveness of sins through Him

> <sup>38</sup> Be it known unto you therefore, men and brethren, that through this man is preached unto you the forgiveness of sins: <sup>39</sup> And by him all that believe are justified from all things, from which ye could not be justified by the law of Moses.
>
> — **Acts 13:38-39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through You is preached the forgiveness of sins, and everyone who believes is justified from all things. I pray that Jewish people everywhere will hear this good news with clarity and love. Send faithful and humble witnesses to Israel, to students and soldiers, to the elderly and the lonely. Use Your Word to bring peace with God to those who have long searched for it. Amen.

### 15. One LORD over all the earth

> *And the LORD shall be king over all the earth: in that day shall there be one LORD, and his name one.*
>
> — **Zechariah 14:9** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You will be King over all the earth, and there will be one LORD and one name. Hasten the day when Israel and all nations bow to You. Until then, make me a faithful friend of the Jewish people, praying with love like Paul in Romans 10:1. Give me patience with those who disagree, and let my life point to Your mercy. Gather Your people in Your time, and let Your kingdom come. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I commit Israel, the Jewish people everywhere, and the land of Judea and Samaria into Your hands. Protect the innocent, comfort the grieving, restrain the violent, and give leaders wisdom. Open the hearts of Your ancient people to know You as Messiah and Lord. Hear these prayers for Your name's sake. Amen.*
