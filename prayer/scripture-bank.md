# Scripture bank (King James Version)

Verified starting points for the daily prayer guide, grouped by theme. Every
reference below resolves in `data/kjv.tsv.gz`, is 4 verses or fewer, and ends
at a sentence break. The routine may use any other passage too; `scripts/build_prayer.py`
checks whatever it picks. Look up the exact wording with
`python3 scripts/bible.py "Psalm 121:3-4"` or search with
`python3 scripts/bible.py --search "keepeth Israel"`.
