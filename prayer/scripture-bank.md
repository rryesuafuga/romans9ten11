# Scripture bank (King James Version)

Verified starting points for the daily prayer guide, grouped by theme. Every
reference below resolves in `data/kjv.tsv.gz`, is 4 verses or fewer, and ends
at a sentence break. The routine may use any other passage too;
`scripts/build_prayer.py` checks whatever it picks. Each guide needs 5
protection and 5 salvation passages, none repeated within 7 days, so the two
largest sections below hold well over the 35 a week that requires.

Look up the exact wording with `python3 scripts/bible.py "Psalm 121:3-4"` or
search with `python3 scripts/bible.py --search "keepeth Israel"`.
