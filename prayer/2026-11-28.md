## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Wisdom for Israel's coalition negotiators before the 6 December deadline

**In the news:** [Eisenkot accuses Likud of stalling coalition talks](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/israels-eisenkot-accuses-netanyahus-party-of-stalling-coalition-talks); [Netanyahu said to be readying an offer of the first turn in a rotation government to Eisenkot](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-27-2026)

> <sup>17</sup> But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy. <sup>18</sup> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.
>
> — **James 3:17-18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the wisdom that comes from above. I pray for Gadi Eisenkot, Benjamin Netanyahu, Avigdor Liberman, Yair Golan, Mansour Abbas and every leader in Israel's coalition talks as the deadline of 6 December draws near. Where there is accusation and suspicion, give them wisdom that is pure, peaceable, gentle and easy to be intreated, without partiality and without hypocrisy. Keep them from deals made in fear or pride, and lead them to a government that serves the whole people justly. Let the fruit of righteousness be sown in peace by those who make peace, and let Your will be done in Israel. Amen.

### 2. The Haredi draft debate and unity in Israeli society

**In the news:** [Yashar and UTJ reported to agree on key principles of a Haredi draft law](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/report-yashar-utj-reach-agreement-on-key-principles-of-haredi-draft-law); [Yashar and UTJ reported to agree on key principles of a Haredi draft law](https://timesofisrael.com/utj-rabbis-said-to-give-go-ahead-to-launching-official-talks-with-eisenkot)

> <sup>10</sup> Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other. <sup>11</sup> Truth shall spring out of the earth; and righteousness shall look down from heaven.
>
> — **Psalm 85:10-11** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You bring mercy and truth together, and make righteousness and peace kiss each other. As Yashar and United Torah Judaism discuss a law on Haredi military service, and the rabbis of the community weigh their counsel, I ask You to bring that meeting of mercy and truth into these talks. Comfort the families of reservists who carry a heavy load, and honour the sincere love of Torah in yeshiva homes. Heal the anger and fear that divide Israeli society on this question. Let truth spring out of the earth in honest agreements, and let righteousness look down from heaven on a nation learning to bear its burdens together. Amen.

### 3. Gaza and Lebanon: restraint of violence and protection for the innocent

**In the news:** [Hamas consults Palestinian factions on a response to Israeli targeted killings in Gaza](https://jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-916264); [Israeli strikes reported in southern and eastern Lebanon as Aoun calls for withdrawal](https://arabamericannews.com/2026/11/27/middle-east-roundup-israeli-strikes-on-lebanon-and-gaza-saudi-houthi-fighting-and-u-s-iran-tensions)

> <sup>11</sup> Let him eschew evil, and do good; let him seek peace, and ensue it. <sup>12</sup> For the eyes of the Lord are over the righteous, and his ears are open unto their prayers: but the face of the Lord is against them that do evil.
>
> — **1 Peter 3:11-12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your eyes are over the righteous and Your ears are open to their prayers. Hamas leaders say they are consulting other factions on a response to Israel's strikes in Gaza, and strikes continue in southern Lebanon and the Beqaa while President Aoun calls for withdrawal. I ask You to restrain every plan of violence, to protect Israeli families near the borders, and to shelter the innocent in Gaza and Lebanon, including the children who have lost parents. Give Israel's commanders and soldiers wisdom and integrity. Turn hearts to eschew evil and do good, and let those in authority seek peace and pursue it. Amen.

### 4. Judea and Samaria: justice for the oppressed and an end to attacks

**In the news:** [Shin Bet says the number of Jewish participants in attacks on Palestinians has doubled to about 600](https://jpost.com/israel-news/article-916132)

> *Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.*
>
> — **Isaiah 1:17** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You call Your people to learn to do well, to seek judgment and to relieve the oppressed. The Shin Bet reports that the number of Jewish participants in attacks on Palestinians in Judea and Samaria has doubled to about six hundred, most from outposts and farms. I pray for Palestinian families whose homes, olive trees and livestock have been harmed, and for Jewish families in the communities of Judea and Samaria who long to live in safety. Give the security services, courts and commanders courage to act justly and without partiality. Turn the hearts of young men drawn to violence, and let them find in You a better way. Amen.

### 5. Jewish communities in Australia and Scotland facing Iran-linked attacks and intimidation

**In the news:** [Australia sanctions two men over the Iran-linked 2024 arson attacks on Adass Israel Synagogue and a Sydney restaurant](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/australia-sanctions-suspects-in-iran-linked-anti-semitic-attacks); [Scottish Jewish bodies call the police response to a protest outside Giffnock synagogue inadequate](https://jewishnews.co.uk/pro-palestinian-protesters-target-scottish-synagogue-over-aliyah-event)

> *The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?*
>
> — **Psalm 27:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the light and salvation of all who trust in You, and the strength of their lives. Australia has sanctioned two men it links to Iran's Revolutionary Guard over the 2024 arson attacks on Melbourne's Adass Israel Synagogue and a Sydney restaurant, and Jewish leaders in Scotland say the police response to a protest outside Giffnock synagogue was inadequate. I pray for Jewish families in Melbourne, Sydney and Glasgow who feel afraid. Be their light so that they need not fear. Give police and governments resolve to protect worshippers, expose hidden networks of hatred, and turn the hearts of those who intimidate. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The land and its borders

> *And I will plant them upon their land, and they shall no more be pulled up out of their land which I have given them, saith the LORD thy God.*
>
> — **Amos 9:15** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised through Amos to plant Your people upon their land, never again to be pulled up out of it. I pray for the cities, farms, kibbutzim and villages of Israel, from the Galilee and the Golan to the Negev and the Gaza border communities still rebuilding. Guard every border against those who plan infiltration, rocket fire and drone attacks. Give the families returning to the north and south courage to replant, rebuild and raise their children in peace. Let the people of Israel know that their dwelling in the land rests on Your faithful word and not on their own strength. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers, police and civilians

> *For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.*
>
> — **Psalm 91:11** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You give Your angels charge over those who trust You, to keep them in all their ways. I lift up the soldiers of the IDF on every front, the reservists and their families, the police who answer calls in Beersheba, Ramat Hasharon and every city, and the civilians who travel each day on roads and buses. Keep them from ambush, from crime and from attacks planned in secret. Give medics, firefighters and rescue teams strength and skill. Let those who stand watch at night know Your presence beside them, and bring every one of them home safely to the people who love them. Amen.

### 8. Jerusalem, the city of the great King

> <sup>1</sup> Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness. <sup>2</sup> Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, is mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King. <sup>3</sup> God is known in her palaces for a refuge.
>
> — **Psalm 48:1-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, great are You and greatly to be praised in the city of our God. Mount Zion is beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, the city of the great King, and You are known in her palaces for a refuge. I pray for Jerusalem's residents, Jewish and Arab, and for the pilgrims who walk her streets. Be a refuge in her homes and synagogues, at her gates and at the Western Wall. Guard her from violence and from the plans of those who would harm her. Let Jerusalem be a place where Your name is honoured and Your peace is known. Amen.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora

> *I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil.*
>
> — **John 17:15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You prayed to the Father not that Your own be taken out of the world, but that they be kept from the evil. I pray in that same spirit for Jewish communities across the world, in Cologne and Melbourne, in Glasgow and London, in Ukraine and Iraq, in the Americas and in Africa. Keep them from the evil of those who plot attacks, desecrate cemeteries and threaten worshippers. Watch over synagogues, schools and Shabbat tables. Give security volunteers alertness, and give neighbours courage to stand with them. Let them live openly and without fear wherever they are. Amen.

### 10. God's covenant faithfulness to the fathers

> *Now I say that Jesus Christ was a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises made unto the fathers:*
>
> — **Romans 15:8** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You came as a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises made to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. I thank You that Your faithfulness to Israel does not fail, even when nations rage and leaders falter. Guard the Jewish people as a people, through every threat from Iran and its allies and every rise in hatred. Remind Israel and the nations that the promises made to the fathers stand firm in You. Keep this people for the day when they will see Your truth fulfilled, and let Your covenant love be their shield. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Open hearts to receive Him

> <sup>11</sup> He came unto his own, and his own received him not. <sup>12</sup> But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:
>
> — **John 1:11-12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You came to Your own, and Your own did not receive You, yet to as many as received You, You gave power to become the sons of God. With the love of Paul for his kinsmen, I ask You to open the hearts of Jewish people in Israel and around the world to receive You. Remove the fear, the pain of history and the misunderstandings that have hidden You from them. Let them hear You speak, and let them see in You the One who came first to Israel. Give many the joy of believing on Your name. Amen.

### 12. The Prophet like Moses

> *The LORD thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken;*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 18:15** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Moses foretold that the LORD would raise up a Prophet from among his brethren, like unto him, and that Israel should hearken to Him. I believe You are that Prophet, born of the house of Israel, who spoke the words of the Father. I pray for rabbis, students and teachers who study the Torah of Moses, that as they read these words they would recognise You. Open the Scriptures to them as You did on the road to Emmaus, and let their hearts burn within them. Let Jewish people everywhere hearken to You and find life. Amen.

### 13. New life by the Spirit

> <sup>12</sup> Therefore prophesy and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, O my people, I will open your graves, and cause you to come up out of your graves, and bring you into the land of Israel. <sup>13</sup> And ye shall know that I am the LORD, when I have opened your graves, O my people, and brought you up out of your graves, <sup>14</sup> And shall put my spirit in you, and ye shall live, and I shall place you in your own land: then shall ye know that I the LORD have spoken it, and performed it, saith the LORD.
>
> — **Ezekiel 37:12-14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through Ezekiel the Lord promised to open the graves of His people, bring them into the land of Israel and put His Spirit in them so that they shall live. I thank You for the gathering of Jewish people to the land, and I ask You now for the second part of the promise: breathe Your Spirit into hearts weary from war, grief and division. Turn mourning into new life. Let Israelis who feel spiritually dry know repentance and forgiveness through You, and let them know that You, the Lord, have spoken it and performed it. Amen.

### 14. Faithful witnesses who teach and preach Jesus

> *And daily in the temple, and in every house, they ceased not to teach and preach Jesus Christ.*
>
> — **Acts 5:42** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your first followers, all of them Jews, did not cease daily in the temple and in every house to teach and preach You as the Christ. I pray for believers in Israel and in the diaspora who share Your name today, in Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Haifa and Beersheba, and in cities around the world. Give them courage, gentleness and wisdom. Protect them from harassment and discouragement. Let their homes be places where friends and neighbours hear the good news with clarity and love, and let many come to know You as Messiah and Lord. Amen.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus and the unity of His body

> <sup>28</sup> There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus. <sup>29</sup> And if ye be Christ’s, then are ye Abraham’s seed, and heirs according to the promise.
>
> — **Galatians 3:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, in You there is neither Jew nor Greek, for all are one, and those who are Yours are Abraham's seed and heirs according to the promise. I pray for Messianic Jewish congregations in Israel and around the world. Strengthen their pastors, their young people and their families serving in the army. Keep them faithful when they are misunderstood by their own people and sometimes by other Christians. Join believers from Uganda, Africa and every nation in love with these brothers and sisters, so that together we may honour You and pray for all Israel to be saved. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I thank You that Your heart's desire and prayer for Israel is that they might be saved. Hear these prayers for Israel's leaders, soldiers and families, for Judea and Samaria, for Gaza and Lebanon, and for Jewish communities in every land. Keep them by Your power, guide them by Your wisdom, and draw them to Yourself as Messiah and Lord. Let Your peace and Your salvation come to Zion. Amen.*
