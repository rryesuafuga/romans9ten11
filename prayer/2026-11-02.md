## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Coalition talks after a deadlocked election

**In the news:** [Eisenkot expected to receive first mandate as Herzog opens consultations](https://jfeed.com/news/eisenkot-first-mandate-herzog-consultations)

> <sup>10</sup> The LORD bringeth the counsel of the heathen to nought: he maketh the devices of the people of none effect. <sup>11</sup> The counsel of the LORD standeth for ever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations.
>
> — **Psalm 33:10-11** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that Your counsel stands for ever. As President Herzog begins consultations and Gadi Eisenkot is expected to receive the first mandate, with neither bloc holding 61 seats, I ask You to give every leader wisdom, humility and a desire to serve the whole nation. Let no scheme of pride or fear prevail, but bring Your good purposes to pass. Give Israel a stable government that protects life, seeks justice and keeps the peace, and let it be sworn in without violence or bitterness. Amen.

### 2. Settler violence and Palestinian families in Judea and Samaria

> *Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.*
>
> — **Isaiah 1:17** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I bring before You the families in Hebron and the villages of Judea and Samaria, and the reports of a record number of settler attacks in October. I pray for the injured and the frightened, for the soldiers and police who must keep order, and for those tempted to violence. Teach all of us to learn to do well, seek justice and relieve the oppressed. Restrain every hand that would harm the innocent, and let truth and fairness come to light for Jew and Arab alike. Amen.

### 3. Israel and the diaspora listening to one another

Give wisdom to those working on antisemitism and on the bond between communities, and let them glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ with one mind and one mouth. Amen.

### 4. The Westmount synagogue in Montreal

> *For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.*
>
> — **2 Timothy 1:7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the Jewish congregation in Westmount, Montreal, whose synagogue windows were broken in a suspected hate crime. Thank You that they still gathered for Shabbat. You have not given us the spirit of fear, but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind. Comfort the rabbi and the families, help the police to find those responsible, and guard every synagogue and Jewish school in Canada and around the world. Turn the hearts of those who hate toward You. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Surrounded as Jerusalem is surrounded

> <sup>1</sup> They that trust in the LORD shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever. <sup>2</sup> As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.
>
> — **Psalm 125:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are round about Your people as the mountains are round about Jerusalem. I ask You to guard the borders of Israel, from Gaza to Lebanon, and the people who live near them. Let those who trust in You be unmoved, and give peace to every family who sleeps near the sirens. Be a wall of protection around Jerusalem and every town, and let Your presence be felt from now and for ever. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians facing danger

> *Be strong and of a good courage, fear not, nor be afraid of them: for the LORD thy God, he it is that doth go with thee; he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 31:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the young soldiers, reservists and police of Israel, and for the civilians they defend. Make them strong and of a good courage. You go with them; You will not fail them nor forsake them. Keep them from ambush, accident and injury, give clear judgment in hard moments, and bring each one home safely to the people who love them. Protect their minds and hearts as well as their bodies. Amen.

### 8. If God is for us

> <sup>31</sup> What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us? <sup>32</sup> He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?
>
> — **Romans 8:31-32** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that the God who spared not His own Son is for us. For Israel and the Jewish people surrounded by enemies and accusations, I ask that You be their defender. Where hostile voices rise at home or abroad, let no plan against them succeed. Show Your people that the Father who gave His Son will freely give what they need, and strengthen all who feel alone and outnumbered today. Amen.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora in the care of the Father

> <sup>6</sup> Are not five sparrows sold for two farthings, and not one of them is forgotten before God? <sup>7</sup> But even the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Fear not therefore: ye are of more value than many sparrows.
>
> — **Luke 12:6-7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for Jewish families in the diaspora: in Europe, North America, Australia, Africa and beyond. Not one sparrow is forgotten before God, and You have numbered the very hairs of every head. Remove their fear. Guard children walking to school, worshippers entering synagogues, and students on campuses. Protect their buildings and give them courage to live openly as Jews. Raise up neighbours and officials who will stand with them. Amen.

### 10. A very present help in trouble

> <sup>1</sup> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. <sup>2</sup> Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea;
>
> — **Psalm 46:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. When the news is heavy and the ground seems to shake, steady the people of Israel and the Jewish people everywhere. Be their refuge in the shelter, on the road and in the hospital ward. Keep Your covenant faithfulness before them, so that they do not fear though the earth be removed, and let them know Your peace. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. A new covenant written on the heart

> <sup>33</sup> But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the LORD, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people. <sup>34</sup> And they shall teach no more every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the LORD: for they shall all know me, from the least of them unto the greatest of them, saith the LORD: for I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more.
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:33-34** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised a new covenant with the house of Israel, with Your law written in their hearts and every sin forgiven. I ask You to open Jewish hearts to You as Messiah and Lord. Let the people of Israel know You in a living way, from the least to the greatest, and let Your forgiveness reach every family. Bring them into the covenant You sealed with Your own blood. Amen.

### 12. A fountain opened for sin and uncleanness

> *In that day there shall be a fountain opened to the house of David and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem for sin and for uncleanness.*
>
> — **Zechariah 13:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You for the fountain opened to the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem for sin and for uncleanness. Bring many Jewish people to that fountain. Give them repentance, cleansing and peace with God in this life, and let those who have sought to keep the law find their rest in You, the One who takes away sin. Amen.

### 13. The same Lord over all

> <sup>12</sup> For there is no difference between the Jew and the Greek: for the same Lord over all is rich unto all that call upon him. <sup>13</sup> For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.
>
> — **Romans 10:12-13** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are rich to all who call on You, Jew and Gentile alike. I pray that Jewish people everywhere would call on the name of the Lord and be saved. Open their mouths to confess You as Lord and their hearts to believe. Raise up faithful messengers with kindness and truth, so that Your good news reaches those who have not yet heard, in Israel and throughout the diaspora. Amen.

### 14. His own received Him not

> <sup>11</sup> He came unto his own, and his own received him not. <sup>12</sup> But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:
>
> — **John 1:11-12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You came to Your own and Your own received You not, yet to as many as receive You You give power to become children of God. I weep over this, and I ask You to turn the hearts of Your own people toward You. Let Jewish men, women and children, young and old, receive You and become children of God. Strengthen Jewish believers who face loneliness or rejection, and keep them faithful. Amen.

### 15. The glory, the covenants and the promises

> <sup>4</sup> Who are Israelites; to whom pertaineth the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the service of God, and the promises; <sup>5</sup> Whose are the fathers, and of whom as concerning the flesh Christ came, who is over all, God blessed for ever. Amen.
>
> — **Romans 9:4-5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that to the Israelites belong the adoption, the glory, the covenants, the law, the service of God and the promises, and that You came from them according to the flesh. Fulfil Your promises to Israel. Give me a love for the Jewish people like Paul's, free of contempt, and let many of them come to know You as Christ, who is over all, God blessed for ever. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, thank You for hearing these prayers for Israel, for the Jews in Israel and in the diaspora, and for Judea and Samaria. Comfort the grieving, guard the vulnerable, give wisdom to leaders and bring Your peace to every home. Draw the Jewish people to Yourself as their Messiah and Lord, and help me to keep praying with love until You come. Amen.*
