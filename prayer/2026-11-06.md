## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Counsel and wisdom as Herzog assigns the mandate

**In the news:** [Eisenkot receives 67 Knesset recommendations; Herzog to assign mandate on Sunday](https://israelnationalnews.com/news/435750); [United Torah Judaism does not recommend Netanyahu again](https://france24.com/en/live-news/20261105-eisenkot-tipped-to-seek-israel-government-as-religious-party-drops-netanyahu)

> *Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.*
>
> — **Proverbs 11:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that there is safety in a multitude of counsellors. Israel's president is to assign the mandate on Sunday, with 67 recommendations for Gadi Eisenkot and 46 for Benjamin Netanyahu, and the parties still refuse to work together. I ask You to give every leader humility to listen, to take counsel, and to put the safety of the people before pride. Let a government be formed that protects every citizen, Jew and Arab, and keep the nation from bitterness and paralysis. Where voices are loud, raise up honest advisers, and make Your peace the fruit of these talks. Amen.

### 2. Peace-makers in Judea and Samaria

**In the news:** [Settlers move into homes near Jericho; clashes reported after IDF raid on outpost](http://jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-914020)

> <sup>17</sup> But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy. <sup>18</sup> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.
>
> — **James 3:17-18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for Judea and Samaria, where settlers have moved into homes near Jericho, an outpost was raided, and cars have been burned, with activists and a Palestinian man reportedly hurt. Pour out the wisdom from above that is pure, peaceable and gentle. Restrain those who plan violence on every side, protect the innocent, and comfort families who are afraid for their homes and their children. Raise up peacemakers who sow righteousness in peace, and let justice be done without hatred. Draw Israelis and Palestinians alike to Yourself, the Prince of Peace. Amen.

### 3. Safety on the northern front

**In the news:** [IDF reports finding over 340 Hezbollah weapons in southern Lebanon](https://israelnationalnews.com/news/435726); [IDF strikes two suspects near Qabrikha; Lebanese authorities report one killed](https://jns.org/news/israel-news/idf-chief-zamir-warns-hezbollah-on-visit-to-northern-front)

> *Ye that love the LORD, hate evil: he preserveth the souls of his saints; he delivereth them out of the hand of the wicked.*
>
> — **Psalm 97:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You preserve the souls of Your saints and deliver them from the hand of the wicked. I pray for Israeli soldiers in southern Lebanon, who are finding weapons and tunnel shafts in homes, and for the families waiting for them. Guard them from drones, ambushes and mistakes. I pray also for the civilians of Lebanon, that the innocent may be spared as the ceasefire is tested. Give wisdom to commanders and restraint to those who would fire. Keep the northern towns of Israel safe, and bring a lasting calm that comes from You. Amen.

### 4. Jewish communities attacked in Brussels and elsewhere

**In the news:** [Brick thrown through window of Brussels synagogue](https://clevelandjewishnews.com/jns/brick-thrown-through-brussels-synagogue-window/article_7e7e9343-108a-5e6c-98d9-777dd2217582.html)

> *The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.*
>
> — **Psalm 34:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the Orthodox Jewish community in Anderlecht, Brussels, where a brick shattered a synagogue window. Let the angel of the LORD encamp around those who fear Him and deliver them. Calm the fear of children and elders who worship there, lead the police to those responsible, and move the neighbours of Brussels to stand with their Jewish friends. Turn hearts that harbour hatred, and protect every congregation that gathers this week. Be near to them, and let them know they are not alone. Amen.

### 5. Comfort ahead of the Kristallnacht anniversary

**In the news:** [Diaspora Ministry warns synagogue attacks are growing more lethal](https://jpost.com/diaspora/article-913992)

> <sup>3</sup> Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort; <sup>4</sup> Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.
>
> — **2 Corinthians 1:3-4** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the Diaspora Ministry warns that attacks on synagogues are becoming more deadly, and the anniversary of Kristallnacht is near on 9 and 10 November. I bring before You the memory of those who were murdered, and the fears of Jews today who gather in worship with guards at the door. You are the Father of mercies and the God of all comfort. Comfort the bereaved and the frightened in all their tribulation, strengthen those who guard and those who lead, and give them confidence to comfort others. Let Your mercy and Your light be seen. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The Lord who gathers the outcasts of Israel

> <sup>2</sup> The LORD doth build up Jerusalem: he gathereth together the outcasts of Israel. <sup>3</sup> He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.
>
> — **Psalm 147:2-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You build up Jerusalem and gather the outcasts of Israel. I ask You to keep guarding the land and its people: the soldiers on every border, the families in the towns that have been hit, and the wounded in hospital. Heal the broken in heart and bind up their wounds, especially those who have lost sons, daughters and parents. Keep Jerusalem secure and bring home those who are far away. Let Your faithful hand be over the nation of Israel today. Amen.

### 7. No weapon formed against them shall prosper

> *No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is of me, saith the LORD.*
>
> — **Isaiah 54:17** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have promised that no weapon formed against Your people shall prosper. I pray that every missile, drone, knife and plot aimed at Israelis and at Jews around the world will fail. Open the eyes of those who guard, warn the watchmen in time, and confuse the plans of those who intend murder. I do not ask for harm on any person, but that violence be restrained and that those who plan it be turned back, for You love all people. Be the shield of Your people in every city. Amen.

### 8. Kept from the evil one

> *I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil.*
>
> — **John 17:15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You prayed that Your people should be kept from the evil. I pray that prayer now for Jewish families in Israel and in the diaspora who live with sirens, threats and suspicion. Keep them from the evil of violence, and also from despair, from bitterness and from fear that robs them of life. Protect children on the way to school, students on campus and the old in their homes. Surround each house with Your peace and keep them in Your name. Amen.

### 9. Delivered from every evil work

> *And the Lord shall deliver me from every evil work, and will preserve me unto his heavenly kingdom: to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.*
>
> — **2 Timothy 4:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You deliver Your own from every evil work. I ask You to protect Jewish people who are targeted for who they are: in synagogues, in kosher shops, at embassies and in the streets. Deliver them from attackers, from hateful rhetoric and from the loneliness of feeling unprotected. Strengthen police and officials to act quickly and fairly. Bring them safely to the end of every day, and preserve them for Your kingdom. To You be glory for ever and ever. Amen.

### 10. The faithful God who keeps covenant

> *Know therefore that the LORD thy God, he is God, the faithful God, which keepeth covenant and mercy with them that love him and keep his commandments to a thousand generations;*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 7:9** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the faithful God who keeps covenant and mercy to a thousand generations. I thank You that Your promises to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob have not been withdrawn. Remember Your people Israel in this season of uncertainty, and let them know that Your faithfulness is greater than politics and war. Hold the land, the cities and the scattered communities in Your covenant love. Make Israel a witness to Your steadfast mercy, and let the nations see that You keep Your word. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Looking on the One they pierced

> *And I will pour upon the house of David, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the spirit of grace and of supplications: and they shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him, as one mourneth for his only son, and shall be in bitterness for him, as one that is in bitterness for his firstborn.*
>
> — **Zechariah 12:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to pour out on Jerusalem the Spirit of grace and of supplications. I pray that Jewish people in Israel and around the world will look upon You, the One who was pierced, and mourn with a godly sorrow that leads to joy. Do this in homes, in the army, in universities and in synagogues. Let the Spirit of grace soften every heart and bring them to know You as Messiah and Lord. Amen.

### 12. The gospel to the Jew first

> *For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.*
>
> — **Romans 1:16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the gospel is the power of God unto salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first. Make me and Your church unashamed of it, and teach us to speak with love and respect, never with contempt. Give Jewish people the courage to hear it, to believe, and to confess You openly even when it costs them family and friends. Let many in Israel and the diaspora find in You forgiveness of sin and life that never ends. Amen.

### 13. Eyes opened to the Scriptures

> <sup>44</sup> And he said unto them, These are the words which I spake unto you, while I was yet with you, that all things must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me. <sup>45</sup> Then opened he their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures, <sup>46</sup> And said unto them, Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead the third day: <sup>47</sup> And that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.
>
> — **Luke 24:44-47** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, after Your resurrection You opened the understanding of Your disciples to see that the law of Moses, the prophets and the Psalms were about You. Open the minds of Jewish readers of the Torah and the Prophets today, in synagogues, in study halls and in quiet homes. Let them see You foretold in the Scriptures they love, and let it bring them to faith. Send them teachers, friends and believers who are gentle and wise. Amen.

### 14. A light to the nations, and salvation to the end of the earth

> *And he said, It is a light thing that thou shouldest be my servant to raise up the tribes of Jacob, and to restore the preserved of Israel: I will also give thee for a light to the Gentiles, that thou mayest be my salvation unto the end of the earth.*
>
> — **Isaiah 49:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Servant who raises up the tribes of Jacob and restores the preserved of Israel, and You are a light to the nations. I pray that the Jewish people would receive their Messiah and then carry Your light to the end of the earth. Strengthen Jewish believers in Jesus in Israel, in Judea and Samaria, in Africa and everywhere they live. Give them joy, courage and unity, and protect them from rejection and violence. Amen.

### 15. The gospel that saves

> <sup>3</sup> For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; <sup>4</sup> And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures:
>
> — **1 Corinthians 15:3-4** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You died for our sins according to the Scriptures, were buried, and rose again the third day. I pray that Jewish men, women and children would hear this good news and believe it. Forgive their sins and mine through Your blood, and give them peace with God. Make this the Passover and the Day of Atonement answered for them. Raise up many Jewish disciples who love You and tell others. For Your name and glory. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, thank You for hearing these prayers. I place Israel, Jews everywhere, Judea and Samaria and every grieving family into Your hands. Protect Your people, give wisdom to leaders, comfort the afraid, and bring Jewish people to know You as Messiah and Lord. Keep my own heart full of love and not of contempt. Let Your peace come to Jerusalem. Amen.*
