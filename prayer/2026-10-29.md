> *If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.*
>
> — **James 1:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that You give wisdom to all who ask and do not scold us for asking. The count after Israel's election leaves neither bloc with a majority, and weeks of negotiation may lie ahead. I ask You to give wisdom, humility and a spirit of service to every leader who must now build a government, Jewish and Arab alike. Calm the anxious, guard the nation's institutions, and let decisions be made for the good of all who live there. Keep any who would exploit the uncertainty from doing harm. Lord Jesus, You are the Prince of Peace; please let peace rest on this process. Amen.

### 2. The displaced families of Hebron

**In the news:** [Hebron operation enters fifth day; displacement reported near 5,000](https://peaceandjusticepost.com/2026/10/28/israeli-siege-of-hebron-displaces-nearly-5000-palestinians-amid-west-bank-wide-lockdown)

> <sup>9</sup> The LORD also will be a refuge for the oppressed, a refuge in times of trouble. <sup>10</sup> And they that know thy name will put their trust in thee: for thou, LORD, hast not forsaken them that seek thee.
>
> — **Psalm 9:9-10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a refuge for the oppressed and a refuge in times of trouble. Reports say the operation in Hebron, which followed the killing of two Israeli soldiers, has now displaced thousands of people, and the exact numbers are disputed. I ask You to shelter every family forced from home, Jewish and Arab, with food, safety and a way back. Protect the soldiers and the civilians in the city, restrain violence, and comfort the bereaved. Give commanders and officials restraint and justice. Let those who seek You find that You have not forsaken them. Amen.

### 3. Soldiers and civilians in Gaza and on the Lebanese border

**In the news:** [IDF says it killed Hamas members in Gaza, including an Oct. 7 kidnapper of Alexander Dancyg](https://jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-913087); [IDF fires interceptor at aerial target over south Lebanon](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/idf-says-it-launched-interceptor-at-target-over-south-lebanon)

>
> — **Psalm 57:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, be merciful to us, for our souls trust in You, and in the shadow of Your wings we make our refuge until these calamities are overpast. I pray for Israeli soldiers in Gaza and along the Lebanese border, and for civilians on every side who live under strikes, interceptors and fear. Keep them from harm, give them rest and clear judgment, and shield the innocent. Comfort families who wait for news. Bring an end to the fighting in Your time, and turn hearts from violence. Lord Jesus, hold the frightened close tonight. Amen.

**In the news:** [Israel revokes travel authorisations for American rabbis and Brad Lander after West Bank olive harvest](https://jta.org/2026/10/27/israel/israel-revokes-travel-permissions-for-brad-lander-american-rabbis); [Liberal US Jewish groups welcome exit polls as rebuke to Netanyahu](https://haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-28/ty-article/.premium/liberal-u-s-jewish-groups-hail-israeli-exit-polls-as-rebuke-to-netanyahu/000001a1-7f54-dbda-a9e5-ffde028a0000)

> <sup>2</sup> With all lowliness and meekness, with longsuffering, forbearing one another in love; <sup>3</sup> Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.
>
> — **Ephesians 4:2-3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You call us to lowliness, meekness and forbearing one another in love. Jewish communities abroad are debating the election and the revoking of some rabbis' travel permissions to Israel, and feelings run strong. I ask You to guard the bond between Israel and the diaspora, and to keep disagreement from turning to contempt. Give Jewish leaders, and all who speak about Israel, gentleness and truth. Keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace wherever Your people gather. Heal what is wounded in these relationships, and let love for one another be seen. Amen.

### 5. A Jewish community under threat in Belgrade

> <sup>19</sup> Oh how great is thy goodness, which thou hast laid up for them that fear thee; which thou hast wrought for them that trust in thee before the sons of men! <sup>20</sup> Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy presence from the pride of man: thou shalt keep them secretly in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.
>
> — **Psalm 31:19-20** · KJV · Old Testament

I ask You to heal the guard and anyone else hurt. Hide Israeli diplomats, Jewish communities and synagogues in the secret of Your presence from the pride of man. Give police and security teams vigilance and calm, and move governments to protect their Jewish citizens. Turn the hearts of those who hate, and let no one fear to be Jewish in any land. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The land, its borders and its defenders

> *The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms: and he shall thrust out the enemy from before thee; and shall say, Destroy them.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 33:27** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the eternal refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms. I ask You to be the refuge of everyone who lives in the land of Israel, and to hold up the soldiers who guard its borders in the north, the south and the east. Give them courage and wisdom, keep them from needless harm, and bring them home to their families. Protect the villages and cities under threat, and give the nation rest from its enemies. Let Your arms hold every frightened child tonight. Amen.

### 7. Jerusalem and its watchmen

> <sup>6</sup> I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night: ye that make mention of the LORD, keep not silence, <sup>7</sup> And give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth.
>
> — **Isaiah 62:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have set watchmen upon the walls of Jerusalem. I join them and will not keep silent. Guard this city, its holy places, its streets and every person in them, whatever their faith or origin. Give its leaders and police patience and righteousness, restrain those who plan violence, and let Jerusalem become a praise in the earth, a place of safety and of peace. Make me a faithful watchman who prays day and night for her. Amen.

### 8. God's covenant faithfulness to His people

> *For the LORD will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance.*
>
> — **Psalm 94:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You will not cast off Your people, nor forsake Your inheritance. I thank You that the promises You made to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob do not fail. When Israel is accused, doubted or abandoned by the nations, remind it that You remain faithful. Strengthen the Jewish people with the knowledge that they are not forgotten, and let Your faithfulness be a rock under their feet in every uncertain season. Amen.

### 9. Jews and the people of Israel not cast away

> *I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.*
>
> — **Romans 11:1** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that God has not cast away His people, and that Paul, an Israelite himself, rested on that truth. Where Jews hear that they are rejected, replaced or unwelcome, speak peace and dignity over them. Raise up friends and defenders who stand with them in every nation, and let the church love the Jewish people as You do. Protect Jewish children in schools, families on their way to worship, and the elderly who remember darker days. Amen.

### 10. Salvation from enemies and from the hand of all who hate

> <sup>68</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people, <sup>69</sup> And hath raised up an horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David; <sup>70</sup> As he spake by the mouth of his holy prophets, which have been since the world began: <sup>71</sup> That we should be saved from our enemies, and from the hand of all that hate us;
>
> — **Luke 1:68-71** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have visited and redeemed Your people, and raised up a horn of salvation in the house of David. I pray that You would save Israel from those who hate it, and deliver Jewish communities from the hand of every attacker, whether on the border, in the street or online. Defeat plans of violence before they are carried out, and bring enemies to turn from hatred. Give the nation the deep peace that only the Son of David can bring. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. A heartfelt longing for Israel

> <sup>1</sup> I say the truth in Christ, I lie not, my conscience also bearing me witness in the Holy Ghost, <sup>2</sup> That I have great heaviness and continual sorrow in my heart. <sup>3</sup> For I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh:
>
> — **Romans 9:1-3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I share the heaviness of Paul for his brethren, my kinsmen in Your story. Give me a sincere love for the Jewish people, free of pride and contempt. Make me willing to pray without ceasing, and to weep as You wept over Jerusalem. I ask that many Jewish men and women in Israel and across the world would come to know You as their Messiah and Lord, and that I would never speak of them except with honour and love. Amen.

### 12. The Redeemer who comes to Zion

> *And the Redeemer shall come to Zion, and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob, saith the LORD.*
>
> — **Isaiah 59:20** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Redeemer who came to Zion and to those in Jacob who turn from transgression. Open the eyes of Jewish people to see You in the prophets they read in synagogue and at home. Show them that You are the Messiah promised to Israel, born in Bethlehem, who bore the sins of His people. Let their turning be real, and let them find in You the forgiveness and righteousness they seek. Amen.

### 13. Israel returning to seek the Lord and David their king

> *Afterward shall the children of Israel return, and seek the LORD their God, and David their king; and shall fear the LORD and his goodness in the latter days.*
>
> — **Hosea 3:5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the prophet foretold that Israel would return and seek the LORD their God and David their king. You are the Son of David. I ask You to draw the hearts of the children of Israel to Yourself, in these uncertain days when many are searching for hope and security. Meet them in their grief, their anxiety and their questions. Let them fear You and Your goodness, and find that You are the answer they have been longing for. Amen.

### 14. Forgiveness of sins through Him

> <sup>38</sup> Be it known unto you therefore, men and brethren, that through this man is preached unto you the forgiveness of sins: <sup>39</sup> And by him all that believe are justified from all things, from which ye could not be justified by the law of Moses.
>
> — **Acts 13:38-39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through You is preached the forgiveness of sins, and everyone who believes is justified from all that the law of Moses could not free us from. I ask You to bring this good news to Jewish people everywhere, in plain words and with great love. Raise up faithful witnesses, Jewish and Gentile, with patience, humility and courage. Remove fear of rejection from new believers, and give them joyful assurance of Your pardon. Amen.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus and the blessed hope

> <sup>13</sup> Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ; <sup>14</sup> Who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify unto himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works.
>
> — **Titus 2:13-14** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You gave Yourself for us to redeem us from all iniquity and to purify a people for Yourself. I pray for Jewish believers in You, in Israel and around the world, who are often misunderstood or pressured. Keep them faithful, joyful and bold, and zealous for good works. Unite Jewish and Gentile believers in one body, and open the eyes of those still seeking so that they read Moses and the prophets and see You. Bring honour to Your name in the land and in the nations. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring before You Israel, the Jewish people in the land and in every nation, and the people of Judea and Samaria. Protect the innocent, comfort the grieving, and give wisdom to those in authority. Draw Your people Israel to know You as Messiah and Lord, and let Your peace, which passes understanding, keep our hearts. To You be glory forever. Amen.*
