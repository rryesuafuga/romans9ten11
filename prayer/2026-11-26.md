## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Safety in the land and along Israel's borders

> *I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety.*
>
> — **Psalm 4:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You alone make Your people dwell in safety. I ask You to guard the land of Israel and its borders with Lebanon, Syria and Gaza, and the towns of the north and south whose families still sleep near danger. Let them lie down in peace and sleep, not because of walls or weapons only, but because You watch over them. Expose and frustrate every plan of attack, guard the people of Judea and Samaria, Jewish and Arab alike, from violence, and give rest to a weary nation.

### 7. A shield for soldiers, police and civilians

> *But thou, O LORD, art a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head.*
>
> — **Psalm 3:3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a shield and the lifter up of the head. I ask You to be a shield around the soldiers of the IDF, the police and the emergency teams, and around ordinary civilians on buses, in schools, homes and markets. Lift the heads of the wounded, the traumatised and those still carrying grief from October 7 and the wars since. Give strength to the reservists and their families, and courage to all who guard life without cruelty. Let them know that their true help comes from You.

### 8. Jerusalem, a quiet habitation

> *Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities: thine eyes shall see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, a tabernacle that shall not be taken down; not one of the stakes thereof shall ever be removed, neither shall any of the cords thereof be broken.*
>
> — **Isaiah 33:20** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You wept over Jerusalem and will reign there in peace. Your word promises that Jerusalem shall be a quiet habitation, a tent whose stakes shall never be removed. I pray for the peace of the city today, for Jews, Arabs and Christians who live in it, for its holy places and its streets. Restrain anyone who plans terror or riot, and give the police wisdom and fairness. Keep the city from being a cause of war, and let it be quiet until it welcomes You as King.

### 9. Peace of heart for Jews in the diaspora

> <sup>6</sup> Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. <sup>7</sup> And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.
>
> — **Philippians 4:6-7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, many Jewish families in Europe, Russia, Ukraine, the Americas, Africa and beyond live with anxiety about threats to their synagogues, schools and cemeteries. I bring their requests before You with thanksgiving for every plot that has been foiled. Guard their communities through watchful neighbours, honest governments and diligent police. And give them the peace of God that passes all understanding, which keeps hearts and minds through You, Christ Jesus, so that fear does not rule their homes.

### 10. God's covenant faithfulness to Israel

> *Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.*
>
> — **Hebrews 13:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee. I thank You that the Father's gifts and calling toward Israel are not withdrawn, and that You have kept the Jewish people through exile, persecution and war. When Israel feels isolated and alone among the nations, remind them that You have not forsaken them. Keep Your promises to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, preserve the people You have loved, and let the nations see Your faithfulness in their survival.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Light in the heart: the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ

> *For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.*
>
> — **2 Corinthians 4:6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the God who commanded light to shine out of darkness can shine into any heart. I pray for the Jewish people in Israel and in every land, that You would give them the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in Your face. Lift the veil from their eyes as they read Moses and the Prophets. Meet secular and religious, young and old, in the quiet places of their lives, and show them that You are the Messiah they have been waiting for.

### 12. The suffering Servant foretold by the prophets

> *I gave my back to the smiters, and my cheeks to them that plucked off the hair: I hid not my face from shame and spitting.*
>
> — **Isaiah 50:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You gave Your back to the smiters and did not hide Your face from shame and spitting. I pray that Jewish readers of Isaiah would recognise You in these words, the Servant who suffered willingly for others. Let rabbis, students and seekers study the prophets with open hearts and see how You fulfilled them. Remove the misunderstandings that centuries of Christian cruelty have caused, and draw Your own brethren to the One who bore shame for them.

### 13. Repentance and full forgiveness

> *Come now, and let us reason together, saith the LORD: though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow; though they be red like crimson, they shall be as wool.*
>
> — **Isaiah 1:18** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through Isaiah You invited, Come now, and let us reason together, promising that sins as scarlet shall be white as snow. I pray that many Jewish people would hear this invitation and come to You in repentance, finding the cleansing that no sacrifice or effort could give. Let them know that Your blood was shed for them, and that forgiveness is offered freely. Take away guilt and shame, and give them clean hearts and new life in You, their Messiah and Lord.

### 14. Faithful witnesses who show from the Scriptures that Jesus is the Christ

> *For he mightily convinced the Jews, and that publickly, shewing by the scriptures that Jesus was Christ.*
>
> — **Acts 18:28** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Apollos mightily convinced the Jews, showing by the Scriptures that You are the Christ. Raise up witnesses like him today, in Israel and in the diaspora, who know the Scriptures well and speak with both boldness and love. Give Christians humility, repentance for past contempt, and a clear testimony of You. Open doors in homes, campuses and workplaces, and let Your word do its work, so that many are persuaded that You are the promised Messiah.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus and a growing harvest

> *Then they that gladly received his word were baptized: and the same day there were added unto them about three thousand souls.*
>
> — **Acts 2:41** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, on the day of Pentecost about three thousand Jewish souls gladly received Your word and were added to Your people in Jerusalem. I pray for Messianic Jewish believers in Israel and around the world, that You would strengthen their congregations, protect them from hostility and isolation, and make them bold and joyful witnesses to their families. Add to their number daily, Lord, until there is again a great harvest among Your own people, gladly receiving Your word.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, my heart's desire and prayer to God for Israel is that they might be saved. Keep Israel and the Jewish people everywhere under Your shield, give wisdom to their leaders, comfort to the grieving, and peace to Jerusalem. Shine the light of Your glory into many hearts, and let them know You as Messiah and Lord. I ask this in Your holy name. Amen.*
