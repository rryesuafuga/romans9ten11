## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The Keeper who delivers from the snare

> <sup>7</sup> Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we are escaped. <sup>8</sup> Our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth.
>
> — **Psalm 124:7-8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth. I pray for Israel and for Jews everywhere who have been hunted by snares, whether open attack or hidden plots. Break the snares of those who plan violence, let the intelligence and watchfulness of the protectors be sharp, and let the people escape as a bird from the fowler. Be the help of every family who feels exposed tonight, and let them know You are near. Amen.

### 7. God does not forget His people

> <sup>15</sup> Can a woman forget her sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? yea, they may forget, yet will I not forget thee. <sup>16</sup> Behold, I have graven thee upon the palms of my hands; thy walls are continually before me.
>
> — **Isaiah 49:15-16** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that a mother may forget her child, yet You will not forget Your people. I pray for Israel's bereaved, wounded and displaced, for soldiers' families and for those who feel forgotten by the world. Remind each one that their walls are continually before You and their names are graven on Your palms. Comfort the mourning, bind the wounds, and hold every child who waits for a parent to return. Let Your faithful compassion be their daily bread. Amen.

### 8. Soldiers and civilians kept in the hand of the Lord

> <sup>28</sup> And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. <sup>29</sup> My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.
>
> — **John 10:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Good Shepherd, and no one can pluck Your sheep from Your hand. I pray for the soldiers on Israel's northern and southern borders and for civilians in range of rockets and drones. Keep them safe in body and mind, and give their commanders wisdom and restraint. Above all, hold them securely in Your hand for eternity. May many who serve and many who shelter come to know You as the One who gives eternal life. Amen.

### 9. If God is for them, who can be against them

> <sup>31</sup> What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us? <sup>32</sup> He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?
>
> — **Romans 8:31-32** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Son of God who was given for us all. I pray for the Jewish people in the face of threats from Iran, from Hezbollah and from hatred around the world. Let them know that if God is for them, no enemy can finally prevail. Restrain those who plan evil and turn their plans aside, and teach Your people to trust You rather than weapons alone. Give them the whole of Your goodness, and bring protection and peace to their land. Amen.

### 10. The covenant love that is a refuge and a shield

> *For the LORD God is a sun and shield: the LORD will give grace and glory: no good thing will he withhold from them that walk uprightly.*
>
> — **Psalm 84:11** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a sun and a shield, and You give grace and glory. I ask You to be a shield over Jerusalem, over the towns of Galilee and the Negev, and over Jewish neighbourhoods and synagogues in every nation. Give grace to leaders who must decide in hard hours, and glory to Your own name by keeping Your promises to Israel. Withhold no good thing from those who walk uprightly, and draw the whole people to walk with You. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The Suffering Servant bearing the iniquity of us all

> <sup>5</sup> But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed. <sup>6</sup> All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned every one to his own way; and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.
>
> — **Isaiah 53:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the prophet Isaiah described You wounded for our transgressions and bruised for our iniquities, and the LORD laid on You the iniquity of us all. I long for my Jewish friends and the whole people of Israel to read this chapter and see You in it. Remove every veil, remove every fear of what it costs, and let them find peace and healing in Your stripes. Bring them gently to the One who carried their sin. Amen.

### 12. Seeking with all the heart

> *And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart.*
>
> — **Jeremiah 29:13** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised that those who search for You with all their heart will find You. I pray for every Jewish man and woman who is searching: in Tel Aviv and Jerusalem, in the kibbutzim and the settlements, and in London, New York and Kampala. Stir hunger for truth in the quiet hour. Meet each seeker, answer their questions, and let them find that the God of their fathers has come near in You. Amen.

### 13. A new heart and a new spirit

> <sup>26</sup> A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. <sup>27</sup> And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.
>
> — **Ezekiel 36:26-27** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to take away the stony heart and give a heart of flesh, and to put Your Spirit within Your people. I ask this for Israel. Pour out Your Spirit on the nation, soften hearts hardened by loss, fear and long sorrow, and cause them to walk in Your ways. Let many receive new life through faith in You, the Messiah, and let forgiveness and a new beginning be real in their homes. Amen.

### 14. God so loved the world

> <sup>16</sup> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life. <sup>17</sup> For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved.
>
> — **John 3:16-17** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the only begotten Son, given because God so loved the world, and sent not to condemn but to save. I pray that Jewish people everywhere would hear this good news in love, without pressure or contempt, and that they would believe and have everlasting life. Give believers the same love for Israel that You have. Make their witness gentle and clear, and let many say, My Lord and my God. Amen.

### 15. The gospel according to the Scriptures

> <sup>3</sup> For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; <sup>4</sup> And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures:
>
> — **1 Corinthians 15:3-4** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You died for our sins according to the Scriptures, were buried, and rose again the third day according to the Scriptures. I pray for the Jewish believers in You, in Israel and across the diaspora, that they would stand firm and speak with courage. Open the eyes of their families and friends to the Scriptures that testify of You. Let the news of Your resurrection bring joy where there has been grief, and bring many to faith in You as Lord. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring Israel, the Jewish people in the land and across the world, and the people of Judea and Samaria before You. Protect the innocent, comfort the grieving, give wisdom to leaders in this election season, and keep Your people safe. Draw Jewish hearts to You as Messiah and Lord, with the love of Romans 10:1. Hear us for Your name's sake. Amen.*
