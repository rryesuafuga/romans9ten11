## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Arson in Palestinian villages and a call to name it plainly

**In the news:** [Overnight settler arson in several Palestinian villages; Eisenkot calls it "Jewish terrorism"](https://cnn.com/2026/11/22/middleeast/west-bank-attacks-eisenkot-intl); [Settler leader condemns the arson but rejects the "Jewish terrorism" label](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/settler-leader-condemns-arson-attacks-in-palestinian-villages-but-criticizes-those-calling-it-jewish-terrorism)

> *He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the LORD require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?*
>
> — **Micah 6:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, homes and cars were set on fire overnight in Palestinian villages of Judea and Samaria, and leaders in Israel are arguing over what to call it. You have shown what is good: to do justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with God. I ask You to give the police and the army the will and skill to find those responsible and to hold them to account. Comfort the families who woke to burned property and fear. Turn the hearts of any who plan more violence, and give Israel's leaders, settler leaders among them, the humility to condemn wrong without excuse. Let justice and mercy walk together in this land.

**In the news:** [Reports of settler attacks on olive harvesters and families; a Palestinian shot dead near Ramallah](https://haaretz.com/west-bank/2026-11-21/ty-article/.premium/idf-shoots-at-palestinian-olive-harvesters-as-settlers-attack-west-bank-villages/000001a1-ff25-d191-a7fd-ff6750ba0000)

> <sup>3</sup> Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy. <sup>4</sup> Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked.
>
> — **Psalm 82:3-4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You command that the poor and fatherless be defended and the afflicted and needy be delivered. I pray for the olive harvesters who went out to their trees and were met with attacks and gunfire, and for the family of the Palestinian man shot dead near Ramallah on Friday. Be near to the grieving, and heal the wounded. Give soldiers and commanders clear sight to protect every innocent person in their care, and let the truth of what happened be found and acted on. Restrain the hand of those who would harm the weak, and bring safety to farmers, children and families on every side.

### 3. Lebanon's leaders, a school in the south, and a stalled ceasefire

**In the news:** [Lebanese President Aoun demands Israeli withdrawal from the south and criticises Iran's interference](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/lebanese-president-demands-israel-withdrawal-decries-irans-interference); [Lebanese PM condemns IDF spokeswoman's visit to a school in southern Lebanon](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/lebanese-pm-condemns-idf-spokeswomans-visit-to-southern-lebanon-school)

> *Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.*
>
> — **Matthew 5:9** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You called the peacemakers blessed. Lebanon's president has demanded Israel's withdrawal from the south and criticised Iran's interference, its prime minister has protested an IDF visit to a school, and the June framework is stalled while Hezbollah keeps its weapons. I pray for the children of southern Lebanon and northern Israel, that their classrooms and homes would be safe. Give Israeli and Lebanese leaders wisdom and courage to make a lasting peace, and restrain every armed group that plans harm. Protect soldiers on the border and civilians on both sides, and raise up true peacemakers in this troubled region.

>
> — **Ephesians 4:2-3** · KJV · New Testament

Israel is divided over who should serve, and the debate runs deep into faith, family and fairness. I ask You for lowliness, meekness and longsuffering among all sides, so that people forbear one another in love. Give Israel's lawmakers and its next government wisdom to settle this question justly and peaceably. Keep protests free of violence, and keep the unity of the people in the bond of peace while the nation faces danger from outside.

> — **1 Timothy 4:12** · KJV · New Testament

Your word says, let no man despise thy youth. I pray for these young people in France and in every country they came from: keep them safe at school and in the street, and let them not be despised or harmed for who they are. Bless the new work in Drancy, a place of deep sorrow. Above all, draw these teens to know You, the Messiah of Israel, and make them examples in word, faith and purity.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The Lord round about Jerusalem

> *As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.*
>
> — **Psalm 125:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, as the mountains stand round about Jerusalem, so the Lord is round about His people from henceforth even for ever. I ask You to surround Jerusalem and every town and village of Israel with Your protection. Guard its streets, its light rail and markets, its schools and synagogues, and its homes from attack. Watch over its borders in the north, the south and the east, and keep watch over those who stand guard. Let the people of Jerusalem lie down and rise up in safety, knowing that You are round about them.

### 7. A very present help in trouble for soldiers and civilians

> <sup>1</sup> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. <sup>2</sup> Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea; <sup>3</sup> Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof. Selah.
>
> — **Psalm 46:1-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Israel's soldiers serve on many fronts, and its civilians live under the threat of missiles from Iran and its allies. I pray that You will be their refuge: shelter them in attacks, give soldiers alertness and courage, and bring them home safely to their families. When the earth seems removed and the mountains shake, take away fear from the hearts of mothers, fathers and children. Comfort the wounded and the bereaved, and let Israel find its strength in You.

### 8. The seed of Israel kept as a nation

> <sup>35</sup> Thus saith the LORD, which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar; The LORD of hosts is his name: <sup>36</sup> If those ordinances depart from before me, saith the LORD, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever.
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:35-36** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You who give the sun by day and the moon and stars by night have promised that the seed of Israel shall not cease from being a nation before You. I thank You for Your faithfulness that has kept the Jewish people through every age. I pray that You will preserve Israel today against every plan to destroy it. Uphold the nation through its political uncertainty and its wars, and let the nations see that Your word stands. As surely as the sun rises tomorrow, keep Your covenant people.

### 9. Kept from evil, wherever they live

> *But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil.*
>
> — **2 Thessalonians 3:3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are faithful, and You stablish and keep Your people from evil. I pray for Jews in the diaspora: in Paris and Brussels, in Toronto and Montreal, in London, New York, Johannesburg and every city where they live. Keep them from evil in their homes, schools, synagogues and workplaces. Expose and restrain those who plan attacks, and give police and community guards wisdom and vigilance. Stablish Jewish families in courage, so that fear does not rule their lives, and surround them with neighbours who stand with them.

### 10. No one able to pluck them from His hand

> <sup>28</sup> And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. <sup>29</sup> My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.
>
> — **John 10:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that no man is able to pluck Your sheep out of Your hand or Your Father's hand. I pray for the Jewish people You have called and are calling, in Israel and beyond: hold them fast. Where hatred rises against them, be their shield. Where they are threatened by war, terror or slander, let no power be able to snatch them away from Your care. Above all, give them the eternal life that is in You alone, so that they shall never perish.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. A new heart and a new spirit

> *A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh.*
>
> — **Ezekiel 36:26** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through the prophet Ezekiel You promised Israel a new heart and a new spirit, to take away the stony heart and give a heart of flesh. I ask You to do this work among the Jewish people today, in Israel and in every land. Soften hearts that have been hardened by suffering, by misunderstanding of You, or by the failures of those who carried Your name. Let Your Spirit move in homes, yeshivas, army units and universities, so that many receive the new life You died and rose to give.

### 12. The child born, the Prince of Peace

> *For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.*
>
> — **Isaiah 9:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the child born and the Son given, Wonderful, Counsellor, the mighty God, the everlasting Father, the Prince of Peace. As Israel searches for a government and longs for peace on its borders, I pray that Jewish people will see that the government rests on Your shoulder. Open the eyes of those who read Isaiah to recognise You as the Messiah he foretold. Let the peace that leaders cannot secure be found in You, and let many in Israel call You by Your names.

### 13. The Deliverer out of Sion

> <sup>26</sup> And so all Israel shall be saved: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob: <sup>27</sup> For this is my covenant unto them, when I shall take away their sins.
>
> — **Romans 11:26-27** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Deliverer who comes out of Sion to turn away ungodliness from Jacob, and Your covenant is to take away their sins. I pray for the day when all Israel shall be saved, and I ask You to bring that day near. Turn Jewish hearts to You in repentance and faith, and take away their sins by Your blood. Let the promise to Jacob be fulfilled in this generation, in families in Jerusalem, Tel Aviv and the towns of Judea and Samaria, and in Jewish communities around the world.

### 14. Repentance and forgiveness in His name

> *Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.*
>
> — **Acts 2:38** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, on the day of Pentecost Peter called the people of Jerusalem to repent and be baptized in Your name for the remission of sins, and thousands believed. I pray that Your Spirit would again convict and draw Jewish people to repentance and faith in You. Give faithful witnesses, Jewish and Gentile, the courage and love to share the good news clearly and gently. Let many receive forgiveness and the gift of the Holy Ghost, and let Your church walk with them in humility and love.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus: not cast away

> *I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.*
>
> — **Romans 11:1** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Paul, an Israelite of the tribe of Benjamin, testified that God has not cast away His people. I thank You for Jewish believers in You today, in Israel and the diaspora. Strengthen them when they face rejection from family or community, and when they are misunderstood by other Christians. Give their congregations wisdom, unity and good leaders. Let their lives show their fellow Jews that following You, the Messiah, is not leaving their people but finding their people's hope, and let many more join them.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring before You Israel, the Jewish people in every land, and all who live in Judea and Samaria. Comfort the grieving, protect the innocent on every side, and give wisdom to leaders in Israel and Lebanon. Keep Your covenant people from evil, and open their eyes to see You as their Messiah and Lord. Let Your peace and Your salvation come to Zion. In Your name I pray. Amen.*
