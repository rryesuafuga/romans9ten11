Expose and restrain those who plan terror, protect the innocent on every side, and bring the day when no one in Gaza or Israel needs to mourn the dead of war. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Salvation for walls and bulwarks: the land and its borders

> *In that day shall this song be sung in the land of Judah; We have a strong city; salvation will God appoint for walls and bulwarks.*
>
> — **Isaiah 26:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word speaks of a strong city in the land of Judah, where God appoints salvation for walls and bulwarks. I ask You to be the true defence of Israel's land and borders: in the north facing Lebanon, in the south beside Gaza, in the Golan and along the Jordan valley. Watch over the fences, crossings and watchtowers, and over the people who live within sight of them. Frustrate every plan to infiltrate, kidnap or kill. Let Israel trust not in walls alone but in You, the God of salvation, and let their song in that day be of Your faithfulness. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians under the shadow of the Almighty

> <sup>1</sup> He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. <sup>2</sup> I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
>
> — **Psalm 91:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the refuge and fortress of all who dwell in the secret place of the Most High. I pray for the soldiers of the IDF on every front, for reservists away from their families, and for the civilians in towns near the borders who live with sirens and alarms. Cover them under the shadow of the Almighty. Guard them on patrol, in their homes and on the roads. Give rest to the weary, healing to the wounded and strength to the families who wait. Let many of them say of You, my God, in whom I will trust. Amen.

### 8. The peace of Jerusalem

> <sup>6</sup> Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee. <sup>7</sup> Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces.
>
> — **Psalm 122:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word tells me to pray for the peace of Jerusalem, and I do so now. Let peace be within her walls and prosperity within her palaces. Protect the people who live and worship in the city: Jews, Arabs and Christians, residents and pilgrims, children going to school and workers on the light rail. Keep the holy sites from becoming flashpoints of violence. Guard the Knesset and the government offices in this tense season, and give the city's leaders wisdom. You wept over Jerusalem and will one day reign there; until then, keep her and bless all who love her. Amen.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora held in the love of God

> <sup>38</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, <sup>39</sup> Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
>
> — **Romans 8:38-39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, nothing present and nothing to come, no height and no depth, can separate Your people from the love of God. I pray for Jewish people scattered across the nations: in Europe, the Americas, Africa, Australia and Asia, in large communities and small ones. Where threats, graffiti, harassment and attacks rise, keep them safe. Guard synagogues, schools, shops and homes, and protect the young on university campuses. Give them friends among their neighbours, and give authorities courage to defend them. Let no power on earth succeed in its plans against them, and let them come to know Your love. Amen.

### 10. God's mercy remembered to Abraham and his seed

> <sup>54</sup> He hath holpen his servant Israel, in remembrance of his mercy; <sup>55</sup> As he spake to our fathers, to Abraham, and to his seed for ever.
>
> — **Luke 1:54-55** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, at Your coming Mary sang that God had helped His servant Israel in remembrance of His mercy, as He spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and to his seed for ever. I thank You that Your promises to the Jewish people have not been withdrawn. In this season of war, political strain and rising hatred, remember Your mercy to Israel again. Keep the nation and the people from destruction, as You have kept them through every generation. Let Your faithfulness to Abraham's seed be seen by the nations, and let it lead Israel to know You, the Son of David. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The covering removed from the eyes

> *And he will destroy in this mountain the face of the covering cast over all people, and the vail that is spread over all nations.*
>
> — **Isaiah 25:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have promised to destroy the covering cast over all people and the veil that is spread over all nations. I pray for Jewish people in Israel and around the world who have never seen You as their Messiah. Remove the veil from their eyes and hearts. Where centuries of hurt done in Your name have made Your name hard to hear, heal those wounds and show them Your true face. Open the eyes of soldiers, students, rabbis, politicians and ordinary families, so that they read the Law and the Prophets and see You there. Amen.

### 12. The Man of sorrows who bore their griefs

> <sup>3</sup> He is despised and rejected of men; a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief: and we hid as it were our faces from him; he was despised, and we esteemed him not. <sup>4</sup> Surely he hath borne our griefs, and carried our sorrows: yet we did esteem him stricken, smitten of God, and afflicted.
>
> — **Isaiah 53:3-4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Isaiah foretold that You would be despised and rejected of men, a Man of sorrows and acquainted with grief, who has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows. Israel is a nation carrying deep grief after years of war, loss and fear. Let the Jewish people recognise in this ancient prophecy the One who was pierced for them. Show bereaved parents, wounded soldiers and anxious families that You understand their sorrow and carried it yourself. Let them stop esteeming You stricken and see You as their own Messiah and Lord. Amen.

### 13. The new covenant written on the heart

> <sup>33</sup> But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the LORD, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people. <sup>34</sup> And they shall teach no more every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the LORD: for they shall all know me, from the least of them unto the greatest of them, saith the LORD: for I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more.
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:33-34** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, through Jeremiah You promised a new covenant with the house of Israel: the law written on their hearts, iniquity forgiven and sin remembered no more. You sealed that covenant with Your own blood. I pray that the Jewish people would turn to You in repentance and receive this forgiveness. Write Your law on the hearts of young and old, secular and religious, in Israel and in the diaspora. Bring the day when they all know You, from the least to the greatest, and let many come to that knowledge in our own time. Amen.

### 14. Witnesses in Jerusalem, Judea and Samaria

> *But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth.*
>
> — **Acts 1:8** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised power by the Holy Spirit so that Your followers would be witnesses in Jerusalem, in all Judaea and in Samaria, and to the ends of the earth. I pray for believers who share the good news with Jewish people today, in Israel and around the world. Fill them with the Spirit, with humility and with love. Give them wisdom in a tense season, protect them from harm, and open doors for honest conversations. Let their lives commend the gospel. Raise up faithful witnesses in Uganda and across Africa who will pray for Israel and love the Jewish people. Amen.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus, one with the nations in Him

> *For he is our peace, who hath made both one, and hath broken down the middle wall of partition between us;*
>
> — **Ephesians 2:14** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are our peace, who made Jew and Gentile one and broke down the middle wall of partition between us. I pray for Jewish believers in You in Israel and the diaspora. Strengthen their congregations, protect them from hostility and isolation, and give them courage to follow You openly. Let them be a living sign to their own people that You are the Messiah of Israel. Make the church among the nations a true family to them, free of pride and contempt. Let Jewish and Arab believers in the land show the peace that only You can give. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I thank You that You are the Keeper of Israel and the Saviour of the world. Watch over the nation of Israel, Jews in the land and in every nation, and the people of Judea and Samaria. Give wisdom to leaders and comfort to the grieving. Fulfil the desire of Romans 10:1, that Israel might be saved, and let them see You as their Messiah and Lord. In Your name I pray. Amen.*
