## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Jerusalem

> *As birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem; defending also he will deliver it; and passing over he will preserve it.*
>
> — **Isaiah 31:5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that as birds flying, so the LORD of hosts will defend Jerusalem. I lift up the city and all who live, work and pray there. Guard its streets, its holy places and its families from every plan of violence. Give wisdom to those who keep order, and let Jerusalem be known for safety and peace. Defend her as You promised, and let her people know that You are near. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers on every front

> <sup>7</sup> The LORD shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul. <sup>8</sup> The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.
>
> — **Psalm 121:7-8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray the words that the LORD shall preserve from all evil, and preserve the going out and the coming in. Keep Israel's soldiers on the northern and southern borders and in the West Bank. Protect their bodies and their minds, give them clear judgment under pressure, and bring them home to their families. Strengthen the reservists and comfort those who have lost comrades. Amen.

### 8. Civilians in the shelter of Your hand

> <sup>27</sup> My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me: <sup>28</sup> And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. <sup>29</sup> My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.
>
> — **John 10:27-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that no one can pluck Your sheep out of Your hand. I pray for ordinary families in Israel who live with sirens, rockets and uncertainty. Hold the children, the elderly and the sick in Your care. Comfort the grieving and the wounded, and let those who know You rest in the promise of eternal life, and let those who do not yet know You hear Your voice. Amen.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora

> *But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil.*
>
> — **2 Thessalonians 3:3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are faithful, and You establish and keep us from evil. After attacks on synagogues and Jewish people in North America and Europe, I pray for Jewish communities everywhere. Protect worshippers, schoolchildren and families. Give police and governments the will to guard them, expose and restrain those who plot hatred, and make Your people safe in the cities where they live. Amen.

### 10. God's covenant faithfulness

> <sup>35</sup> Thus saith the LORD, which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar; The LORD of hosts is his name: <sup>36</sup> If those ordinances depart from before me, saith the LORD, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever.
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:35-36** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the One who gave the sun for light by day and the moon and stars for light by night, and You have promised that the seed of Israel shall not cease from being a nation before You. I thank You for Your covenant faithfulness. Preserve Israel and the Jewish people through every age of war and threat, and make Your promises known to them as true. Let them see that Your word never fails. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. A new heart and spirit

> <sup>26</sup> A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. <sup>27</sup> And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.
>
> — **Ezekiel 36:26-27** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to give a new heart and put Your spirit within Your people. I pray for Jewish people in Israel and across the world who feel tired, hurt or cynical. Soften what is hard, open hearts to You, and give each one a new spirit to walk in Your ways. Let Your Holy Spirit move among them in power and gentleness. Amen.

### 12. The suffering Messiah

> <sup>5</sup> But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed. <sup>6</sup> All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned every one to his own way; and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.
>
> — **Isaiah 53:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Isaiah wrote that the Servant was wounded for our transgressions and that the LORD laid on Him the iniquity of us all. Open the eyes of Jewish readers of this chapter to see You as the Messiah who bore our sins. Lead them to read, to ask, and to find in You the peace and healing they long for. Amen.

### 13. No other name

> *Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.*
>
> — **Acts 4:12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Peter proclaimed that there is salvation in no other name than Yours. I pray for Jewish people in Israel and in the diaspora to hear this with an open heart. Draw them to repentance, forgiveness and new life in You, and take away fear and pride that hold them back. Make Your name precious to them. Amen.

### 14. Confessing Jesus as Lord

> <sup>9</sup> That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. <sup>10</sup> For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.
>
> — **Romans 10:9-10** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Paul wrote that whoever confesses You with the mouth and believes in the heart that God raised You from the dead shall be saved. I pray for faithful witnesses, Jewish and Gentile, to speak and live the good news with love and without contempt. Give courage to those who hesitate, and bring many Jewish people to confess You as Lord and Messiah. Amen.

### 15. The righteous Branch

> <sup>5</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the LORD, that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth. <sup>6</sup> In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell safely: and this is his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS.
>
> — **Jeremiah 23:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Jeremiah promised a righteous Branch of David, a King who will reign and prosper, whose name is THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS. I pray for Jewish believers in You, in Israel and around the world: keep them strong, united and bold. Let their lives show You as the promised King, and let many see the Branch of David and be saved. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring before You Israel, the Jewish people in the land and in every nation, and the people of Judea and Samaria. Protect the vulnerable, comfort the grieving, give wisdom to leaders, and above all draw Your people to Yourself as Messiah and Lord. Hear these prayers, and let Your peace reign. Amen.*
