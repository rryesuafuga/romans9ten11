## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Leaders facing coalition decisions after the election

> <sup>6</sup> For promotion cometh neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south. <sup>7</sup> But God is the judge: he putteth down one, and setteth up another.
>
> — **Psalm 75:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the judge who puts down one and sets up another, and no vote count surprises You. The final results leave neither Netanyahu's bloc nor Eisenkot's with 61 seats, and the new Knesset is expected to be sworn in on 10 November. I ask You to give every leader humility, patience and care for the whole nation, Jewish and Arab alike. Steer coalition talks toward what is good and just, calm the anxious, and let the outcome serve peace and the safety of all who live there. Rule in these decisions, Prince of Peace. Amen.

### 2. Families displaced in Hebron

**In the news:** [Hebron displacement continues](https://jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-913326)

> <sup>35</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in: <sup>36</sup> Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me.
>
> — **Matthew 25:35-36** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that whoever shelters the stranger and visits the sick has done it to You. In Hebron more than three thousand people have been driven from their homes after two Israeli soldiers were killed, and reports say neighbourhoods have been evacuated ahead of demolitions. I ask You to comfort the families of the soldiers and the families who have lost their homes. Protect children, the sick and the elderly, move those in authority to restraint and mercy, and restrain anyone who plans violence on any side. Be a refuge to the frightened tonight. Amen.

### 3. The northern border and regional tension

**In the news:** [Israel continues strikes on Hezbollah targets in southern Lebanon](https://timesofisrael.com/hezbollah-rearming-for-possible-renewed-conflict-with-israel-lebanese-sources-say); [Israel reportedly holds multi-front security meeting](https://themedialine.org/headlines/israel-to-hold-multi-front-security-meeting-on-iran-gaza-lebanon-and-syria)

> <sup>7</sup> Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the LORD our God. <sup>8</sup> They are brought down and fallen: but we are risen, and stand upright.
>
> — **Psalm 20:7-8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, some trust in chariots and horses, but I will remember Your name. Israel faces tension on several fronts, with strikes continuing in southern Lebanon and fear of a wider escalation involving Iran. I ask You to guard soldiers and civilians on every side of these borders, and to give wisdom to those in the security meetings who must weigh each decision. Hold back those who plan war, open a way for patient talks, and keep ordinary families in Israel and Lebanon from harm. Let our trust rest in You, not in weapons. Amen.

### 4. Jewish communities in Montreal and Giessen

**In the news:** [Montreal synagogue windows hit by apparent pellet-gun fire](https://jns.org/news/world/montreal-synagogue-targeted-for-second-time-in-six-months); [Arson at a synagogue in Giessen, Germany](https://israelbehindthenews.com/2026/10/30/growing-pogrom-like-atmosphere-german-antisemitism-commissioner-issues-warning-after-synagogue-arson-attack)

> *In righteousness shalt thou be established: thou shalt be far from oppression; for thou shalt not fear: and from terror; for it shall not come near thee.*
>
> — **Isaiah 54:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promise a people established in righteousness, far from oppression and terror. Windows at a Montreal synagogue were struck by apparent pellet fire, and a synagogue in Giessen, Germany, was targeted by arson, with a commissioner warning of a growing climate of hostility. I ask You to comfort the congregations who felt afraid, to protect worshippers and children, and to help police find those responsible. Soften hearts that harbour hatred toward the Jewish people, and let these communities gather in peace without fear. Amen.

### 5. A Jewish researcher attacked in Rome

**In the news:** [Two men arrested over attack on Jewish researcher in Rome](https://algemeiner.com/2026/10/30/two-men-arrested-jewish-researcher-brutally-attacked-rome)

> <sup>19</sup> Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord. <sup>20</sup> Therefore if thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink: for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head. <sup>21</sup> Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.
>
> — **Romans 12:19-21** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You taught us not to be overcome by evil but to overcome evil with good. Two men have been arrested after a Jewish researcher was attacked in Rome after leaving a synagogue, and the local Jewish community is shaken. I ask You to heal the victim in body and mind, to strengthen their family and friends, and to let justice be done fairly. Turn those who hate toward repentance, and make Your people and their neighbours in Rome bold to answer violence with kindness and courage. Be near the one who was hurt. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The land and its borders

> *A land which the LORD thy God careth for: the eyes of the LORD thy God are always upon it, from the beginning of the year even unto the end of the year.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 11:12** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Lord who cares for the land, and Your eyes are upon it from the beginning of the year to its end. I ask You to watch over the whole land of Israel and its borders, over farmers and families, towns and villages. Keep the land from bloodshed and from fear, restrain those who would harm it, and give its people peace in their homes. Let the people who live there, Jewish and Arab, know that You see them and care for them. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians

> <sup>5</sup> Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day; <sup>6</sup> Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday. <sup>7</sup> A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee.
>
> — **Psalm 91:5-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are my refuge, and You promise that terror by night and the arrow by day need not prevail. I pray for Israeli soldiers at checkpoints and on the borders, and for civilians who sleep near sirens and shelters. Cover them with Your protection, keep them from ambush, from rockets and from violent hands, and guard their minds from fear. Comfort the wounded and the bereaved, and give soldiers wisdom to protect innocent life on every side. Be their shield. Amen.

### 8. Jerusalem

> <sup>1</sup> Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness. <sup>2</sup> Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, is mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King. <sup>3</sup> God is known in her palaces for a refuge.
>
> — **Psalm 48:1-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, great are You, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God. I pray for Jerusalem, for residents, pilgrims and visitors of every faith who walk its streets. Be known in her palaces and her homes as a refuge. Guard the city from violence and quiet its tensions, give her leaders wisdom, and let her be a place of safety for the elderly, the children and the worshippers. Come as King of Peace to the city You love. Amen.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora

> *These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.*
>
> — **John 16:33** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You told Your disciples that in the world we will have tribulation, but that in You we have peace, for You have overcome the world. I pray for Jewish families, students and congregations in every nation who feel exposed and uncertain. Give them peace in You, guard their homes, schools and places of worship, and raise up neighbours, police and leaders who stand with them. Let them be of good cheer, because You are greater than every threat. Amen.

### 10. God's covenant faithfulness

> <sup>28</sup> As concerning the gospel, they are enemies for your sakes: but as touching the election, they are beloved for the fathers’ sakes. <sup>29</sup> For the gifts and calling of God are without repentance.
>
> — **Romans 11:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are faithful, and the gifts and calling of God are without repentance. The Jewish people remain beloved for the fathers' sakes, and Your promises to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob do not fail. I thank You that Your covenant love does not depend on the circumstances of any year. Hold Israel and Jews everywhere in Your faithful care, give us humility toward them, and let the world see Your steadfast promises kept. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Opened eyes and hearts

> *And in that day shall the deaf hear the words of the book, and the eyes of the blind shall see out of obscurity, and out of darkness.*
>
> — **Isaiah 29:18** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised a day when the deaf would hear the words of the book and the blind would see out of darkness. I pray this for the Jewish people. Open eyes to see You in the Law and the Prophets, and open ears to hear Your voice. Remove every veil, and where there is fear or hurt that has closed hearts, bring tender light and welcome. Let many see You as their Messiah, with love and without pressure or contempt. Amen.

### 12. The Messiah foretold

> *Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.*
>
> — **Isaiah 7:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are Immanuel, God with us, the sign the prophet promised to the house of David. I ask that Jewish people reading Isaiah and the Psalms would see You there: born of a virgin, a son given, a servant who suffers, a King who reigns. Bring Your word to families and students who study the prophets, and let the Spirit show them that the promised One has come, and that He is You. Amen.

### 13. Repentance and forgiveness

> *If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.*
>
> — **1 John 1:9** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are faithful and just to forgive our sins and cleanse us from all unrighteousness. I pray for Jewish people who carry guilt or long for peace with God. Show them that Your sacrifice answers the need that every atonement pointed to. Give them the courage to confess, and the joy of being forgiven and made clean. Draw them gently, and let no one feel too far away to be forgiven. Amen.

### 14. Faithful witnesses

> *But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth.*
>
> — **Acts 1:8** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You sent Your followers as witnesses in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the uttermost part of the earth. Raise up Spirit-filled witnesses today in Israel, in Judea and Samaria and in every Jewish community abroad. Make us loving, honest and patient, listening before speaking. Give believers words and kindness that adorn Your gospel, protect them in hostile places, and give them open doors and trusted friendships. Amen.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus

> <sup>25</sup> For I would not, brethren, that ye should be ignorant of this mystery, lest ye should be wise in your own conceits; that blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the Gentiles be come in. <sup>26</sup> And so all Israel shall be saved: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob:
>
> — **Romans 11:25-26** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have revealed a mystery: that blindness in part has come upon Israel until the fullness of the Gentiles, and that all Israel shall be saved, as the Deliverer comes out of Zion. I pray for Jewish believers in You, in Israel and around the world, who are often isolated or pressured. Strengthen their faith, unite their congregations, and bring many more to You. Hasten the day this promise is fulfilled. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring all of this to You: the leaders weighing coalitions, the families displaced in Hebron, soldiers and civilians near the borders, and Jewish communities hurt by hatred in Montreal, Giessen and Rome. Protect Your people, comfort the grieving, restrain the violent, and bring many to know You as Messiah and Lord. I trust You with what I cannot see. Amen.*
