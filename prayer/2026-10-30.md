## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Wisdom for leaders after a deadlocked election

**In the news:** [Final count confirms no majority for either bloc](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/29/israel-election-result-shows-no-overall-majority-as-vote-count-complete)

> *A man’s heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps.*
>
> — **Proverbs 16:9** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You direct the steps of every person, and no count of votes surprises You. The completed tally leaves neither bloc in Israel with a majority, and weeks of negotiation or even another election may follow. I ask You to give every leader humility, patience and a care for the whole nation, Jewish and Arab alike. Where hearts are set on their own ways, steer them toward what is good and just. Calm the anxious, guard the institutions that keep the vote honest, and let the outcome serve peace and the safety of all who live there. You are the Prince of Peace; rule in these decisions. Amen.

### 2. The families displaced by the Hebron lockdown

**In the news:** [A week of lockdown in Hebron](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/29/a-week-of-israels-lockdown-in-hebron-whats-happened-when-will-it-end)

> *For the oppression of the poor, for the sighing of the needy, now will I arise, saith the LORD; I will set him in safety from him that puffeth at him.*
>
> — **Psalm 12:5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You see the sighing of the needy, and You have promised to arise and set them in safety. A week of lockdown in Hebron has left about three thousand people displaced, after two Israeli soldiers were killed near the city. I ask You to comfort the grieving families of the soldiers and the families driven from their homes. Protect children, the sick and the elderly, and move those in authority to act with restraint and mercy. Restrain anyone who plans violence on either side, and bring a safety that is real for every innocent person. Be near all who are afraid tonight, and show Yourself to them as a refuge. Amen.

### 3. Peace in the raids across Judea and Samaria

**In the news:** [Palestinians injured and arrested in raids across the West Bank](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/29/palestinians-injured-arrested-in-israeli-raids-across-west-bank)

> *If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.*
>
> — **Romans 12:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You call Your people to live peaceably with all, as far as it depends on them. Across Judea and Samaria raids continue, with injuries and arrests reported. I pray for soldiers, who face real danger at every checkpoint, and for the Palestinian families living under the pressure of these operations. Guard the wounded, and bring healing to the injured. Give commanders wisdom to protect life and to act justly. Give neighbours of every background the courage to choose peace over revenge. Lord Jesus, make a way for calm in these towns and villages, and let Your peace be known where there is fear. Amen.

### 4. Orthodox Jews afraid of immigration enforcement

**In the news:** [NJ Assembly member says Orthodox Jews were detained by ICE after voting as non-citizens](https://jta.org/2026/10/29/ny/orthodox-jews-were-detained-by-ice-after-voting-as-non-citizens-says-nj-pol)

> *Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.*
>
> — **1 Peter 5:7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You invite us to cast all our care on You because You care for us. A New Jersey leader reports that some Orthodox Jews who are not citizens were detained after voting, and many in that community are anxious. I ask You to give them calm and clear information about their rights. Surround any detained person with Your presence, and bring them swift and fair treatment. Give wisdom to officials who enforce the law, and fairness in how it is applied to every community. Guard the families who now fear for their futures, and let their trust in You steady them. Amen.

### 5. Unity between Jews in Israel and abroad

**In the news:** [U.S. and Israeli Jews drawing closer, survey finds](https://jta.org/2026/10/26/ideas/u-s-and-israeli-jews-are-drifting-apart-the-data-tell-a-different-story)

> *Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!*
>
> — **Psalm 133:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You for the good news of a study that finds Jews in America and Israel feeling closer in a shared identity. Many disagree sharply, and recent weeks have brought tension over travel permissions for visiting rabbis. I ask You to bless every effort that brings brothers and sisters together, and to heal the divisions between those who hold different views. Teach leaders in Israel and in the diaspora to listen, to speak kindly and to honour one another. Let the unity that is good and pleasant grow, and let it prepare hearts to hear of You, the Messiah of Israel. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Jerusalem and the land guarded by the Lord

> *For I, saith the LORD, will be unto her a wall of fire round about, and will be the glory in the midst of her.*
>
> — **Zechariah 2:5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to be a wall of fire around Jerusalem and the glory in her midst. I ask You to guard the city, its borders and every town and village of the land of Israel. Turn aside every plan of violence, shield families in their homes and travellers on their roads, and let Your glory be known there. Give those who watch and defend the nation wisdom and alertness, and keep them from harm. Let Jerusalem be a place of safety, worship and peace, held securely by Your hand and not by human strength alone. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians facing war

> <sup>1</sup> The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? <sup>2</sup> When the wicked, even mine enemies and my foes, came upon me to eat up my flesh, they stumbled and fell. <sup>3</sup> Though an host should encamp against me, my heart shall not fear: though war should rise against me, in this will I be confident.
>
> — **Psalm 27:1-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are light and salvation, and with You there is no reason to be afraid. I pray for Israeli soldiers in Gaza, on the Lebanese border and across the north and south, and for the civilians who live under sirens and threats from Iran and its allies. Cover them in the day of battle, give them courage that does not harden into cruelty, and guard them from harm. Comfort every family waiting for news. Restrain those who plan evil, and bring a just and lasting peace that spares innocent lives on every side. Amen.

### 8. Jews everywhere under threat

> <sup>35</sup> Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword? <sup>36</sup> As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter. <sup>37</sup> Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.
>
> — **Romans 8:35-37** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, nothing can separate Your people from Your love, not trouble, danger or the sword. I pray for Jewish communities in every nation, who face hostility, threats and attacks, including the recent assault at the Israeli embassy in Belgrade. Protect synagogues, schools and homes, and give police and governments the will to guard them. Give the fearful a calm confidence that You are with them. Raise up neighbours who will stand beside Jewish friends. Let Your love be more certain to them than any fear, and make them more than conquerors in You. Amen.

### 9. God's unchanging promise to Israel

> <sup>17</sup> Wherein God, willing more abundantly to shew unto the heirs of promise the immutability of his counsel, confirmed it by an oath: <sup>18</sup> That by two immutable things, in which it was impossible for God to lie, we might have a strong consolation, who have fled for refuge to lay hold upon the hope set before us:
>
> — **Hebrews 6:17-18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You confirmed Your promise with an oath, and it is impossible for God to lie. I thank You that Your purposes for Israel and for Abraham's children stand firm, whatever the nations may say or do. Give strong consolation to every Jewish family that feels tired, scattered or alone, and let them find refuge in Your faithfulness. Remind Your people that Your word does not fail. Help me to pray with hope for the land, its people and Your plan, and to trust that the One who promised will do it. Amen.

### 10. A stronghold in the day of trouble

> *The LORD is good, a strong hold in the day of trouble; and he knoweth them that trust in him.*
>
> — **Nahum 1:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are good, a stronghold in the day of trouble, and You know those who trust in You. I bring before You every Israeli and every Jew who is in trouble today: the hostage families still waiting, the wounded, the bereaved and the frightened. Be their strong hold when their strength is gone. Draw near to those who feel forgotten, and make Yourself known to them as faithful and kind. Guard them in the night, comfort them in the morning, and lead them through this hard season toward the peace that only You can give. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Open eyes to see wonders

> *Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law.*
>
> — **Psalm 119:18** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask that You open the eyes of Jewish people everywhere to see the wonders in the Law and the Prophets, and to see You in them. Lift every veil of misunderstanding or hurt that has kept hearts from You. Bring a hunger for Scripture to the young and the old, the secular and the devout, in Israel and in every nation. Let those who read the words of Moses and the Prophets meet You as the One they point to. Do this in Your mercy, and let it be done with love. Amen.

### 12. The Messiah promised from Bethlehem

> *But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.*
>
> — **Micah 5:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You were born in Bethlehem as the ruler foretold from long ago, whose goings forth are from everlasting. I ask that Jewish people may see that the promise of Micah has been kept in You. Bring this passage to the attention of those who study it, and let its words reveal You to them. Give believers gentle and clear words to explain how the prophets point to You. Let the Shepherd of Israel gather His people, and let many in this generation call You Messiah and Lord. Amen.

### 13. Repentance and times of refreshing

> <sup>19</sup> Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord; <sup>20</sup> And he shall send Jesus Christ, which before was preached unto you:
>
> — **Acts 3:19-20** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that Peter called his own people to repent and be converted so that their sins might be blotted out. I pray the same invitation would reach Jewish hearts today, with the same gentleness. Give the gift of repentance, and let forgiveness come to many in Israel and the nations through Your name. Send the times of refreshing from Your presence on families, communities and whole towns. Let those who are weary of war and grief find rest in You, the Jesus whom God sent. Amen.

### 14. Faithful messengers of the gospel

> *And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!*
>
> — **Romans 10:15** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You send out those who bring good news of peace. I ask You to raise up and protect messengers to the Jewish people, in Israel and in every country, who speak with love and not contempt. Give them wisdom, courage and gentleness, and open doors for the gospel in homes, universities, army bases and synagogues. Let their feet be beautiful in the lives of those who hear. Guard them from discouragement and danger, and let the word they bring bear lasting fruit for Your glory. Amen.

### 15. The remnant of grace

> *Even so then at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace.*
>
> — **Romans 11:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, there is a remnant in Israel according to the election of grace, and I thank You for every Jewish person who follows You. Strengthen Jewish believers in Israel and the diaspora who face misunderstanding, pressure or loss. Give them joy, boldness and good fellowship, and protect their families. Let their lives and words draw others toward You. Grow that remnant until many more in Israel confess You as Messiah, and let Your grace be seen in every congregation. I ask this with a love that wants only their good. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I commit Israel, the Jewish people everywhere, and the people of Judea and Samaria into Your hands. Protect the innocent, comfort the grieving, give wisdom to those who lead, and bring peace where there is fear. Above all, bring the Jewish people to know You as their Messiah and Lord, for my heart's desire and prayer is that they might be saved. Hear these prayers in Your mercy, and let Your name be glorified. Amen.*
