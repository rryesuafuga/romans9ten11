## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Wisdom for Israel's voters as the election nears

**In the news:** [Voting at Israel's missions abroad ends with higher turnout](https://timesofisrael.com/israeli-emissaries-abroad-end-voting-with-turnout-17-percentage-points-higher-than-2022); [Israel election poll: Eisenkot leads Netanyahu, but no clear path to a majority](https://jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-911622)

> *If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.*
>
> — **James 1:5** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, nine days remain before Israel votes on 27 October, and polls show neither side near a majority. I ask You, as this word says, to give wisdom liberally to every voter, candidate and leader who lacks it. Thank You that voting abroad has already drawn many people to take part. Let no one be moved by fear or by anger, but guide each decision toward what is good for all who live in the land. Give clarity, honest campaigns and a peaceful count, and let those who govern next seek You first. You do not scold the one who asks, so I ask boldly for Your wisdom to rest on Israel now. Amen.

### 2. Calm and watchfulness while the army warns of escalation

**In the news:** [IDF chief warns of 'potential for escalation' across all fronts, decries settler violence](https://timesofisrael.com/idf-chief-warns-of-potential-for-escalation-across-all-fronts-decries-settler-violence)

> <sup>18</sup> Behold, the eye of the LORD is upon them that fear him, upon them that hope in his mercy; <sup>19</sup> To deliver their soul from death, and to keep them alive in famine. <sup>20</sup> Our soul waiteth for the LORD: he is our help and our shield.
>
> — **Psalm 33:18-20** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the IDF chief has warned of possible escalation on every front, and families along the borders live with that weight. Your eye is upon those who hope in Your mercy, so I ask You to watch over soldiers, reservists and civilians in the north and south. Deliver them from death, keep them alive, and be their help and their shield. Give commanders sound judgment and restrain every hand that plans violence. Lord, our souls wait for You; let Your peace be greater than the threat, and keep the border communities steady in trust. Amen.

### 3. Justice and protection for the vulnerable in Judea and Samaria

**In the news:** [Masked settlers allegedly attack European diplomats and Palestinians near Ramallah](https://jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-911973)

> *Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.*
>
> — **Isaiah 1:17** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the villagers and olive pickers near Ramallah, and for the diplomats who were reportedly attacked in Taybeh. Your word tells us to seek justice and relieve the oppressed. I ask that the police investigation be thorough and fair, that facts come to light, and that those responsible be held to account whoever they are. Protect Palestinian and Jewish families in the land from violence, and comfort the injured. Give the leaders of Israel courage to uphold the law, and let truth, not slogans, shape what follows. Amen.

### 4. Overcoming evil with good in a divided public debate

**In the news:** [Netanyahu's office urges waiting for facts on the attack on diplomats](https://timesofisrael.com/amid-outrage-netanyahu-claims-no-compelling-evidence-settlers-attacked-european-diplomats)

> *Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.*
>
> — **Romans 12:21** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the reports of the attack on the convoy have stirred outrage, and leaders are trading charges, with the prime minister's office urging patience and others using harsh words. Teach us not to be overcome by evil but to overcome it with good. Guard Israelis and those who live beside them from bitterness and revenge. Give officials honesty in what they say, and give ordinary people the grace to condemn violence without hating whole communities. Let Your peace and humility settle in a tense season, and let Your goodness win. Amen.

### 5. Jews abroad and the bond between Israel and the diaspora

**In the news:** [Bennett and Lapid pledge to strengthen Israel-diaspora ties](https://forward.com/fast-forward/855722/bennett-lapid-israel-diaspora-ties-election); [Non-Zionist Jewish institutions grow in the US](https://jta.org/2026/10/14/ny/they-want-jewish-life-without-zionism-now-theyre-building-the-institutions-to-match)

> *And he shall set up an ensign for the nations, and shall assemble the outcasts of Israel, and gather together the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.*
>
> — **Isaiah 11:12** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for Jewish communities around the world as parties in Israel speak of strengthening ties with them. You promised to gather the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth. Be with Jewish families in every country, in their synagogues, schools and debates about identity and Zion. Keep them safe, and give them honest friends. Grant that whoever forms the next government would treat the diaspora with respect and care, and let Your purposes for Your people go forward. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The Lord who is light and strength

> *The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?*
>
> — **Psalm 27:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are my light and my salvation, so whom shall I fear? I pray for every Israeli who walks to school, work or the shelter with fear in the heart. Be the strength of their life. Light the way for soldiers on night patrols and for families awake with worry. Let Your presence quiet what frightens them, and teach me to rest in You as well. Keep Israel, Your land and people, under Your watchful care today. Amen.

### 7. The peace of Jerusalem

> <sup>6</sup> Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee. <sup>7</sup> Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces.
>
> — **Psalm 122:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I obey Your word and pray for the peace of Jerusalem. Let peace be within her walls and prosperity within her palaces. Guard the city's residents, Jews, Arabs and visitors of every faith, from knives, rockets and rage. Protect the police, the paramedics and the pilgrims. Prince of Peace, make Jerusalem a city of truth, and let those who love her prosper in Your mercy. Amen.

### 8. The Lord rejoicing over His people

> *The LORD thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing.*
>
> — **Zephaniah 3:17** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that the LORD in the midst of Israel is mighty and will save. Let the people hear You rejoicing over them with singing, even in a time of war and elections. Quiet the anxious and strengthen the weary, the widowed and the bereaved. Rest Your love upon the grieving and show them they are not forgotten. Be mighty for Israel and let her know Your joy. Amen.

### 9. Love nothing can sever

> <sup>38</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, <sup>39</sup> Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
>
> — **Romans 8:38-39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for Jews in every nation who feel hated or unsafe. Let them know that no power, no threat and nothing in the present or the future can separate them from the love of God that is in You. Protect them from violence and from slander. Give courage to parents, rabbis and teachers, and strengthen the bonds of their communities. Hold them in Your love that never lets go. Amen.

### 10. The blessing of the Lord upon His people

> *And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.*
>
> — **1 Thessalonians 5:23** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask the God of peace to sanctify and keep Israel, and to preserve the whole spirit, soul and body of Your people blameless until Your coming. Make Your face shine upon the land, be gracious to every family, and give them peace. Remember Your covenant faithfulness to the descendants of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Watch over every soldier, child and elder, and keep them safe. Give them peace in the land and in their hearts. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Light rising upon Israel

> <sup>1</sup> Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee. <sup>2</sup> For, behold, the darkness shall cover the earth, and gross darkness the people: but the LORD shall arise upon thee, and his glory shall be seen upon thee.
>
> — **Isaiah 60:1-2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the light of the world. Where darkness covers the earth, let Your glory rise upon Israel, and let the Jewish people see You as their Messiah. I pray that light would reach into homes in Tel Aviv, Jerusalem and the diaspora. Open eyes that have been closed for centuries, and draw hearts toward You with love. Shine, Lord, and let Your people arise in Your light. Amen.

### 12. Good news for the brokenhearted

> <sup>18</sup> The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, <sup>19</sup> To preach the acceptable year of the Lord.
>
> — **Luke 4:18-19** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You were anointed to preach the gospel, to heal the brokenhearted and to set the captives free. I pray for Jewish people who carry grief from war, loss and a long history of suffering. Come to them with Your healing, and set them free from fear and sorrow. Let them hear that You came to bind up the wounded, and let them know You as Messiah and Lord. Amen.

### 13. The gospel to the Jew first

> *For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.*
>
> — **Romans 1:16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God to salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first. Give Jewish people the boldness to listen and the faith to believe. Raise up faithful witnesses who speak with gentleness and love, never with contempt. Let Your power work in hearts across Israel and the nations, and let many come to know You as Messiah. Amen.

### 14. Repentance and the gift of the Spirit

> *Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.*
>
> — **Acts 2:38** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, on that day Peter called Jerusalem to repent and be baptized in Your name for the forgiveness of sins. I pray that the same Spirit would move among Jewish people today. Give them a sorrow for sin that leads to life, and the assurance of forgiveness through You. Pour out Your Spirit on Jewish believers who follow You, and strengthen their witness in their families and communities. Amen.

### 15. Beautiful feet bringing good tidings

> *How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, that publisheth peace; that bringeth good tidings of good, that publisheth salvation; that saith unto Zion, Thy God reigneth!*
>
> — **Isaiah 52:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, how beautiful are the feet of those who bring good tidings and publish salvation, and who say to Zion, Your God reigns. Send messengers to Israel and to the diaspora, speaking in love and in their own languages. Protect Jewish believers who share Your name, and give them joy under pressure. Let many in Zion hear that You reign, and trust in You. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I commit Israel, the Jewish people and the people of Judea and Samaria into Your hands. Protect the innocent, give wisdom to leaders, and comfort those who mourn. Draw Your people Israel to know You as Messiah and Lord, with the love of Romans 10:1. Hear these prayers for Your name's sake. Amen.*
