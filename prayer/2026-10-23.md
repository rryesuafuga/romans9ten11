## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Jerusalem and its people surrounded by the Lord

> *As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.*
>
> — **Psalm 125:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, as the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so You are round about Your people from now and for ever. I ask You to surround Jerusalem and every town and village in Israel with Your protection tonight. Guard the streets, the schools, the markets and the homes. Give the city peace within its walls and rest to those who cannot sleep from worry. Hold back every plan of violence, and let Your presence be a wall that no weapon can pass. Keep Your people in Your sight, Lord, and let all who live in Jerusalem, of every faith, know safety under Your care. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and their families

> *Be strong and of a good courage, fear not, nor be afraid of them: for the LORD thy God, he it is that doth go with thee; he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 31:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the soldiers of Israel, for reservists called away from their families, and for the parents and spouses who wait at home. Be strong in them and give them good courage, for You go with them and You will not fail them nor forsake them. Guard them from harm and from the weight of what they have seen. Give their commanders wisdom and restraint, and keep them from needless loss of life on every side. Comfort the wounded and the grieving, and hold each family together in Your peace. Let no one feel forgotten by You, Lord, and bring them home safely. Amen.

### 8. God's faithfulness to His covenant with Israel

> <sup>35</sup> Thus saith the LORD, which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar; The LORD of hosts is his name: <sup>36</sup> If those ordinances depart from before me, saith the LORD, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever.
>
> — **Jeremiah 31:35-36** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the LORD of hosts who gave the sun for light by day and the moon and the stars by night, and who promised that the seed of Israel will not cease from being a nation before You. I thank You that Your word does not change with the polls, the elections or the wars. Keep Your covenant faithfulness before Israel's eyes in these uncertain days. Remind Your people that their preservation rests on Your promise and not on their strength. Give me faith to stand with them in prayer, and let the nations see that You are faithful, Lord. Amen.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora, that their hearts would not be troubled

> *Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me.*
>
> — **John 14:1** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said, Let not your heart be troubled; ye believe in God, believe also in me. I bring You Jewish families in every country who feel uneasy walking to a synagogue, wearing a kippah, or speaking about Israel. Quiet their hearts, Lord, and give them courage without bitterness. Raise up friends, neighbors and officials who will stand beside them. Let the strain between communities be eased by understanding, and let every threat be stopped before it harms anyone. Draw them to Yourself as their true peace and their safe place, wherever they live in the world. Amen.

### 10. Hope and peace for a people long weary of war

> *Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost.*
>
> — **Romans 15:13** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the God of hope is the One I ask to fill Israel with joy and peace in believing. So many have lived through war, rocket fire, funerals and fear, and their strength is tired. Pour out Your Holy Spirit on the weary, the anxious and the bereaved, so that they abound in hope. Let children grow up without the sound of sirens. Let the elderly and the sick find rest. Heal minds marked by trauma, and restore the joy that violence has stolen. Be the hope that does not disappoint, Lord, and let that hope be seen across the whole land. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. A fountain opened for cleansing from sin

> *In that day there shall be a fountain opened to the house of David and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem for sin and for uncleanness.*
>
> — **Zechariah 13:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised through Zechariah that a fountain would be opened to the house of David and to the people of Jerusalem for sin and for uncleanness. I ask You to open that fountain to Jewish people today, and to let them see in You the cleansing they need. Show them, in their own homes and quiet hours, that You are the Messiah who forgives. Soften every heart weighed down by guilt or religious weariness, and wash it clean in Your grace. Give me love for my Jewish neighbors rather than judgment, and keep me praying until the fountain flows. Amen.

### 12. Look to Him and be saved

> <sup>22</sup> Look unto me, and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth: for I am God, and there is none else. <sup>23</sup> I have sworn by myself, the word is gone out of my mouth in righteousness, and shall not return, That unto me every knee shall bow, every tongue shall swear.
>
> — **Isaiah 45:22-23** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the LORD who says, Look unto me, and be ye saved, for I am God and there is none else. I pray that many in Israel and among the Jewish people everywhere would turn their eyes to You. Draw secular Jews, religious Jews, the young and the old, the grieving and the proud. Let them lift their gaze from fear and from the news to see You, and let each find forgiveness and peace in You alone. I ask for open eyes where eyes have been closed. Let the day come when every knee bows and every tongue confesses You as Lord. Amen.

### 13. The Way, the Truth and the Life

> *Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.*
>
> — **John 14:6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me. I pray that Jewish people who long to know God would find the Father through You. Give them a hunger for truth that cannot be satisfied by anything less than You. Lead the seeker, the doubter and the skeptic to read the Scriptures with fresh eyes and to meet You in them. Bring believers across their path who live gently and answer honestly. Let none who sincerely seek You walk away without a clear invitation to follow You. Amen.

### 14. The Deliverer who comes out of Zion

> <sup>26</sup> And so all Israel shall be saved: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob: <sup>27</sup> For this is my covenant unto them, when I shall take away their sins.
>
> — **Romans 11:26-27** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says that all Israel shall be saved, that the Deliverer shall come out of Zion and turn away ungodliness from Jacob, and that this is Your covenant when You take away their sins. I hold on to that promise tonight and pray toward its fulfilment. Let Your Spirit move among the Jewish people with power and tenderness. Remove ungodliness, forgive sins, and bring families, towns and whole communities to trust You. Give me patience and confidence while I wait, and use my prayers as You see fit. You keep Your promises, Lord, and I thank You in advance. Amen.

### 15. Welcoming Him who comes in the name of the Lord

> *For I say unto you, Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.*
>
> — **Matthew 23:39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You told Jerusalem that they would not see You until they say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord. I pray for that day, and for every Jewish person who says it from the heart now. Strengthen Jewish believers in Israel and around the world, especially those who face loneliness, family pressure or fear for their jobs and homes. Give them joy in You and wisdom in how they speak. Let the welcome that Jerusalem will one day give You begin in many hearts today. Come, Lord Jesus, and be blessed among Your own people. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring You Israel, the Jews in the land and in every nation, and Judea and Samaria. Protect the weak, comfort the grieving, restrain those who plan harm, and guide the leaders who will choose in days. Above all, reveal Yourself as Messiah and Lord to the people who first received Your promises. Hear these prayers for Your name's sake. Amen.*
