## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Jerusalem and its peace

> <sup>6</sup> Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee. <sup>7</sup> Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces.
>
> — **Psalm 122:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You tell us to pray for the peace of Jerusalem, and so I do. Let peace be within her walls and prosperity in her palaces. Guard her streets, her markets, her light rail, her holy places and the families who go out in the morning. Quiet the voices of hatred, bless those who love the city, and rule over her, Prince of Peace, until she is called a city of righteousness. Amen.

### 7. Jacob saved and at rest

> <sup>10</sup> Therefore fear thou not, O my servant Jacob, saith the LORD; neither be dismayed, O Israel: for, lo, I will save thee from afar, and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and shall be in rest, and be quiet, and none shall make him afraid. <sup>11</sup> For I am with thee, saith the LORD, to save thee: though I make a full end of all nations whither I have scattered thee, yet will I not make a full end of thee: but I will correct thee in measure, and will not leave thee altogether unpunished.
>
> — **Jeremiah 30:10-11** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised Jacob that he need not fear, for You are with him to save. I pray for the people of Israel in the land and scattered in the nations: be with them, rescue them from every danger, and let them live in rest and quiet with no one making them afraid. Be faithful to the promises You have made, and give Your people courage for the days ahead. Amen.

>
> — **Psalm 18:2-3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a rock, a fortress and a deliverer. I lift up the soldiers and reservists on the borders, at the checkpoints and in Gaza, Lebanon and Hebron, and the civilians within the range of rockets and gunmen. Be their high tower. Save them from their enemies, shield them from the missile, the knife and the ambush, and bring them home safe. Give their commanders clear judgment and moral courage. Amen.

> *And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.*
>
> — **John 17:11** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You prayed that the Father would keep Your own through His name. I ask the same for Jewish families in Israel and across the world: keep them through Your name, in danger, in sorrow and in the pressure of those who hate them. Hold them together in unity, strengthen communities that are afraid, and let Your peace guard their homes, schools and synagogues. Amen.

I trust Your faithfulness for Israel, and I rest in it today. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Light in the face of Jesus

> *For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.*
>
> — **2 Corinthians 4:6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the God who commanded light to shine out of darkness. Shine now in the hearts of Jewish people in Israel and in every nation, and give them the knowledge of the glory of God in Your face. Open eyes that have not yet seen You as Messiah. Let them look at You and live, and draw them with the same love that drew Paul. Amen.

### 12. The light to the nations

> *And he said, It is a light thing that thou shouldest be my servant to raise up the tribes of Jacob, and to restore the preserved of Israel: I will also give thee for a light to the Gentiles, that thou mayest be my salvation unto the end of the earth.*
>
> — **Isaiah 49:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the prophet foretold a Servant who would raise up the tribes of Jacob and restore the preserved of Israel, and be a light to the Gentiles. I thank You that You are that Servant and Messiah. Bring Israel's children to see that You are the promise, and let Your salvation reach from Jerusalem to the ends of the earth. Amen.

### 13. Repentance and forgiveness

> <sup>36</sup> Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ. <sup>37</sup> Now when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles, Men and brethren, what shall we do? <sup>38</sup> Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.
>
> — **Acts 2:36-38** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Peter said that all the house of Israel should know that God has made You both Lord and Christ. Let that word cut the hearts of my Jewish friends and neighbours, as it did on that day, until they ask what they must do. Grant repentance, forgiveness of sins and the gift of the Holy Spirit. Make Your mercy the welcome of every person who turns to You. Amen.

### 14. Faithful witnesses

> *How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher?*
>
> — **Romans 10:14** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, how shall they call on You if they have not heard? Send gentle, faithful witnesses to Jewish people in Israel and in the diaspora: believers who speak with love, honesty and respect. Give them courage and open doors, protect them from fear and from bitterness, and let no one share You with contempt. Give the hearer ears to hear. Amen.

### 15. Calling on the name of the LORD

> *And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered: for in mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, as the LORD hath said, and in the remnant whom the LORD shall call.*
>
> — **Joel 2:32** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that whoever calls on the name of the LORD shall be delivered, and that deliverance is in Zion and Jerusalem and in the remnant You call. Call Your people, Lord. Strengthen Jewish believers in Jesus in Israel and abroad, and keep them in the face of pressure, loneliness and opposition. Gather more to call on You, and let them know You as Messiah and Lord. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I have laid before You the election, the families of Hebron, the grieving, the innocent in Gaza and Your people everywhere. Protect Israel and the Jewish people, bring peace to Jerusalem, and open the eyes of Your people to know You as Messiah and Lord. Hear me for Your mercy's sake. Amen.*
