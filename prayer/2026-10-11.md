## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Jerusalem and the watchmen on her walls

> <sup>6</sup> I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night: ye that make mention of the LORD, keep not silence, <sup>7</sup> And give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth.
>
> — **Isaiah 62:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have set watchmen on the walls of Jerusalem, and You call Your people to give You no rest until You make her a praise in the earth. I pray for Jerusalem's residents, for the police, soldiers and guards who watch day and night, and for every family who walks her streets. Keep terror from her gates, keep her holy places safe for all who worship, and let peace settle in her. Strengthen those who stand watch, and teach the church to pray faithfully for her, until Your promise for the city is fulfilled. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians under threat on every border

> *The LORD also shall roar out of Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heavens and the earth shall shake: but the LORD will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel.*
>
> — **Joel 3:16** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the hope of Your people and the strength of the children of Israel. I pray for the soldiers on the northern border, on the Gaza line and in the West Bank, and for the civilians in range of rockets, drones and knives. Be their refuge in the day of trouble. Shield them from attacks, give them clear judgment under pressure, and bring every one safe home to those who wait. Let Your voice be heard from Zion and be stronger than every threat spoken against Your people. Amen.

I pray for Jewish families in London, Manchester, Toronto, New York, Sydney and everywhere else, as the National Security Council warns of risks around this anniversary. Guard every synagogue, school and community centre. Protect men, women and children who wear a kippah or a star of David on the street, and expose plots before they are carried out. Be near each lonely family and bring Your people home as You have promised. Amen.

### 9. God's covenant mercy toward Israel

> <sup>54</sup> He hath holpen his servant Israel, in remembrance of his mercy; <sup>55</sup> As he spake to our fathers, to Abraham, and to his seed for ever.
>
> — **Luke 1:54-55** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have helped Your servant Israel in remembrance of Your mercy, as You spoke to Abraham and his seed for ever. I thank You that Your faithfulness to Israel does not rest on her strength, her politics or her merit but on Your word. When the nation is anxious about an election, a war and a hostile world, remind her that You keep Your promises. Preserve the Jewish people through every generation, and let Your mercy rest on Israel and on all who turn to You. Amen.

>
> — **2 Timothy 4:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You deliver Your people from every evil work and preserve them for Your heavenly kingdom. I ask this for Israel and for Jews everywhere: deliver them from terrorist plots, from lies and slander, from fear and despair, and from every scheme meant to harm them. Deliver leaders from corruption and hasty decisions, deliver soldiers from error, and deliver ordinary families from losing hope. Keep the nation faithful to You, and let Your glory be seen over her for ever and ever. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The sign of Immanuel foretold

> *Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.*
>
> — **Isaiah 7:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your own prophet Isaiah told the house of David that a virgin would conceive and bear a son and call His name Immanuel, God with us. I pray that Jewish people who read this promise in their own Scriptures would see that You are the One who was promised. Open the eyes of those who study Isaiah in synagogues, in universities and at home. Draw Your people by Your Word and Your Spirit, so that they know You are with them and that You are their Messiah. Amen.

### 12. The ruler born in Bethlehem

> *But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.*
>
> — **Micah 5:2** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the One born in Bethlehem Ephratah, whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting. Many Jewish readers know Micah's words. I ask that they would recognise You as the ruler promised to Israel, the Shepherd and King. Give Jewish believers courage to share what they know, and give those who are searching a quiet certainty that You are real. Prepare hearts across Israel and the diaspora to receive You as the Messiah and Lord. Amen.

### 13. He came to His own

> <sup>11</sup> He came unto his own, and his own received him not. <sup>12</sup> But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:
>
> — **John 1:11-12** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You came to Your own, and Your own received You not, yet to as many as received You You gave power to become the sons of God. I grieve over the unbelief of many in Israel and I do not blame them. I ask that You would give each Jewish person who hears of You the freedom to receive You and believe on Your name. Remove fear of losing family and community, and welcome every one who comes into Your household as a beloved son or daughter. Amen.

### 14. Great heaviness and continual sorrow for Israel

> <sup>1</sup> I say the truth in Christ, I lie not, my conscience also bearing me witness in the Holy Ghost, <sup>2</sup> That I have great heaviness and continual sorrow in my heart. <sup>3</sup> For I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh:
>
> — **Romans 9:1-3** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, like Paul, I carry heaviness and continual sorrow for my brothers and sisters of Israel. Give me and Your whole church the same love, free of pride and contempt. Let our prayers for Jewish people come from a heart that would give anything for them to know You. Make us faithful friends to Jewish neighbours, and let nothing we say or do ever add to their pain. Bring many in Israel to You, and may I never stop praying until they do. Amen.

### 15. Repentance, refreshing and the times of restitution

> <sup>19</sup> Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord; <sup>20</sup> And he shall send Jesus Christ, which before was preached unto you:
>
> — **Acts 3:19-20** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Peter told the men of Israel to repent and be converted so that their sins might be blotted out and times of refreshing might come from Your presence. I pray that same call would reach Jewish hearts today. Give them godly sorrow, forgiveness and the refreshing of Your Spirit. Send faithful witnesses to Israel and to the Jewish communities of the nations. Let the One who was preached before come to be known as their Messiah and Lord, and may refreshing come to all Your people. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I place Israel, the Jews of the diaspora and the people of Judea and Samaria in Your hands. Comfort the grieving, protect the innocent, give wisdom to those who lead, and restrain every hand that plots violence. Draw the Jewish people to Yourself as Messiah and Lord, and bring Your peace to Jerusalem and to all who live in this troubled region. For Yours is the kingdom, the power and the glory, for ever. Amen.*
