## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–8)

### 6. God as a refuge for Israel in trouble

> *God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.*
>
> — **Psalm 46:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the refuge and strength of Your people and a very present help in trouble. I ask You to be that for the nation of Israel today, in every city and every border community, and for every soldier, medic and family that is under pressure. Be a wall of protection against rockets, drones, infiltrators and those who plan terror. Give leaders and the army wisdom, and keep innocent people on every side safe. I ask You to hold Israel in the hour of danger and to make Yourself known as the one who helps. Lord Jesus, be Israel's shelter. Amen.

### 7. If God be for us, who can be against us

> <sup>31</sup> What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us? <sup>32</sup> He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?
>
> — **Romans 8:31-32** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are for Your people, and nothing can finally stand against what You have promised. I pray this over Jewish families in Israel and across the world who have lately been afraid at synagogue, on campus and in the street. Let them hear that God is for them. I ask You to protect Jewish communities in Britain, Canada, Australia, the United States, Africa and everywhere from hatred, vandalism and violence. Move police, neighbours and governments to stand with them. Because the Father gave His own Son for us, I ask boldly for these needs, believing You will freely give what is good. Amen.

### 8. No one can pluck Your own out of Your hand

> <sup>28</sup> And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. <sup>29</sup> My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.
>
> — **John 10:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that those who are Yours shall never perish and that no one can pluck them out of Your hand. I pray for Jewish believers in You in Israel and around the world, and for all who feel hunted or alone. Hold them fast. Keep Your hand over Jews in every nation, and over those who grieve, who are threatened, or who are displaced. Let Your Father's strength be greater than every enemy and every fear. Keep Your people safe and give them the confidence that they are held. Thank You that Your grip is stronger than ours. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people (points 9–10)

### 9. A new heart for Israel

> *A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh.*
>
> — **Ezekiel 36:26** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised through Ezekiel a new heart and a new spirit for Israel, taking away the heart of stone and giving a heart of flesh. I long for that to be fulfilled in my Jewish friends and neighbours, in Israel and across the world. Soften hearts that grief, anger or old wounds have hardened. Give those who are weary of war a thirst for You. Open their eyes to see You, the Messiah of Israel. Do this work that only You can do, with the love that makes You worth following. Give a new heart to Your people, Lord Jesus. Amen.

### 10. God has made Jesus both Lord and Christ

> *Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ.*
>
> — **Acts 2:36** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Peter told the house of Israel that God has made You both Lord and Christ. I pray that many Jewish people would hear that same message in their own place and language and know it to be true. Raise up believers who speak with love and humility, with no contempt and with respect for the Jewish people. Where there has been hostility between churches and Jews, heal and forgive. Let the Holy Spirit convict gently and give joy in return. May many in Israel and in the diaspora say that You are Lord and Messiah. Draw them to Yourself, Lord Jesus. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I give You Israel, the Jewish people in Israel and around the world, and Judea and Samaria. Comfort the grieving, protect the vulnerable, and give wisdom to those who lead. Bring peace where there is violence, and bring many of the Jewish people to know You as Messiah and Lord. I trust You with everything I have prayed, and I wait for You. Amen.*
