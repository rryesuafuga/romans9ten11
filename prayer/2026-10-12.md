## Part 1 · Praying over today's news (points 1–5)

### 1. Counsel and wisdom for Israel's voters and leaders, 15 days before the election

**In the news:** [Poll: Eisenkot bloc slips as Netanyahu's coalition gains ground](https://haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-11/ty-article/israel-election-poll-eisenkot-bloc-continues-slump-as-coalition-gains-ground/000001a1-2c63-d7a5-a7b1-be7397560000); [Bennett open to Netanyahu as a minister; opposition leaders rule it out](https://haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-12/ty-article/.premium/bennett-open-to-netanyahu-as-minister-opposition-leaders-rule-it-out/000001a1-2c6a-d618-adfb-befa77b90000)

> *Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.*
>
> — **Proverbs 11:14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, polls show Israel divided, with neither bloc near a majority, and leaders are weighing who may serve with whom, including whether to demand an inquiry into 7 October. Your word says that where no counsel is, the people fall, and that in a multitude of counsellors there is safety. Give voters and leaders humility to listen, honesty about the past, and wisdom for the future. Guard the vote itself from fear and violence, and let the coming government seek the good of every citizen, Jewish and Arab. Lord, You are the true Counsellor; lead this nation in Your peace. Amen.

### 2. Calm minds as security chiefs weigh Iran, Gaza and Lebanon

**In the news:** [PM convenes security officials amid concerns of Iran escalation](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-11-2026)

> *Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.*
>
> — **Isaiah 26:3** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the prime minister has gathered security officials to consider Iran, Gaza and Lebanon, while reports speak of tension and no final decisions. I ask that You keep in perfect peace those whose minds are stayed on You: the leaders deciding, the soldiers waiting, and the families who fear another night of sirens. Restrain those who plan violence, give clear judgment to those who defend the innocent, and make a way for de-escalation. Be the peace of Israel this week, and keep every civilian in Your care. Amen.

### 3. One year into the Gaza ceasefire: the dead, the wounded and the fragile calm

**In the news:** [PM convenes security officials amid concerns of Iran escalation](https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-11-2026)

> *If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.*
>
> — **Romans 12:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, a year after the ceasefire began, the Hamas-run ministry reports many killed since, and the IDF reports striking a militant commander. Your word calls us to live peaceably with all, as far as it lies with us. I pray for the innocent in Gaza and in Israel's border communities, for soldiers who must act, and for families still grieving. Strengthen every effort that lasts, restrain those who break the calm, comfort the bereaved, and let mercy and truth meet where there has been only loss. Amen.

### 4. Olive harvesters in Judea and Samaria, and the soldiers between them and settlers

**In the news:** [Settlers and soldiers reported attacking olive harvesters](https://newarab.com/news/israeli-settlers-attack-west-bank-olive-harvesters)

> *But they shall sit every man under his vine and under his fig tree; and none shall make them afraid: for the mouth of the LORD of hosts hath spoken it.*
>
> — **Micah 4:4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, reports say farmers picking olives have been met with tear gas and destroyed trees, and I know that claims from different sides differ. Your word promises a day when every man sits under his vine and fig tree, and none make him afraid. Protect the harvesters, Palestinian, Israeli and foreign, from violence, and move soldiers and settlers alike to restraint and justice. Bring truth to light, heal the wounded, and let the land know peace in Your time. Amen.

### 5. Jews in Dublin and Canada living with fear and rising hate

**In the news:** [Dublin event marks 7 October; speakers say Jews feel afraid to say they are Jewish](https://irishtimes.com/ireland/2026/10/11/people-afraid-to-say-they-are-jewish-dublin-event-marks-october-7th-attacks-on-israel); [Canadian Jewish groups highlight rising hate incidents on 7 October anniversary](https://cp24.com/news/canada/2026/10/07/jewish-organizations-highlight-antisemitic-attacks-in-canada-on-oct-7-anniversary)

> *For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.*
>
> — **2 Timothy 1:7** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Jews in Dublin say they are afraid to say who they are, and Canadian Jewish groups report rising hate and heightened guards at synagogues after an attack on Yom Kippur. Your word says God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love and a sound mind. Give these families courage, and give police and neighbors the will to stand beside them. Protect every synagogue and school, turn hearts from hatred, and let them worship without being afraid. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The angel of the Lord encamped around

> *The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.*
>
> — **Psalm 34:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says the angel of the Lord encamps round about those who fear Him and delivers them. I ask that You encamp around the nation of Israel and the city of Jerusalem, over every border, road and home. Guard the soldiers who serve and the civilians who sleep under alarms. Make Your presence a wall that no enemy can break, and give Israel a security that rests on You and not on weapons alone. Amen.

### 7. Preserved in going out and coming in

> <sup>7</sup> The LORD shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul. <sup>8</sup> The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.
>
> — **Psalm 121:7-8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to preserve from all evil, and to keep the going out and the coming in. I pray for every Jew who leaves home today, whether in Tel Aviv or Toronto, to take a child to school, to pray at a synagogue, or to ride a bus. Preserve their souls and their steps. Keep them from the stabber, the gunman and the rocket, and bring them home safely tonight. Amen.

### 8. No weapon formed against them shall prosper

> *No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is of me, saith the LORD.*
>
> — **Isaiah 54:17** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, many weapons are aimed at Israel and at Jewish communities, and many tongues speak lies against them. Your word says no weapon formed against Your people shall prosper. Turn aside missiles, drones and plots before they strike, expose false accusations with truth, and protect the innocent whoever they are. I ask this not in hatred of any, but for the safety of the vulnerable and the restraint of those who plan harm. Amen.

### 9. The gifts and calling that cannot be revoked

> <sup>28</sup> As concerning the gospel, they are enemies for your sakes: but as touching the election, they are beloved for the fathers’ sakes. <sup>29</sup> For the gifts and calling of God are without repentance.
>
> — **Romans 11:28-29** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have said that the gifts and calling of God are without repentance, and that the Jewish people are beloved for the fathers' sake. Thank You for Your faithfulness to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Remember Your covenant over this people today, in the land and in every nation, and hold them through every trial. Let their survival through the centuries remind the world that You keep Your word. Amen.

### 10. Peace in Jesus amid tribulation

> *These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.*
>
> — **John 16:33** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You told Your own that in the world they would have tribulation, yet You have overcome the world and give peace. I pray for Jews in Israel and across the diaspora: the elderly who are alone, young soldiers, families in the north and south, and rabbis and teachers in difficult cities. Give them Your peace and good cheer in the dark hours, and keep them in Your overcoming care. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Seek the Lord while He may be found

> <sup>6</sup> Seek ye the LORD while he may be found, call ye upon him while he is near: <sup>7</sup> Let the wicked forsake his way, and the unrighteous man his thoughts: and let him return unto the LORD, and he will have mercy upon him; and to our God, for he will abundantly pardon.
>
> — **Isaiah 55:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Isaiah calls us to seek the Lord while He may be found, and promises abundant pardon to all who return to Him. I pray that Your people Israel would hear this invitation and turn to You, the Messiah, for mercy. Open eyes that have not yet seen and hearts that have long been closed. Let those who wander, secular or religious, find You near, and let Your pardon be received with joy. Amen.

### 12. The Spirit of grace poured out on Jerusalem

> *And I will pour upon the house of David, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the spirit of grace and of supplications: and they shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him, as one mourneth for his only son, and shall be in bitterness for him, as one that is in bitterness for his firstborn.*
>
> — **Zechariah 12:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised to pour out the Spirit of grace and supplication on the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, that they may look on the One they pierced. Fulfil this word, Lord. Pour out Your Spirit on Jerusalem and on Jewish families everywhere, turn mourning into repentance and then into joy, and let many look on You and live. Amen.

### 13. Whoever calls on the name of the Lord

> *And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered: for in mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, as the LORD hath said, and in the remnant whom the LORD shall call.*
>
> — **Joel 2:32** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You declared that whoever calls on the name of the Lord shall be delivered, and that deliverance is found in Zion and Jerusalem. I pray that Jewish people, secular and religious, in Israel and abroad, would call on Your name and be saved. Where fear, grief or doubt press hard this week, let them find You near, and let the remnant You call answer You gladly. Amen.

### 14. Confessing Jesus as Lord with the heart and mouth

> <sup>9</sup> That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. <sup>10</sup> For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.
>
> — **Romans 10:9-10** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says that if we confess with our mouth that You are Lord and believe in our heart that God raised You from the dead, we shall be saved. With the love of Paul for his own people, I ask that many Jewish friends would believe and confess You. Remove every fear of losing family or community, give them courage, and surround each new believer with love. Amen.

### 15. The way, the truth and the life

> *Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.*
>
> — **John 14:6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said You are the way, the truth and the life, and that no one comes to the Father but by You. I pray that Jewish people would hear these words with gentleness and not with contempt. Raise up faithful, humble witnesses whose lives match their words, and open hearts to the One who loved them and gave Himself for them. Let Your name be honored in Israel and among the Jewish people everywhere. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I place Israel, the Jewish people everywhere, and the people of Judea and Samaria in Your hands today. Protect the innocent, comfort the grieving, give wisdom to leaders, and restrain all who plan harm. Draw Your people to Yourself as Messiah and Lord, and let Your peace rule in Jerusalem and across the nations. Hear us for Your name's sake. Amen.*
