## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The apple of His eye: Israel guarded by God

> *For thus saith the LORD of hosts; After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you: for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.*
>
> — **Zechariah 2:8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Lord of hosts, and Your people are the apple of Your eye. I pray for the nation of Israel in a season of election, war on several fronts and threats from Iran. Guard her borders, her cities and her homes. Confuse the plans of those who plan harm, and defend the families in the north and the south who sleep with sirens near. Let every Israeli know that You have not forgotten them, and let the nations learn that to harm Your people is no small thing. Keep Israel under the shadow of Your wings. Amen.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians: no need to fear

> *The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?*
>
> — **Psalm 27:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the light and the salvation of those who trust You. I pray for the soldiers on the Lebanese border, in Gaza and in Judea and Samaria, and for the civilians whom they protect. Be the strength of their life when they are tired, wounded or afraid. Protect them from ambush, from missiles and from mistakes, and bring each one home. Comfort the bereaved families and let Your presence replace their fear with courage. Give commanders restraint and wisdom, that innocent life is spared. Lord, whom shall we fear when You are near? Amen.

### 8. Jews scattered among the nations: the Shepherd seeks them

> <sup>11</sup> For thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out. <sup>12</sup> As a shepherd seeketh out his flock in the day that he is among his sheep that are scattered; so will I seek out my sheep, and will deliver them out of all places where they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day.
>
> — **Ezekiel 34:11-12** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the Good Shepherd who searches for His sheep and delivers them from every cloudy and dark day. I pray for Jewish communities in every country where they have been scattered, especially those facing hatred, threats and vandalism around synagogues and schools. Protect worshippers at prayer, children on the way to school and the elderly who are afraid. Move police and governments to act with courage and justice. Let Jewish families know they are sought and loved by You, and give their neighbours the grace to stand beside them. Amen.

### 9. Nothing can separate them from God's love

> <sup>38</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, <sup>39</sup> Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
>
> — **Romans 8:38-39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for the Jewish people in Israel and around the world, and for every family broken by war, terror and loss. Thank You that neither death nor life, nor things present nor things to come, can separate Your people from Your love. When fear presses in, remind them that You are near. Hold the widows, the orphans and the wounded in Your arms. Let the memory of 7 October and the ongoing losses be met with Your comfort and not with despair, and give them strength to rebuild and to hope. I trust Your faithful love to carry them through. Amen.

### 10. The Lord is their helper

> <sup>5</sup> Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee. <sup>6</sup> So that we may boldly say, The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me.
>
> — **Hebrews 13:5-6** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You have said that You will never leave us nor forsake us. I pray this promise over Jerusalem and over every Jewish home in Israel and the diaspora. Be their helper when threats are spoken and when plots are made. Let them say with boldness that the Lord is their helper and they will not fear what man can do. Strengthen leaders, security services and community watch groups who guard the vulnerable. Keep Your people from bitterness and from violence in return, and let Your presence be their security every day and every night. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. A heart that is renewed

> <sup>5</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the LORD, that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth. <sup>6</sup> In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell safely: and this is his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS.
>
> — **Jeremiah 23:5-6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the righteous Branch of David, the King who reigns and prospers, and the name by which Israel shall be called is THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS. I pray that You would open the eyes of the Jewish people to see You as the Messiah promised through the prophets. Remove the hurt and the history that stand between Your people and You, and let them find in You the salvation that lets Judah be saved and Israel dwell safely. Give them a hunger for Your word and a desire to walk with You. I ask this in love for them, as for my own family, and I trust You to finish what You begin. Amen.

### 12. The Messiah who brings good tidings to Zion

> *How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, that publisheth peace; that bringeth good tidings of good, that publisheth salvation; that saith unto Zion, Thy God reigneth!*
>
> — **Isaiah 52:7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, how beautiful are the feet of those who bring good tidings and publish salvation, who say to Zion, Thy God reigneth. I pray that this word would reach Israel today through every honest voice and every quiet kindness. Open the ears of Jewish people to hear that You reign, that You are the promised Servant and King, and that salvation is in You. Raise up messengers in Jerusalem, in Tel Aviv and in the cities of the diaspora, who speak with humility and love. Let the good news be received with joy. Amen.

### 13. Repentance, forgiveness and a promise to their children

> <sup>38</sup> Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost. <sup>39</sup> For the promise is unto you, and to your children, and to all that are afar off, even as many as the Lord our God shall call.
>
> — **Acts 2:38-39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, on the day of Pentecost Peter called Jewish listeners to repent and receive forgiveness in Your name, and promised the gift of the Holy Ghost to them and their children. I pray that same call for Jewish families today. Convict hearts gently of sin and draw them to You for forgiveness. Let parents and children come to You together, and let the promise reach those who are far away from their roots and from faith. Meet every seeker with grace, and give courage to those who count the cost of following You. Amen.

### 14. Witnesses to every nation, beginning at Jerusalem

> <sup>46</sup> And said unto them, Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead the third day: <sup>47</sup> And that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.
>
> — **Luke 24:46-47** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You told Your disciples that repentance and forgiveness of sins should be preached in Your name to all nations, beginning at Jerusalem. I pray for the Jewish and Arab believers who bear witness in Israel and for Christians worldwide. Make our witness gentle, humble and free from contempt. Give wisdom to Messianic congregations, protection for those who face pressure from family and community, and open doors for the gospel in Jerusalem and beyond. Let Your resurrection be the answer that Your people finally find, and let many hear and believe. Amen.

### 15. The gospel, to the Jew first

> *For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.*
>
> — **Romans 1:16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth, to the Jew first. I pray for Jewish believers in You, in Israel and in every nation, and for those on the edge of faith. Strengthen those who face rejection by family or community. Bind them together in love, and make their lives a clear light to their people. Give them boldness, joy and perseverance. Let the number of those who confess You as Messiah and Lord grow, until all Israel is saved and You are glorified. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I bring You Israel, Jews at home and abroad, and the people of Judea and Samaria. Keep the soldiers and the civilians safe, comfort the grieving, give wisdom to leaders and voters, and restrain those who would do harm. Above all, open the hearts of Your own people to know You as Messiah and Lord. For Your name's sake. Amen.*
