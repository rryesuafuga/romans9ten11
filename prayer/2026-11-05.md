### 1. A government to be formed after a deadlocked election

**In the news:** [Israel's opposition leaders back Eisenkot to form a government](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/3/israeli-opposition-leaders-back-eisenkot-to-oust-netanyahu); [Political deadlock leaves Netanyahu's future uncertain](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/3/israel-political-deadlock-leaves-netanyahus-future-hanging-in-the-balance); [President Herzog begins consultations on forming a government](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/israels-president-isaac-herzog-starts-process-to-form-new-government-after-netanyahus-claims-of-election-fraud-parliamentary-arab-led-party-opposition-leader-gadi-eisenkot-coalition)

> *And he changeth the times and the seasons: he removeth kings, and setteth up kings: he giveth wisdom unto the wise, and knowledge to them that know understanding:*
>
> — **Daniel 2:21** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the One who changes times and seasons, removes kings and sets up kings. I pray for Israel while President Herzog consults the party leaders and neither bloc has a clear majority. Give wisdom and understanding to Gadi Eisenkot, Benjamin Netanyahu and every leader involved. Guard the country from bitterness and division, let the election results be respected, and let truth and calm govern how the claims about the vote are settled. Raise up leaders who fear You and seek the good of every citizen, Jew and Arab alike. Be glorious in the midst of this uncertainty, and bring Israel through it in peace. Amen.

> — **Psalm 82:3-4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I bring to You the families in the West Bank who have been displaced or hurt, including those whose homes near Jericho were entered and those returning to damaged houses in Hebron. Defend the poor and needy, and do justice for the afflicted. Protect Israeli and Palestinian farmers and children during the olive harvest, and restrain those who would use violence on any side. Give soldiers and officials wisdom to act fairly and to keep the peace. Comfort the injured and frightened, and let Your justice and mercy be seen in Judea and Samaria. I ask for protection for the innocent, whoever they are. Amen.

### 3. Peacemakers in Judea and Samaria

**In the news:** [Settlers take over eight homes in Diyuk near Jericho; two injured](https://newscord.org/article/israeli-settlers-seize-eight-palestinian-homes-in-diyuk-neighborhood-near-jerich--Story_20261104_8a4cc2999); [Palestinian Information Center reports renewed settler attacks across the West Bank](https://english.palinfo.com/news/2026/11/04/373125)

> *Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.*
>
> — **Matthew 5:9** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You said that the peacemakers are blessed and shall be called children of God. I pray for peacemakers among Jews and Arabs in Judea and Samaria, in the villages, the settlements and the army. Raise up men and women who calm anger rather than inflame it, who speak truth with kindness, and who refuse revenge. Where homes are disputed and tempers are high, send Your Spirit of reconciliation. Soften hard hearts, and let neighbours find ways to live without fear. Bless those who work for ordinary safety and fairness, and be the peace between peoples who cannot yet find it. Amen.

### 4. Calm for northern Israel and Lebanon

>
> — **Philippians 4:6-7** · KJV · New Testament

As the IDF chief warns Hezbollah against rebuilding along the border, I ask that Your peace, which passes all understanding, would keep the hearts and minds of the soldiers, the commanders and the families waiting at home. Give wisdom to every leader so that war is prevented. Restrain those who plan violence, protect civilians in Israel and Lebanon, and let anxious people bring their requests to You with thanksgiving. Amen.

> *The LORD is on my side; I will not fear: what can man do unto me?*
>
> — **Psalm 118:6** · KJV · Old Testament

The Lord is on their side, so they need not fear what man can do to them. Comfort the worshippers and give them courage to gather. Help the police find and stop those responsible, and give community leaders wisdom about safety. Be near every Jewish family in the diaspora who feels afraid this week, and soften the hearts of those who hate. Let Your peace rest on each synagogue and school. Amen.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. The people God kept in the wilderness

> *He found him in a desert land, and in the waste howling wilderness; he led him about, he instructed him, he kept him as the apple of his eye.*
>
> — **Deuteronomy 32:10** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are the God who found Israel in a desert land and kept her as the apple of Your eye. I thank You that You have led, taught and guarded this people through every wilderness in their history. Keep Israel today as the apple of Your eye: guard her borders, her towns and her homes, and protect the soldiers and civilians who serve and live there. Teach her leaders as You taught Your people, and lead the nation again through danger into safety. Let every Israeli child sleep in security under Your watching care. Amen.

### 7. Jerusalem surrounded

> <sup>6</sup> Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee. <sup>7</sup> Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces.
>
> — **Psalm 122:6-7** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You call us to pray for the peace of Jerusalem, and I do: let peace be within her walls and prosperity within her palaces. I ask You to surround Jerusalem and every city of Israel with Your protection. Guard the pilgrims, the families, the police and the soldiers, and the worshippers at every holy place. Turn aside every plot against the city, whether from rockets, knives or any weapon. Let Jerusalem be known as a city of peace, and let all who love her find rest. I trust Your watchfulness over this city day and night. Amen.

### 8. Shelter for the needy and the afflicted

> *For thou hast been a strength to the poor, a strength to the needy in his distress, a refuge from the storm, a shadow from the heat, when the blast of the terrible ones is as a storm against the wall.*
>
> — **Isaiah 25:4** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are a strength to the poor and needy in distress, a refuge from the storm and a shadow from the heat. I pray for Israelis who run to shelters, for those who have lost loved ones, and for families displaced by war. Be a refuge for the wounded, the widowed and the orphaned, and a shadow from the heat of fear. When the blast of terrible ones comes like a storm against the wall, hold the wall firm. Give the lonely and traumatised healing, and let them feel Your presence. Amen.

### 9. Greater is He who is in us

> *Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.*
>
> — **1 John 4:4** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray for Jewish believers and for all Your people in Israel and across the diaspora who face hostility. You have overcome the world, and greater is He that is in us than he that is in the world. Fill every Jewish family and every believer with courage that comes from You. Protect those at risk of attack because of who they are, and guard students, children and the elderly in places where hatred is rising. Break the power of fear and lies, and let love be stronger than hate in every community. Amen.

### 10. Covenant love that cannot be broken

> <sup>38</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, <sup>39</sup> Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
>
> — **Romans 8:38-39** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that nothing in life or death, no power or principality, nothing present or to come, can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord. I pray this over Israel and the Jewish people everywhere, who have been scattered, hunted and hated, yet kept. Confirm Your faithfulness to them in this generation. Let them know they are loved and held, and let the nations see that the God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob keeps His word. Guard Your people to the end, and bring them into the fullness of Your promise. Amen.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. The God we have waited for

> *And it shall be said in that day, Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is the LORD; we have waited for him, we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation.*
>
> — **Isaiah 25:9** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I pray that Jewish people everywhere would one day say, Lo, this is our God; we have waited for Him, and He will save us. Show Yourself to Israel as the promised Messiah and Saviour, the One for whom generations of her people have waited. Open the eyes of those who long for redemption, in Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, New York and Kampala, so that they see You as the answer. Let their waiting turn into rejoicing in Your salvation. I ask this with love for the people from whom You came. Amen.

### 12. Seeking with all the heart

> <sup>13</sup> And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart. <sup>14</sup> And I will be found of you, saith the LORD: and I will turn away your captivity, and I will gather you from all the nations, and from all the places whither I have driven you, saith the LORD; and I will bring you again into the place whence I caused you to be carried away captive.
>
> — **Jeremiah 29:13-14** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You promised that those who seek God with all their heart will find Him. I pray for every Jewish person who is searching, whether through study, grief, doubt or the shock of this season. Be found by them. Draw their hearts toward You as the Messiah and Lord, and bring them out of fear and into peace. Gather Your scattered people, not only to the land but to You. Send them dreams, friends, Scripture and quiet moments that lead them to Your truth, and let them find You when they search with all their heart. Amen.

### 13. God so loved the world

> <sup>16</sup> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life. <sup>17</sup> For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved.
>
> — **John 3:16-17** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I thank You that God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, so that whosoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life. You came not to condemn but to save. I pray that Jewish people would hear this good news with fresh ears and receive forgiveness of sins through You. Remove the shame, the misunderstanding and the history of pain that has stood between Your people and You. Let repentance and faith rise in the hearts of Israelis and Jews in every nation, and let them find everlasting life in You. Amen.

### 14. Faithful witnesses who speak with love

> *How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher?*
>
> — **Romans 10:14** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You asked how they can call on Him in whom they have not believed, and how they can hear without a preacher. Raise up faithful, humble and loving witnesses to the Jewish people, in Israel and in every diaspora community. Give them kindness and wisdom, and keep them from arrogance and from any contempt toward the people You love. Let their lives and words be sweet and true. Send believers who will listen, answer questions and pray, and open Jewish hearts to hear the gospel in a way they can understand. Amen.

### 15. One new man in Messiah

> <sup>14</sup> For he is our peace, who hath made both one, and hath broken down the middle wall of partition between us; <sup>15</sup> Having abolished in his flesh the enmity, even the law of commandments contained in ordinances; for to make in himself of twain one new man, so making peace; <sup>16</sup> And that he might reconcile both unto God in one body by the cross, having slain the enmity thereby:
>
> — **Ephesians 2:14-16** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You are our peace, who broke down the middle wall of partition and made Jew and Gentile one in Yourself through the cross. I thank You for every Jewish believer in You in Israel and around the world. Strengthen them in faith, protect them from rejection and isolation, and give them boldness and joy. Join them with the Gentile church in true love, so that the world can see the reconciling work of the cross. Bring many more of Your Jewish brothers and sisters into this peace, until Your people are one in You. Amen.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, I give Israel, the Jews in the land and across the nations, and the people of Judea and Samaria into Your hands today. Protect the innocent, give wisdom to leaders, comfort the grieving, and draw Your people to Yourself as Messiah and Lord. Let Your peace and truth reign, and let my prayer be answered according to Your good and faithful love. Amen.*
