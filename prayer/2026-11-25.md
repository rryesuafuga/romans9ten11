I pray for every family in Gaza City grieving after the strikes reported this week, and for the innocent who were caught in them. Restrain all who plan violence and terror, and give wisdom to commanders. Save those of a contrite spirit, and let the grieving on every side meet Your comfort and hear of the peace that only You can give.

> <sup>18</sup> Behold, the eye of the LORD is upon them that fear him, upon them that hope in his mercy; <sup>19</sup> To deliver their soul from death, and to keep them alive in famine. <sup>20</sup> Our soul waiteth for the LORD: he is our help and our shield.

Expose real danger and silence false alarms. Keep the people of Israel, and the people of Iran, from the horror of war and weapons that destroy without mercy. Deliver souls from death. Let Israel's leaders wait upon You, for You are our help and our shield.

Make Jewish and Arab residents swift to hear, slow to speak and slow to wrath. Protect families from terror and from revenge attacks alike. Give soldiers, police and officials wisdom to act justly and impartially. Turn hearts away from fear and anger, and let Your righteousness, not human fury, shape what happens in these hills.

I pray for the Jewish community there, and for Jews in Europe and America who hear public calls for their expulsion and feel the weight of political hostility, as Chief Rabbi Mirvis warned. Strengthen them, help them, and uphold them with the right hand of Your righteousness. Give police and courts diligence, and turn the hearts of those who hate toward repentance and toward You.

## Part 2 · Divine protection of Israel and the Jewish people (points 6–10)

### 6. Peace in the land and safety within its borders

> *And I will give peace in the land, and ye shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid: and I will rid evil beasts out of the land, neither shall the sword go through your land.*
>
> — **Leviticus 26:6** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, I ask You to give peace in the land of Israel, so that its people may lie down and none shall make them afraid. Guard the borders in the north and the south, the farms and towns near Gaza and Lebanon, and the roads of Judea and Samaria. Let the sword not go through the land. Restrain every plan of terror, uncover hidden weapons, and keep children safe in their homes and schools. Let the people of Israel rest under Your care, and know that peace is Your gift rather than their own achievement.

### 7. Soldiers and civilians preserved in their going out and coming in

> <sup>7</sup> The LORD shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul. <sup>8</sup> The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.
>
> — **Psalm 121:7-8** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, the LORD shall preserve thee from all evil. I pray for Israel's soldiers on every front, for reservists away from their families, and for civilians who drive, work and study where sirens sound. Preserve their going out and their coming in. Keep the wounded from despair and the weary from bitterness. Comfort mothers and fathers who wait for news. Preserve their souls as well as their bodies, and draw each one to know You as the One who watches over them from this time forth and even for evermore.

### 8. Jerusalem defended and preserved

> *As birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem; defending also he will deliver it; and passing over he will preserve it.*
>
> — **Isaiah 31:5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, as birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem. You wept over this city and You will return to it. I pray for its residents, Jewish and Arab, for its holy places, and for those who travel its streets and light rail each day. Defend it from attack, deliver it from those who plot harm, and pass over it to preserve it. Keep watch over its schools, markets and synagogues. Let peace be within its walls, and let Jerusalem become a place where many seek You and find You.

### 9. Jews in the diaspora kept through His name

> *And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.*
>
> — **John 17:11** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You prayed to the Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me. I pray for Jewish people scattered among the nations: in Russia, in Britain and France, in Canada and America, in Ethiopia and Uganda, and in small communities that feel alone. Keep them safe in their synagogues, schools and homes. Guard their young people from fear and from hiding who they are. Give their leaders wisdom about security, and surround them with neighbours who stand with them. Hold them in Your care wherever they live.

### 10. The unchangeable counsel of God to the heirs of promise

> <sup>17</sup> Wherein God, willing more abundantly to shew unto the heirs of promise the immutability of his counsel, confirmed it by an oath: <sup>18</sup> That by two immutable things, in which it was impossible for God to lie, we might have a strong consolation, who have fled for refuge to lay hold upon the hope set before us:
>
> — **Hebrews 6:17-18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, God confirmed His counsel by an oath, and it is impossible for Him to lie. I thank You that the promises He spoke to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob have not failed through all the centuries of exile and danger. Uphold the Jewish people according to that immutable word. When nations rage and leaders waver, keep them by Your faithfulness. Let them find strong consolation in You, and let them flee for refuge to lay hold upon the hope set before them, which is fulfilled in You, the Son of David.

## Part 3 · Salvation of the Jewish people in Jesus Christ (points 11–15)

### 11. Eyes opened, from darkness to light

> *To open their eyes, and to turn them from darkness to light, and from the power of Satan unto God, that they may receive forgiveness of sins, and inheritance among them which are sanctified by faith that is in me.*
>
> — **Acts 26:18** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You sent Paul to open their eyes, and to turn them from darkness to light. I pray for Jewish men and women in Israel and in every nation who have never truly considered who You are. Open their eyes to see You as the Messiah of Israel. Turn them from every power that blinds, and bring them to God, that they may receive forgiveness of sins and an inheritance among the sanctified. Begin with those who are searching today, and let them hear Your voice through Your word.

### 12. The Anointed One foretold by Isaiah

> *The Spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek; he hath sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and the opening of the prison to them that are bound;*
>
> — **Isaiah 61:1** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, You stood in the synagogue at Nazareth and read these words, and said they were fulfilled in You. You are the One anointed to preach good tidings to the meek and to bind up the brokenhearted. I pray that Jewish readers of Isaiah would recognise You in his prophecy. Bind up hearts broken by war, grief and hatred. Proclaim liberty to those held captive by fear, shame and unbelief, and open the prison of despair. Let many in Israel know that the Anointed One has come.

### 13. Returning to seek the LORD and David their king

> <sup>4</sup> For the children of Israel shall abide many days without a king, and without a prince, and without a sacrifice, and without an image, and without an ephod, and without teraphim: <sup>5</sup> Afterward shall the children of Israel return, and seek the LORD their God, and David their king; and shall fear the LORD and his goodness in the latter days.
>
> — **Hosea 3:4-5** · KJV · Old Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Hosea foretold that the children of Israel would abide many days without a king and without a sacrifice, and afterward return to seek the LORD their God and David their king. You are the Son of David and the King of Israel, and Your sacrifice is complete. I ask You to bring that return in this generation. Lead Jewish people to repentance and to the goodness of God. Let them seek You with humble hearts and find in You the forgiveness and the King they have long awaited.

### 14. Faithful witnesses who reason from Moses and the prophets

> *And when they had appointed him a day, there came many to him into his lodging; to whom he expounded and testified the kingdom of God, persuading them concerning Jesus, both out of the law of Moses, and out of the prophets, from morning till evening.*
>
> — **Acts 28:23** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Paul spent from morning till evening persuading his Jewish brethren concerning You out of the law of Moses and out of the prophets. Raise up witnesses like him today: patient, loving, humble and well grounded in the Scriptures. Give believers in Israel and in the diaspora open doors to speak about You at home, at work and among friends. Keep them from contempt or pride, and fill them with the love of Romans 10:1. Let many who listen be persuaded that You are the Christ.

### 15. Jewish believers in Jesus and those yet to be grafted in

> *And they also, if they abide not still in unbelief, shall be graffed in: for God is able to graff them in again.*
>
> — **Romans 11:23** · KJV · New Testament

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, Your word says God is able to graff them in again. I thank You for Jewish believers in You in Israel and around the world, and I ask You to strengthen their congregations, protect their families and give them courage when they are misunderstood. Let them be a living sign that Israel is not forgotten. And for those who do not yet believe, I ask that they abide not still in unbelief, but be grafted back into their own olive tree through faith in You.

## Closing prayer

*Lord Jesus, my heart's desire and prayer to God for Israel is that they might be saved. Keep the Jewish people in the land and among the nations, comfort the grieving on every side, give wisdom to leaders, and restrain those who plan harm. Open many eyes to see You as Messiah and King, and let me pray faithfully until that day. Amen.*
